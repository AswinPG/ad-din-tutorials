Assumptions

What “Payment Module” covers

Track 11 (madrasah fees, invoices and payments) is removed. Donations (Track 08) and subscriptions (Track 02) stay in. Video 14.4 keeps report cards and interview bookings but drops fee history, and 12.4 skips the payment report category.

How the videos are made

As with the Staff Attendance videos: screen recording runs off a script against the real app with a seeded demo organisation, with AI voice-over and captions. A human presenter or professional voice-over adds about 1.5–2 hours per video.

Calendar time

One person full time: about 21 weeks. Two people working in parallel by track: about 11 weeks, plus about a week first to build the shared demo data and recording setup.

Not included

Translations or extra languages, fixing app bugs found while recording, and redesigning screens before filming. Open questions in the series plan (for example whether Staff Attendance is live) may move videos between tracks but don’t change the total much.

Companion to “Ad-Din Tutorial Series” (series plan, 2 November 2026). Video counts and runtimes come from that plan, with Track 11 removed.
