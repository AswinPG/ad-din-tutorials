# 06.1 Prepare online enrolment

## Goal
Set up everything parents see when they enrol online: the enrolment link and QR code, required documents, your declaration and agreement, FAQs, extra questions, and which classes are open.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Enrolment link and QR code, Required Documents, Declaration & Agreement, FAQs, Custom Fields
- open a class for enrolment
- Why online enrolment; the print sheet and pop-ups; the no-masjid-linked line
- Hidden Document, other user roles, Edit / Update a document
- The standard agreement when yours is empty; FAQ Edit and Delete
- Parent, Child, Teacher and Admin Fields; Edit, Delete (Confirm Delete Field) or switch off a field
- Program page (pointer to 05.8); what happens when a parent submits (admins told, Pending Enrollments)

## Demo data
- Demo madrasah "Maple Grove Madrasah" with a logo; the demo account (Yusuf Rahman) is its Madrasah Admin, with Premium
- "Maple Grove Masjid" linked on the Madrasah/School Details tab (so the "Parents using this link will also get Maple Grove Masjid as their home masjid." line shows)
- No required documents yet; one added on camera: "Birth Certificate", User Role Student, Mandatory
- Empty agreement and FAQs, so Load Default Agreement and Load Default FAQs fill them on camera
- No custom fields yet; one added on camera: Child Fields → "Language spoken at home", Dropdown-Single select, options English / Arabic / Urdu, Required
- Class "Weekend Quran" with Open for enrollment off, turned on during the video; "Hifz Class A" already open
- Pop-ups allowed in the recording browser (Print opens a new window)
- For the [3.3a] inset only: a madrasah with no linked masjid (any second demo madrasah the admin manages, or the same page recorded before the masjid is linked)

## Script

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** In this video for madrasah admins, you'll set up online enrolment: everything parents see when they enrol a child with the link you share.
**Show:** Title card: "Prepare online enrolment".

#### [1.1a] Why use it
**Say:** With online enrolment, families apply from home at any time, with no paper forms to type in later. Each application waits for you to approve it, so nobody joins a class without your say.
**Show:** Card: "Families apply online · No paper forms · You approve each one".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your classes are already in place. You'll set up the link, required documents, your agreement, FAQs and extra questions, then open a class for enrolment.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need, with the five parts listed.

### 2. Open Enrollment Settings
#### [2.1] Find your madrasah
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Admin Portal**, then **Organization Management**. Find your madrasah and tap **Manage**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Organization Management. The organisation picker; click "Manage" on "Maple Grove Madrasah". The "Manage Madrasah" page opens.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Tap the **Enrollment Settings** tab. Everything for online enrolment is on this one page.
**Show:** Click the "Enrollment Settings" tab. Slow scroll from top to bottom past each card, then back to the top.

### 3. Share your enrolment link
#### [3.1] The link
**Say:** At the top is **Share Enrolment Link**. Parents who use this link sign up and land straight on your enrolment form. Tap **Copy**, then paste the link into a message or onto your website.
**Show:** Spotlight the "Enrolment link" field, then click "Copy". Toast "Link copied".

#### [3.2] The QR code
**Say:** For your notice board, use the QR code. Tap **Download QR** to save it as a picture, or **Print** for a ready-made sheet with your logo.
**Show:** Zoom on the QR code. Click "Print": the print sheet with the logo, the madrasah name and the QR code opens. Close the print dialog.

#### [3.2a] The print sheet
**Say:** The sheet shows your logo, your madrasah's name, the words "Scan to enrol your child", the QR code and the link. If nothing opens when you tap **Print**, allow pop-ups for Ad-Din in your browser.
**Show:** Hold on the print sheet; spotlight the heading "Scan to enrol your child" and the link under the QR code. Close it.

#### [3.3]
**Say:** Because a masjid is linked on the **Madrasah/School Details** tab, parents who join through this link get that masjid as their home masjid too.
**Show:** Spotlight the green line under the link: "Parents using this link will also get … as their home masjid."

#### [3.3a]
**Say:** If no masjid is linked, the line says so instead, and parents get only your madrasah. To add one, link your masjid on the details tab.
**Show:** Inset card (recorded separately on a madrasah with no linked masjid): the line "Link a masjid on the Details tab so parents get it automatically."

### 4. Ask for documents
#### [4.1] Add a document
**Say:** Next is **Required Documents Management**. To ask parents for a file, such as a birth certificate, tap **Add New Document**.
**Show:** Scroll to the card. Click "Add New Document"; the "Add Required Document" window opens.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Enter a **Document Name**, and choose who it belongs to under **User Role**, usually Student or Parent. A short **Description** helps parents know what to upload.
**Show:** Type "Birth Certificate". User Role → Student. Type a one-line description.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** Tick **Mandatory Document** if parents can't continue without it, then tap **Add**.
**Show:** Tick "Mandatory Document", click "Add". Toast "Document created successfully"; the row appears in the table.

#### [4.3a] Hidden documents
**Say:** **Hidden Document** keeps a document off the parents' form. Only admins see it, so use it for papers your madrasah keeps itself. You can also choose Teacher or Admin under **User Role**, for papers you keep for your staff.
**Show:** Reopen "Add New Document"; spotlight "Hidden Document", then open User Role to show Student, Teacher, Parent and Admin. Click "Cancel".

#### [4.3b] Change a document later
**Say:** Each document has an **Edit** button in the table. Change any detail and tap **Update**. To stop asking for a document, untick **Mandatory Document** or tick **Hidden Document**.
**Show:** Click "Edit" on "Birth Certificate"; the "Edit Required Document" window opens. Spotlight "Mandatory Document" and "Hidden Document", then click "Cancel".

### 5. Your declaration and agreement
#### [5.1] Agreement
**Say:** **Parent/Guardian Declaration & Agreement** is the text parents must accept before they submit. Tap **Load Default Agreement** for a ready-made version. It replaces any text already there and saves straight away.
**Show:** Scroll to the card. Click "Load Default Agreement"; the text fills the editor. Toast "Agreement and FAQs saved successfully".

#### [5.2]
**Say:** Change any wording to suit your madrasah, then tap **Save**.
**Show:** Edit one sentence in the editor. Click "Save". Toast "Agreement and FAQs saved successfully".

#### [5.2a]
**Say:** Parents must tick that they agree before they can submit. If you leave this box empty, parents see Ad-Din's standard agreement. Ask your committee to check the wording, especially about fees.
**Show:** Scroll the agreement slowly; spotlight the "Fee Responsibility" paragraph.

### 6. Answer common questions
#### [6.1] FAQs
**Say:** Under **Add FAQs**, tap **Load Default FAQs** for a set of common questions. It replaces any FAQs you already have. Or tap **Add FAQ** to write your own.
**Show:** Click "Load Default FAQs"; the numbered list appears. Then click "+ Add FAQ".

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Type the **Question** and the **Answer**, then tap **Save**. Parents see these FAQs beside the list of programs.
**Show:** Type a question ("Is there a sibling discount?") and a short answer. Click "Save"; it joins the list. Toast "Agreement and FAQs saved successfully".

#### [6.2a] Change or remove an FAQ
**Say:** Tap a question to open or close its answer. **Edit** changes it, and **Delete** removes it straight away. Good FAQs answer what parents ask you most: class times, fees and what to bring.
**Show:** Click a question to open its answer, then close it. Spotlight "Edit" and "Delete" on the new FAQ; don't click.

### 7. Ask extra questions
#### [7.1] Custom Fields
**Say:** To ask parents something more, such as the language spoken at home, use **Custom Fields**. Choose **Child Fields**, then tap **Add Field**.
**Show:** Scroll to "Custom Fields". Click "Child Fields", then "Add Field"; the "New field" form opens.

#### [7.1a] Four groups
**Say:** **Parent Fields** and **Child Fields** are asked on the enrolment form, under **Other details**. **Teacher Fields** and **Admin Fields** appear on those people's profiles in User Management.
**Show:** Spotlight the four buttons: Parent Fields, Child Fields, Teacher Fields, Admin Fields. Keep Child Fields selected.

#### [7.2]
**Say:** Give it a **Label** and pick a **Type**, such as short text, a dropdown, checkboxes or a date. For a dropdown, add each option.
**Show:** Type "Language spoken at home". Type → "Dropdown-Single select"; the Options list starts empty. Click "Add option" three times and fill English, Arabic and Urdu.

#### [7.3]
**Say:** Turn on **Required** if parents must answer, then tap **Save field**. To stop asking later, switch the field off. It stays in the list for next time.
**Show:** Toggle "Required", click "Save field". Toast "Custom field saved successfully". Spotlight the Active switch on the new field.

#### [7.3a] Edit or delete a field
**Say:** **Edit** changes a field. **Delete** asks you to confirm, because it also deletes every answer parents gave. If you might need those answers, switch the field off instead.
**Show:** Click "Delete" on the new field: the "Confirm Delete Field" box. Spotlight its message, then click "Cancel".

### 8. Open a class for enrolment
#### [8.1] Choose the classes
**Say:** Last, choose which classes parents can pick. In the sidebar, open **Admin Portal**, then **Class**. Select a class and tap **Edit**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Class. Click the row "Weekend Quran", then "Edit". The "Edit Class" window opens.

#### [8.2]
**Say:** Turn on **Open for enrollment**, then tap **Save**. Parents can now choose this class when they enrol a child.
**Show:** Zoom on "Open for enrollment" and its hint "Parents can pick this class when enrolling a child." Turn it on, click "Save".

#### [8.2a] Help parents choose
**Say:** In the same window, **Program page** adds a description and a picture, which parents see under **Program details**. Video 05.8 shows how. When a class is full, turn **Open for enrollment** off again.
**Show:** Reopen "Edit" on "Weekend Quran"; spotlight the "Program page" section and "Add description & picture". Click "Cancel".

### 9. You're ready
#### [9.1] Done
**Say:** Your enrolment form is ready. Share the link or put up the QR code, and applications will start to arrive.
**Show:** Back on Enrollment Settings, hold on the link and QR code.

#### [9.1a] What happens next
**Say:** When a parent submits an application, every admin of your madrasah gets an email and a push notification. The application waits under **Pending Enrollments** until you approve or decline it.
**Show:** Card: "Parent submits → every admin is told by email and push → Pending Enrollments".

#### [9.2] Next
**Say:** Next, video 06.2: Enrol your child online, to see the form from a parent's side, and video 06.3: Approve or decline enrolment applications.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "06.2 Enrol your child online" and "06.3 Approve or decline enrolment applications".
