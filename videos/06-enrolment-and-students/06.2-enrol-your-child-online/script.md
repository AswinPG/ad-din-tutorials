# 06.2 Enrol your child online

## Goal
Use your madrasah's enrolment link to sign up, choose a program, fill in your details and your child's, and submit the application.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- The enrolment link or QR code from your madrasah
- Your child's details, and any documents the madrasah asks for (for example a birth certificate)
- A web browser or the Ad-Din app

## Covers
- Enrolment link → sign up → Select Program → Parent/Guardian Details → Child Details → Review and Submit
- FAQs and Enrollments cards; Sign Out & Register a New Parent Account; a second child (Enroll Selected, Add New Child)
- Required fields and mobile number; spouse GUID and Fetch; Same for entire family
- Username rules and the availability tick; number-only Juz/Para; optional Medical Card No. and Date Joined
- Document file types and the required-document check; extra emergency contacts
- Remove, Add more course and Previous on the review step
- Confirmation email and push; Edit → Update Parent / Update Child while pending; Approved card; enrolling costs nothing

## Demo data
- Demo madrasah "Maple Grove Madrasah" (logo, Toronto) with the enrolment settings from 06.1: a mandatory "Birth Certificate" document for students, the default agreement and FAQs, and the custom child field "Language spoken at home"
- Two classes open for enrolment with program descriptions and pictures: "Weekend Quran" and "Hifz Class A"
- A new parent who isn't in Ad-Din yet: "Khadija Malik", username `khadija_malik`, demo email, and demo password typed off screen or masked; reCAPTCHA off in the demo
- No children linked to the new parent yet (so the "Add New Child" table doesn't appear), and no existing enrolments
- Demo child "Musa Malik": username `musa_malik`, demo email, date of birth, one allergy note; a small sample PDF to upload as the birth certificate; one emergency contact (demo name and mobile), entered on the Child Details step
- After submit, the Enrollments card shows Musa with "Weekend Quran" and status Pending
- For the [3.4a] inset: a second demo parent who already has one linked child in a class (the "Select your child or add new." list); for the [7.2a] inset: an approved enrolment card with section and teacher (for example Samir Karim's Zaid in Hifz Class A)
- For the [4.2a] cut-in: Applicant is a Single Parent → No, then Yes to "Does your spouse already have an Ad-Din account?", so the Spouse GUID box and Fetch show (no GUID typed on camera)

## Script

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** In this video for parents, you'll enrol your child at a madrasah online, using the link or QR code your madrasah gives you.
**Show:** Title card: "Enrol your child online".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Have your child's details ready, and any documents the madrasah asks for. Applying through Ad-Din is free, and you can do it in a web browser or in the Ad-Din app.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

### 2. Open the link and sign up
#### [2.1] The link
**Say:** Open the link, or scan the QR code with your phone's camera. The page shows your madrasah's name under **Enrolling at**.
**Show:** Browser opens the enrolment link. The sign-up page with the left panel "Enrolling at Maple Grove Madrasah" and "Powered by Ad-Din.ca". Zoom on the madrasah name.

#### [2.2] New to Ad-Din
**Say:** If you're new to Ad-Din, the **Sign Up** form is already open. Enter a username, your email and a password, tick the box to agree, and tap **Sign Up**.
**Show:** Fill Username, Email, Password and Confirm Password (masked). Tick the agree box; keep the shot wide, no zoom on the two links. Click "Sign Up".
**Caption:** If you're new to Ad-Din, fill in the Sign Up form, tick the box to agree, and tap Sign Up.

#### [2.3]
**Say:** If you already have an account, tap **Login here** instead. Either way, Ad-Din takes you straight to your madrasah's enrolment form.
**Show:** Spotlight "Already have an account? Login here" without clicking it. Then the "Setting up your enrolment…" screen, and the form opens.

### 3. Choose a program
#### [3.1] Select Program
**Say:** **Select Program** shows the classes your madrasah has opened for enrolment. Tap **Program details** to read more about one.
**Show:** The Select Program page with the madrasah banner and "Program offered". Click "Program details" on "Weekend Quran"; the details panel opens, then close it.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Tap **Select** on each program you want for your child, then tap **Proceed**.
**Show:** Click "Select" on "Weekend Quran"; it changes to "Selected" with a tick. The bar shows "1 program selected". Click "Proceed".

#### [3.2a] FAQs and your applications
**Say:** On the same page, **FAQs** answers questions the madrasah hears often, such as what you need to apply. **Enrollments** lists any applications you have already sent.
**Show:** Before clicking Proceed (cut-in): spotlight the "FAQs" card and open one question; then spotlight the "Enrollments" card with "No Active Enrollments".

#### [3.3]
**Say:** Ad-Din checks that you're the parent or guardian. Choose **Yes, I am logged in as the parent or guardian**, then tap **Continue to Enrollment**.
**Show:** The confirmation window with "YOU ARE LOGGED IN AS" and the parent's name. Pick the Yes option, click "Continue to Enrollment →".

#### [3.4]
**Say:** If you're signed in on your child's account by mistake, choose No. Enrolment must come from a parent or guardian account. Tap **Sign Out & Register a New Parent Account**, then sign in with yours or create one.
**Show:** Quick cut, not recorded live: the No option selected, with the notice "Enrollment not available for your account type." and the button "Sign Out & Register a New Parent Account"; don't click it. Then back to Yes.

#### [3.4a] A second child
**Say:** If a child is already linked to your account, Ad-Din lists them first. Pick that child and tap **Enroll Selected**, or tap **Add New Child** to enrol someone new.
**Show:** Inset (recorded separately with a parent who already has one child): the list "Select your child or add new." with the child's class and section. Spotlight "Enroll Selected" and "Add New Child".

### 4. Your details
#### [4.1] Parent/Guardian Details
**Say:** Step one is **Parent/Guardian Details**. Choose your **Relationship with child**: Father, Mother or Guardian. Then check your name, **Mobile Number** and **Email**.
**Show:** Progress bar on "Parent/Guardian Details". Pick "Mother". Fill Salutation, First Name, Last Name and Mobile Number.

#### [4.1a]
**Say:** Fields with a star must be filled in. Your mobile number is needed so the madrasah can reach you. Type the digits; a plus sign with your country code is fine too.
**Show:** Zoom on the stars next to Salutation, First Name, Last Name, Mobile Number and Email. Spotlight the typed mobile number.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Add your **Address**. Under **Spouse Details**, answer **Applicant is a Single Parent**. If you answer No, you can link your spouse's Ad-Din account or add their details.
**Show:** Fill the Address card. In Spouse Details click "No" briefly to show the extra question ("Does your spouse already have an Ad-Din account?…"), then choose "Yes" for this demo.

#### [4.2a] Your spouse and your address
**Say:** If your spouse already uses Ad-Din, type their GUID, their personal ID in the app, and tap **Fetch**. Video 00.5 shows where to find it. Under **Same for entire family**, choose **Yes** if your child lives at this address too.
**Show:** Cut-in (Single Parent No, spouse has an account Yes): spotlight the "Spouse GUID" box and "Fetch". Then back on this take, spotlight "Same for entire family" with "Yes" selected.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** If the madrasah asks for parent documents, upload them under **Documents**. When your details are complete, tap **Save & Next**.
**Show:** Scroll past the Documents card (no parent documents in this demo). Click "Save & Next".

### 5. Your child's details
#### [5.1] Child Details
**Say:** Step two is **Child Details**. Enter your child's name. Your child gets their own Ad-Din login, so give them a **Username** and an **Email** as well.
**Show:** Progress bar on "Child Details". Fill Salutation, First Name "Musa", Last Name "Malik", Username and Email.

#### [5.1a] Choosing a username
**Say:** A username needs at least three characters: letters, numbers, dots, underscores or hyphens, with no spaces. A green tick shows it's free. If you see a cross, someone already has it, so try another.
**Show:** Type "musa_malik" in Username; zoom on the green tick beside it.

#### [5.2]
**Say:** Add the **Date of Birth**, and anything teachers should know, such as **Allergies** or a **Special Note**. For Hifz, you can say how much your child has memorised.
**Show:** Pick a date of birth, type an allergy. Spotlight "Quran Type Preference (13 Line vs 15 Line)" and "Total Existing Quran Juz/Para Memorized".

#### [5.2a]
**Say:** For the amount memorised, type a number only, such as 5 or 5.5, without the word Juz. **Medical Card No.** and **Date Joined** are optional.
**Show:** Type "2" in "Total Existing Quran Juz/Para Memorized". Spotlight "Medical Card No." and "Date Joined", left empty.

#### [5.3] Media consent
**Say:** Under **Media Consent**, choose Yes or No. Yes lets the madrasah share your child's photos and work on school platforms. The info icon shows the details.
**Show:** Hover the info icon: "Student Media Release Consent" panel. Choose "Yes".

#### [5.4] Documents and questions
**Say:** Upload the files the madrasah asked for under **Documents**, and answer any questions under **Other details**.
**Show:** In Documents, click "Choose file" and pick the sample PDF for "Birth Certificate". In Other details, pick "English" for "Language spoken at home".

#### [5.4a]
**Say:** You can upload a PDF, a photo or an Office document. If a required document is missing, Ad-Din won't continue until you add it. A photo of the paper taken with your phone is fine.
**Show:** Zoom on the uploaded "Birth Certificate" row with the file name.

#### [5.5] Emergency contact
**Say:** Add someone to call in an emergency under **Emergency Contacts**, then tap **Save & Next**.
**Show:** Fill one emergency contact (Salutation, Full Name, Mobile Number). Spotlight "Add Photo" and "Add another emergency contact". Click "Save & Next".
**Caption:** Add an emergency contact, then tap Save & Next.

#### [5.5a]
**Say:** Choose someone other than you, such as a grandparent or a neighbour, so the madrasah has a second person to call. **Add another emergency contact** adds more.
**Show:** Card: "Emergency contact: someone other than the parents".

### 6. Review and submit
#### [6.1] Review and Submit
**Say:** Step three, **Review and Submit**, shows everything together. Tap **Edit** on any card to fix a detail.
**Show:** Progress bar on "Review and Submit". Scroll past the selected program, "Parent Details" and the child card. Spotlight an "Edit" link.

#### [6.1a]
**Say:** On the program card, tap **Remove** to drop a program, or **Add more course** to add another. **Previous** takes you back a step.
**Show:** Spotlight "✕ Remove" and "Add more course" on the selected program card, then "Previous" at the bottom. Don't click.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Open **Parent/Guardian Declaration & Agreement** and read it. Then tick **I agree and accept.**
**Show:** Expand the agreement, scroll the text briefly, click "I agree and accept.": the box is ticked.

#### [6.3]
**Say:** For a copy for your records, tap **Download PDF**. Then tap **Submit Enrollment**.
**Show:** Spotlight "Download PDF", then click "Submit Enrollment". Toast "Enrollment successful!"; the home page opens.

### 7. After you submit
#### [7.1] Check your application
**Say:** Ad-Din sends you an email and a push notification to confirm your application arrived. Your madrasah then reviews it, and Ad-Din lets you know when they approve or decline it. If you hear nothing, contact the madrasah.
**Show:** Home page. A notification arrives in the inbox (demo).

#### [7.2]
**Say:** To check on it, open **Madrasah / School**, then **Signup for a Program**. **Enrollments** lists each application and its status. If one is declined, you'll see why, and can tap **Review & Resubmit**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Madrasah / School → Signup for a Program. Spotlight the Enrollments card: Musa Malik, Weekend Quran, Pending.
**Caption:** To check on it: Madrasah / School → Signup for a Program → Enrollments. A declined application shows why, with Review & Resubmit.

#### [7.2a] Change it while you wait
**Say:** While it's still pending, you can correct it. Tap **Edit**, then **Update Parent** or **Update Child**. Once approved, the card shows **Approved** with the section and teacher.
**Show:** On Musa's card, click "Edit"; spotlight "Update Parent" and "Update Child"; close the menu. Inset: an approved card with Section, Teacher and "Approved".

#### [7.2b] Costs
**Say:** Enrolling through Ad-Din costs you nothing. Any fees are between you and your madrasah.
**Show:** Card: "Enrolling through Ad-Din: free · Fees: ask your madrasah".

#### [7.3] Next
**Say:** Once your child is enrolled, see video 14.1: Your parent home in the app, and video 14.2: Check attendance and report an absence.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "14.1 Your parent home in the app" and "14.2 Check attendance and report an absence".
