# 05.8 Your public program pages

## Goal
Know what parents and your community see about your programs, teachers and timetables, and where to update each part.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Program Details and Subjects, Staff List, Program Timetables: what parents see
- why: families decide before they enrol; check the pages after each change
- program page: tap the picture to see it larger; the description hint as a guide
- subjects: Search subjects, card and list view, "No teacher assigned" and "No syllabus"; the edit pencil for admins
- Staff List shows teachers, with their profile picture; Search
- timetable: tap a period for its subject, teacher and time

## Demo data
- Demo madrasah "Maple Grove Madrasah"; the demo admin has Premium
- Class "Hifz Boys" with a program description and cover picture (from 05.3), subjects "Quran Hifz" (with syllabus, two teachers), "Tajweed" (no syllabus) and "Islamic Studies"
- Teachers "Bilal Hussain" (with a short biography and profile picture) and "Fatima Siddiqui" (no biography)
- A fourth subject "Seerah" in Hifz Boys with no teacher yet, so it shows "No teacher assigned"
- The Term 1 timetable for "Hifz Boys - Hifz A" (from 05.6)
- A second browser signed in as a parent whose home madrasah is Maple Grove Madrasah, for the parent's view

## Script

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** In this video for madrasah admins, you'll see the program pages parents and your community browse before they enrol, and where each part comes from.
**Show:** Title card: "Your public program pages".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your classes, subjects and timetable are already set up. Everything here fills in from them.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

#### [1.2a] Why it matters
**Say:** Many parents choose a program before they enrol. These pages tell them what is taught, by whom and when, without a phone call to the office. So check them after you change a class, a subject or a timetable.
**Show:** Back on the opening card; then a quick cut of the "Hifz Boys" program page as a parent sees it.

### 2. Where families find them
#### [2.1]
**Say:** Families find your programs under **Madrasah / School** in the sidebar: **Program Details and Subjects**, **Signup for a Program**, **Staff List** and **Program Timetables**. Anyone who chose your madrasah as their home madrasah sees them, without Ad-Din Premium.
**Show:** Parent's browser (side panel: "Signed in as: a parent"). Sidebar → Madrasah / School, expanded (it also lists Introduction & Services, covered elsewhere). Spotlight the four pages.

### 3. Program details and subjects
#### [3.1] Pick a program
**Say:** Open **Program Details and Subjects**, then pick a program under **Program Name**.
**Show:** Click "Program Details and Subjects". Pick "Hifz Boys".

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Parents see the cover picture and the **Program Description**. These come from the class's program page.
**Show:** The cover and "Program Description". Scroll slowly.

#### [3.2a]
**Say:** Parents can tap the picture to see it larger. When you write the description, follow the hint on the page: say what the program covers, how it is graded and what students need to bring.
**Show:** Click the cover; it opens larger; close it. Quick cut, admin's browser, on a class with no description: zoom on "Tell parents what this program covers, how it is graded and what students need to bring."

#### [3.3]
**Say:** Below, **Subjects** lists each subject with its book, its teachers and a **Syllabus** link.
**Show:** Scroll to Subjects. Spotlight "Quran Hifz": the book line, the two teacher avatars, the Syllabus link.

#### [3.3a]
**Say:** Parents can search the subjects, and switch between cards and a list. A subject without a teacher or a syllabus shows **No teacher assigned** or **No syllabus**, so families can see what's missing too.
**Show:** Type "Taj" in "Search subjects, textbooks or teachers", then clear it. Click "List view", then "Card view". Spotlight "No syllabus" on "Tajweed" and "No teacher assigned" on "Seerah".

#### [3.4] Keep it up to date
**Say:** As an admin, you see **Edit** on the program, which opens the class so you can change it. **Needs syllabus** shows the subjects still missing one.
**Show:** Admin's browser (side panel: "Signed in as: Admin"), same page. Spotlight "Edit". Click "Needs syllabus": "Tajweed" shows.

#### [3.4a]
**Say:** The pencil on a subject opens it, so you can add the missing syllabus or teacher straight away, as video 05.5 shows.
**Show:** Spotlight the pencil on "Tajweed". Click "All" to show every subject again.

### 4. Staff list
#### [4.1]
**Say:** **Staff List** shows your teachers. Families tap a teacher to read their **Biography**.
**Show:** Parent's browser (side panel: "Signed in as: a parent"). Madrasah / School → Staff List. Click "Bilal Hussain"; the Biography panel shows.

#### [4.1a]
**Say:** The list shows your teachers, with the profile picture each teacher added. With many teachers, families can type a name in **Search**.
**Show:** Spotlight Bilal's picture and Fatima's placeholder picture. Type "Fat" in Search, then clear it.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Teachers write their own biography in **User Profile**, under **Personal info**. If it's empty, families see Not Available.
**Show:** Click "Fatima Siddiqui": "Not Available." Quick cut: User Profile → Personal info, spotlight "Biography".

### 5. Program timetables
#### [5.1] By class
**Say:** **Program Timetables** shows the weekly routine. On **View By Class**, pick the **Class**, **Section** and **Term**.
**Show:** Madrasah / School → Program Timetables. Pick "Hifz Boys", "Hifz A", "Term 1". The week grid appears.

#### [5.1a]
**Say:** Tap a period to see its subject, teacher and time under **Scheduled Items**.
**Show:** Click Monday Period 1: the Scheduled Items panel shows "Quran Hifz", "Bilal Hussain" and the time. Close it.

#### [5.2] By teacher
**Say:** **View By Teacher** shows one teacher's whole teaching week.
**Show:** Click "View By Teacher". Pick "Bilal Hussain" and "Term 1"; the Teacher Schedule appears.

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Done
**Say:** That's what families see about your programs. When they're ready, **Signup for a Program** takes them to your enrolment form.
**Show:** Spotlight "Signup for a Program" in the sidebar.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Next, video 06.1: Prepare online enrolment, and video 06.2: Enrol your child online, which shows the steps a parent goes through.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "06.1 Prepare online enrolment" and "06.2 Enrol your child online".
