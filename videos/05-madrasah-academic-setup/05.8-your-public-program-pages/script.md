# 05.8 Your public program pages

## Goal
Know what parents and your community see about your programs, teachers and timetables, and where to update each part.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Program Details and Subjects, Staff List, Program Timetables: what parents see

## Demo data
- Demo madrasah "Al-Noor Madrasah"; the demo admin has Premium
- Class "Hifz Boys" with a program description and cover picture (from 05.3), subjects "Quran Hifz" (with syllabus, two teachers), "Tajweed" (no syllabus) and "Islamic Studies"
- Teachers "Ibrahim Yusuf" (with a short biography and profile picture) and "Amina Saleh" (no biography)
- The Term 1 timetable for "Hifz Boys - Hifz A" (from 05.6)
- A second browser signed in as a parent whose home madrasah is Al-Noor Madrasah, for the parent's view

## Script

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Parents and community members who choose your madrasah can browse your programs before they enrol. This video shows those pages, and where their content comes from.
**Show:** Title card: "Your public program pages".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your classes, subjects and timetable are already set up. Everything here fills in from them.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

### 2. Where families find them
#### [2.1]
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Madrasah / School**. Four of its pages show families your programs: **Program Details and Subjects**, **Signup for a Program**, **Staff List** and **Program Timetables**.
**Show:** Parent's browser. Sidebar → Madrasah / School, expanded (it also lists Introduction & Services, covered elsewhere). Spotlight the four pages.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Anyone whose home madrasah is yours sees these pages. They don't need Ad-Din Premium.
**Show:** Hold on the expanded menu.

### 3. Program details and subjects
#### [3.1] Pick a program
**Say:** Open **Program Details and Subjects**, then pick a program under **Program Name**.
**Show:** Click "Program Details and Subjects". Pick "Hifz Boys".

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Parents see the cover picture and the **Program Description**. These come from the class's program page.
**Show:** The cover and "Program Description". Scroll slowly.

#### [3.3]
**Say:** Below, **Subjects** lists each subject with its book, its teachers and a **Syllabus** link.
**Show:** Scroll to Subjects. Spotlight "Quran Hifz": the book line, the two teacher avatars, the Syllabus link.

#### [3.4] Keep it up to date
**Say:** As an admin, you see **Edit** on the program, which opens the class so you can change it. **Needs syllabus** shows the subjects still missing one.
**Show:** Admin's browser, same page. Spotlight "Edit". Click "Needs syllabus": "Tajweed" shows.

### 4. Staff list
#### [4.1]
**Say:** **Staff List** shows your teachers. Families tap a teacher to read their **Biography**.
**Show:** Parent's browser. Madrasah / School → Staff List. Click "Ibrahim Yusuf"; the Biography panel shows.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Teachers write their own biography in **User Profile**, under **Personal info**. If it's empty, families see Not Available.
**Show:** Click "Amina Saleh": "Not Available." Quick cut: User Profile → Personal info, spotlight "Biography".

### 5. Program timetables
#### [5.1] By class
**Say:** **Program Timetables** shows the weekly routine. On **View By Class**, pick the **Class**, **Section** and **Term**.
**Show:** Madrasah / School → Program Timetables. Pick "Hifz Boys", "Hifz A", "Term 1". The week grid appears.

#### [5.2] By teacher
**Say:** **View By Teacher** shows one teacher's whole teaching week.
**Show:** Click "View By Teacher". Pick "Ibrahim Yusuf" and "Term 1"; the Teacher Schedule appears.

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Done
**Say:** That's what families see about your programs. When they're ready, **Signup for a Program** takes them to your enrolment form.
**Show:** Spotlight "Signup for a Program" in the sidebar.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Next, prepare online enrolment, and see the enrolment steps a parent goes through.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "06.1 Prepare online enrolment" and "06.2 Enrol your child online".
