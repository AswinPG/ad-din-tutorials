# 05.2 Create academic terms and grade sets

## Goal
Add your academic terms, keep the right ones enabled, and build the grade sets your classes are marked with.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Terms Management (Add Term, enable it), grade sets
- why: timetables, activities, report cards and attendance reports are organised by term; grade sets turn marks into grades
- the term list: columns, Select term to edit; naming and dates good practice; adding a term sends no message
- Delete Term or Disable Instead in the Confirm Delete pop-up
- the Default grade set (A+ to F, marks 0 to 100); Edit, Delete and View Grades on a grade set
- grade ranges without gaps (decimals), Edit, Delete, Export and View Grade Sets on the grade list

## Demo data
- Demo madrasah "Maple Grove Madrasah"; the demo admin has Premium
- One older term already in the list (for example "Term 3"), enabled, to disable on camera
- New term added on camera: "Term 1", with a start and end date in the coming months
- Grade Sets: the default grade set (shown in blue) and no others; "Hifz Grades" added on camera
- Grades added on camera in "Hifz Grades": "Mumtaz" (point 4, marks 90 to 100) and "Jayyid Jiddan" (point 3, marks 80 to 89.99)
- The Default grade set's grades (A+ 95 to 100 down to F 0 to 34.99), as every madrasah gets them

## Script

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** This video is for madrasah admins. You'll add your academic terms and build the grade sets used to mark students.
**Show:** Title card: "Create academic terms and grade sets".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** You need to be a Madrasah Admin with Ad-Din Premium. Start with terms, because each timetable is built for one term.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

#### [1.2a] Why it matters
**Say:** A term is one part of your school year. Ad-Din organises timetables, activities, report cards and attendance reports by term. A grade set turns a mark, such as 92, into a grade your parents understand.
**Show:** Back on the opening card; then a quick cut of the Term box on the Build Schedule tab (Admin Portal → Class Routine), with "Term 3" in it.

### 2. Open Terms Management
#### [2.1] The term list
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Admin Portal**, then **Terms Management**. The **Terms** page lists each term with its start date, end date and status.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Terms Management. The Terms page with "Term 3" in the list.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Enabled terms show in green and come first.
**Show:** Zoom on the Status column: "Enabled" in green.

#### [2.2a]
**Say:** Tap a column heading, such as **Start Date**, to sort the list. As the hint says, select a term to edit it.
**Show:** Click the "Start Date" heading; the order changes. Click it again. Spotlight "Select term to edit".

### 3. Add a term
#### [3.1] Add Term
**Say:** Tap **Add Term**. Give the term a **Name** your teachers and parents will recognise, such as Term 1.
**Show:** Click "Add Term"; the "Add Term" sheet slides up. Type "Term 1" in Name.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Pick the **Start** and **End** dates. If the end comes before the start, the boxes turn red.
**Show:** Pick a start date, then an end date before it: both boxes turn red. Fix the end date.

#### [3.3]
**Say:** Tap **Add Term**. The new term is enabled straight away and appears in the list.
**Show:** Click "Add Term" in the sheet. A confirmation message; "Term 1" shows as Enabled.

#### [3.3a] Good practice
**Say:** Add every term of the year at the start, so teachers can plan ahead. Make sure the dates don't overlap, because Ad-Din doesn't check that for you. Adding a term doesn't send a message to anyone.
**Show:** The list with "Term 1"; spotlight its Start and End dates next to those of "Term 3".

#### [3.3b]
**Say:** If you change your mind before saving, tap **Cancel** or the cross to close the sheet.
**Show:** Click "Add Term"; spotlight "Cancel" and the close cross; click "Cancel".

### 4. Enable or disable a term
#### [4.1] Edit a term
**Say:** To change a term, tap its row. In **Edit Term** you can fix the name or dates, and set the **Status** to **Enabled** or **Disabled**.
**Show:** Click the "Term 3" row; the "Edit Term" sheet opens. Spotlight the Status box.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Only enabled terms can be chosen for a timetable. When a term is over and its timetable is copied to the next term, set it to **Disabled**, then tap **Edit Term** to save. It moves down the list, in red.
**Show:** Choose "Disabled", click "Edit Term". "Term 3" moves below "Term 1", shown in red.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** Avoid deleting old terms. If you tap **Delete**, Ad-Din warns that the term's history will be lost, and offers **Disable Instead**.
**Show:** Open "Term 3", click "Delete". The "Confirm Delete" pop-up; spotlight "Disable Instead". Close it without deleting.

#### [4.3a]
**Say:** **Delete Term** removes the term for good, and this can't be undone. **Disable Instead** does the same as setting the status to **Disabled**: the term and its history stay, and it can't be chosen for new timetables.
**Show:** Replay the pop-up: spotlight "Delete Term", then "Disable Instead". Click the cross to close it.

### 5. Build a grade set
#### [5.1] Grade Sets
**Say:** Now the grades. In the **Admin Portal**, open **Grade**. The **Grade Sets** page lists your sets. The default set is shown in blue and can't be edited.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Grade. The Grade Sets page; spotlight the default set in blue.

#### [5.1a] The Default set
**Say:** Every madrasah starts with this set, called Default. It has letter grades from A+ down to F, for marks from 0 to 100. Use it as it is, or make your own set, for example with Arabic grade names.
**Show:** Click "Default"; its grades list opens: A+, A, A-, down to F, with Mark From and Mark To. Click "View Grade Sets" to go back.

#### [5.2]
**Say:** To make your own, tap **Add**, type a **Grade Set Name**, and tap **Save**.
**Show:** Click "Add". The "Add Grade Set" sheet; type "Hifz Grades"; click "Save". It appears in the list.

#### [5.2a]
**Say:** Select a set to use the buttons above the list: **Edit** to rename it, **Delete** to remove it, and **View Grades** to open it. Before you delete a set, check that no class still uses it.
**Show:** Select "Hifz Grades"; spotlight "Edit", "Delete" and "View Grades". Clear the selection.

#### [5.3] Add grades
**Say:** Tap the set's name to open it, then tap **Add**. Enter the **Grade Name**, its **Grade Point**, a number such as 4, and the marks it covers, from **Mark From** to **Mark Upto**.
**Show:** Click "Hifz Grades"; the Grade page opens. Click "Add". In "Add Grade" type "Mumtaz", 4, 90, 100.

#### [5.4]
**Say:** Add a **Comment** if you like, then tap **Save**. Repeat for each grade in the set.
**Show:** Type a short comment; click "Save". Add "Jayyid Jiddan", 3, 80, 89.99 the same way. Both rows show in the list.

#### [5.4a] Leave no gaps
**Say:** Make the ranges meet, with no gaps and no overlaps, so every mark has one grade. The Default set uses ranges such as 87 to 94.99, so a mark of 89.5 still fits. Do the same: here, 80 to 89.99.
**Show:** Zoom on the Mark From and Mark To columns: 80 to 89.99, then 90 to 100.
**Caption:** Make the ranges meet, with no gaps or overlaps. For example, 80 to 89.99, then 90 to 100.

#### [5.4b]
**Say:** To fix a grade, select it and tap **Edit**. **Delete** removes it, after you confirm. **Export** downloads the list, and **View Grade Sets** takes you back to your sets.
**Show:** Select "Jayyid Jiddan"; spotlight "Edit", "Delete", "Export" and "View Grade Sets". Clear the selection.

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Done
**Say:** Your terms and grade sets are ready. You'll choose a grade set for each class when you create it.
**Show:** Quick cut: the Terms list, then the Hifz Grades list.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Next, video 05.3: Create classes.
**Show:** End card with one tile: "05.3 Create classes".
