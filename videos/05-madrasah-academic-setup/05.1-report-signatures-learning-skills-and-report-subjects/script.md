# 05.1 Report signatures and learning skills

## Goal
Choose who signs your madrasah's report cards, make sure their signatures are in Ad-Din, and set the learning skills teachers rate on report cards.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- Ad-Din Premium, as a Madrasah Admin
- Your madrasah's details set up (03.1)
- Your teachers and admins added to the madrasah (03.4)

## Covers
- Manage Madrasah: principal, vice-principal and council signatures
- Learning Skill
- why: report cards print the principal's name and signature, set once for every card
- which role prints where: Academic School Principal on every report card, Academic School Council Chair on the grade 9 to 12 card
- the council chair saves only with an Academic School Principal or Vice Principal chosen
- the Details tab Save needs the tab's required boxes, such as Email; no message is sent
- signature pad: Clear and Cancel; change or remove a signature later
- teachers sign the same way; Add digital signature when report cards are made (pointer to 12.2)
- learning skills: why use them, descriptions print on the card, good practice

## Demo data
- Demo madrasah "Maple Grove Madrasah"; the demo account is its Madrasah Admin; the madrasah's details are complete, including Email (the Madrasah/School Details tab won't save without it)
- Teachers and admins in the madrasah, for example "Bilal Hussain" (to be Madrasah Principal) and "Fatima Siddiqui" (to be Madrasah Vice Principal), plus two more teachers
- All five principal and council boxes start on "Select Staff"
- After [3.3]: "Bilal Hussain" as Academic School Principal and Madrasah Principal, "Fatima Siddiqui" as Madrasah Vice Principal; the other two Academic rows on "Select Staff"
- For the cut-ins in [1.2a] and [4.3b]: a demo report card (for example Maryam Rahman's Term 1 card) with the principal's and the teacher's names and signatures; for [5.2a], a demo Islamic Studies Provincial report card showing the Learning Skills and Work Habits box
- "Bilal Hussain" signed in on a second browser for chapter 4, with no signature saved yet
- No learning skills saved yet, so Load Default fills the list on camera

## Script

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** In this video for madrasah admins, you'll choose who signs your report cards, make sure their signatures are in Ad-Din, and set the learning skills that teachers rate on report cards.
**Show:** Title card: "Report signatures and learning skills".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your teachers and admins are already added to the madrasah. They're the people you'll choose from.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

#### [1.2a] Why it matters
**Say:** Report cards print the principal's name and, when they have added one, their signature. You choose the signers once, and every report card your teachers make uses them.
**Show:** Cut-in: the signature area at the bottom of the demo report card, names and signatures visible. Back to the opening card.

### 2. Open your madrasah's settings
#### [2.1] Organization Management
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Admin Portal**, then **Organization Management**. Find your madrasah and tap **Manage**. The **Manage Madrasah** page opens on the **Madrasah/School Details** tab.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Organization Management. The "Organisation Management" page; click "Manage" on "Maple Grove Madrasah". The Manage Madrasah page opens; spotlight the "Madrasah/School Details" tab.

### 3. Choose who signs
#### [3.1] The five roles
**Say:** Scroll down to **Other Organization Services / Settings**. Here you choose the people who sign your report cards.
**Show:** Scroll to the card "Other Organization Services / Settings".

#### [3.2]
**Say:** There are five roles: **Academic School Principal**, **Academic School Vice Principal**, **Academic School Council Chair**, **Madrasah Principal** and **Madrasah Vice Principal**.
**Show:** Spotlight each row as it is named.
**Caption:** Five roles: Academic School Principal, Vice Principal and Council Chair; Madrasah Principal and Vice Principal.

#### [3.2a] The name on report cards
**Say:** The **Academic School Principal** is the name and signature printed on your report cards, for religious and academic classes alike. So choose this role even if your madrasah teaches only Quran and Islamic studies.
**Show:** Spotlight the "Academic School Principal" row.

#### [3.3]
**Say:** Each list shows your teachers and admins. Pick the right person for each role, or leave **Select Staff** if your madrasah doesn't have that role.
**Show:** Open "Academic School Principal" and pick "Bilal Hussain". Pick "Bilal Hussain" again for "Madrasah Principal", and "Fatima Siddiqui" for "Madrasah Vice Principal". Leave "Academic School Vice Principal" and "Academic School Council Chair" on "Select Staff".

#### [3.3a] Council chair
**Say:** The **Academic School Council Chair** prints on the grade 9 to 12 report card. It's saved only when an Academic School Principal or Vice Principal is also chosen, so choose the principal first.
**Show:** Spotlight "Academic School Council Chair", then the "Academic School Principal" row with Bilal Hussain.

#### [3.4] Save
**Say:** Tap **Save** at the bottom of the page. Ad-Din saves the whole tab and takes you back to your list of organisations.
**Show:** Scroll to the bottom; click "Save". A confirmation message, then the Organisation Management page.

#### [3.4a]
**Say:** This **Save** saves everything on the tab, so its required boxes must be filled, such as **Email**. If one is empty, Ad-Din asks you to fill it in and doesn't save. Saving doesn't send a message to anyone.
**Show:** Quick cut, before the save: the Email box empty and marked in red, with the toast "Please fill in the required fields." Then the filled Email box.
**Caption:** Save needs the tab's required boxes, such as Email. Saving sends no message.

### 4. Add the signatures
#### [4.1] Each person signs once
**Say:** Each person you chose adds their own signature, once, in their profile. They open **User Profile** from the profile menu. It opens on the **Personal info** tab.
**Show:** Second browser, signed in as Bilal Hussain (side panel: "Signed in as: Bilal Hussain, a teacher"). Profile menu → User Profile. The "Personal info" tab.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Under **Signature**, they tap the pen and sign in the box with a mouse or a finger.
**Show:** The "Signature" section is already open (don't click its header, that collapses it); spotlight "Used to digitally sign student report cards". Click the pen; the "Sign here" pad opens. Draw a signature.

#### [4.2a]
**Say:** If the signature doesn't look right, tap **Clear** and sign again. **Cancel** closes the pad without saving.
**Show:** Draw a rough line; click "Clear"; the pad empties. Draw the signature again. Spotlight "Cancel" without clicking it.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** Then tap **Save**. The signature now shows in their profile, ready for report cards.
**Show:** Click "Save". The pad closes and the signature appears in the Signature box.

#### [4.3a] Change it later
**Say:** To change the signature later, tap the pen and sign again. To remove it, tap the cross on the signature, then tap **Update**.
**Show:** Spotlight the pen, then the cross on the signature box, then "Update". Don't click them.

#### [4.3b] Teachers sign too
**Say:** Ask your class teachers to add their signatures the same way. Report cards can carry the teacher's signature beside the principal's. Teachers tick **Add digital signature** when they make the cards, as video 12.2 shows.
**Show:** Cut-in: the signature area of the demo report card, the teacher's signature beside the principal's.

### 5. Set the learning skills
#### [5.1] Services tab
**Say:** Back in **Organization Management**, tap **Manage** again and open the **Services** tab.
**Show:** Admin browser (side panel: "Signed in as: Admin"). Organization Management → Manage on "Maple Grove Madrasah" → click the "Services" tab.

#### [5.2]
**Say:** Scroll to **Madrasah Learning Skill and Work Habits for Reports**. These skills appear on the Islamic Studies Provincial report card, where teachers rate each student from Excellent to Needs Improvement.
**Show:** Scroll to the card. Hover the info icon; the tooltip shows the E, G, S, N scale. Zoom on it.

#### [5.2a] Why use them
**Say:** If your madrasah uses that report card, set the skills here once. Each card then lists every skill with its description, and the teacher chooses a grade for each one.
**Show:** Cut-in: the "Learning Skills and Work Habits" box of the demo Islamic Studies Provincial report card: skill names, descriptions and grades. Back to the Services tab.

#### [5.3]
**Say:** Tap **Load Default** for six ready-made skills, such as Responsibility, Organization and Collaboration. Loading the defaults replaces the list.
**Show:** Click "Load Default". Six rows appear: Responsibility, Organization, Independent Work, Collaboration, Initiative, Self-regulation.

#### [5.4]
**Say:** Change any name or description to suit your madrasah. Tap the plus to add a skill, or the cross to remove one. Then tap **Save**.
**Show:** Edit one description. Click the plus: an empty row appears; remove it with its cross. Click "Save". Toast: "Skill set updated successfully".

#### [5.4a] Good practice
**Say:** The descriptions print on every card, so keep them short and in words parents understand. Remove empty rows before you save. If you tap **Load Default** again later, your own changes are replaced.
**Show:** Zoom on the edited description. Spotlight "Load Default".

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Done
**Say:** Your report cards now show who signs them and which skills teachers rate.
**Show:** Back on Madrasah/School Details: the chosen names in the Academic School Principal, Madrasah Principal and Madrasah Vice Principal rows.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Next, video 05.2: Create academic terms and grade sets. When you're ready for report cards, video 12.1: Map a report card template.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "05.2 Create academic terms and grade sets" and "12.1 Map a report card template".
