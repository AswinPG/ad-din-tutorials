# 05.1 Report signatures, learning skills and report subjects

## Goal
Choose who signs your madrasah's report cards, make sure their signatures are in Ad-Din, and set the learning skills teachers rate on report cards.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- Ad-Din Premium, as a Madrasah Admin
- Your madrasah's details set up (03.1)
- Your teachers and admins added to the madrasah (03.4)

## Covers
- Manage Madrasah: principal, vice-principal and council signatures
- Learning Skill
- Subject for report

## Demo data
- Demo madrasah "Al-Noor Madrasah"; the demo account is its Madrasah Admin; the madrasah's details are complete, including Email (the Madrasah/School Details tab won't save without it)
- Teachers and admins in the madrasah, for example "Ibrahim Yusuf" (to be Madrasah Principal) and "Amina Saleh" (to be Madrasah Vice Principal), plus two more teachers
- All five principal and council boxes start on "Select Staff"
- "Ibrahim Yusuf" signed in on a second browser for chapter 4, with no signature saved yet
- No learning skills saved yet, so Load Default fills the list on camera

## Script

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Report cards in Ad-Din can show who leads your madrasah, with their signatures, and the learning skills each student is rated on. This video sets those up.
**Show:** Title card: "Report signatures, learning skills and report subjects".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your teachers and admins are already added to the madrasah. They're the people you'll choose from.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

### 2. Open your madrasah's settings
#### [2.1] Organization Management
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Admin Portal**, then **Organization Management**. Find your madrasah and tap **Manage**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Organization Management. The "Organisation Management" page; click "Manage" on "Al-Noor Madrasah".

#### [2.2]
**Say:** The **Manage Madrasah** page opens on the **Madrasah/School Details** tab.
**Show:** The Manage Madrasah page with its tabs. Spotlight "Madrasah/School Details".

### 3. Choose who signs
#### [3.1] The five roles
**Say:** Scroll down to **Other Organization Services / Settings**. Here you choose the people who sign your report cards.
**Show:** Scroll to the card "Other Organization Services / Settings".

#### [3.2]
**Say:** There are five roles: **Academic School Principal**, **Academic School Vice Principal**, **Academic School Council Chair**, **Madrasah Principal** and **Madrasah Vice Principal**.
**Show:** Spotlight each row as it is named.
**Caption:** Five roles: Academic School Principal, Vice Principal and Council Chair; Madrasah Principal and Vice Principal.

#### [3.3]
**Say:** Each list shows your teachers and admins. Pick the right person for each role, or leave **Select Staff** if a role doesn't apply to you.
**Show:** Open "Madrasah Principal" and pick "Ibrahim Yusuf". Pick "Amina Saleh" for "Madrasah Vice Principal". Leave the three Academic rows on "Select Staff".

#### [3.4] Save
**Say:** Tap **Save** at the bottom of the page. Ad-Din saves the whole tab and takes you back to your list of organisations.
**Show:** Scroll to the bottom; click "Save". A confirmation message, then the Organisation Management page.

### 4. Add the signatures
#### [4.1] Each person signs once
**Say:** The signature itself comes from each person's own profile. Ask your principal to open **User Profile** and stay on **Personal info**.
**Show:** Second browser, signed in as Ibrahim Yusuf. Profile menu → User Profile. The "Personal info" tab.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Under **Signature**, which is used to digitally sign student report cards, they tap the pen and sign in the box.
**Show:** The "Signature" section is already open (don't click its header, that collapses it); spotlight "Used to digitally sign student report cards". Click the pen; the "Sign here" pad opens. Draw a signature.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** Then tap **Save**. The signature now shows in their profile, ready for report cards.
**Show:** Click "Save". The pad closes and the signature appears in the Signature box.

### 5. Set the learning skills
#### [5.1] Services tab
**Say:** Back in **Organization Management**, tap **Manage** again and open the **Services** tab.
**Show:** Admin browser. Organization Management → Manage on "Al-Noor Madrasah" → click the "Services" tab.

#### [5.2]
**Say:** Scroll to **Madrasah Learning Skill and Work Habits for Reports**. These skills appear on the Islamic Studies Provincial report card, where teachers rate each student from Excellent to Needs Improvement.
**Show:** Scroll to the card. Hover the info icon; the tooltip shows the E, G, S, N scale. Zoom on it.

#### [5.3]
**Say:** Tap **Load Default** for six ready-made skills, such as Responsibility, Organization and Collaboration. Loading the defaults replaces the list.
**Show:** Click "Load Default". Six rows appear: Responsibility, Organization, Independent Work, Collaboration, Initiative, Self-regulation.

#### [5.4]
**Say:** Change any name or description to suit your madrasah. Tap the plus to add a skill, or the cross to remove one. Then tap **Save**.
**Show:** Edit one description. Click the plus: an empty row appears; remove it with its cross. Click "Save". Toast: "Skill set updated successfully".

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Done
**Say:** Your report cards now know who signs them and which skills teachers rate.
**Show:** Back on Madrasah/School Details: the two chosen names in the Madrasah Principal rows.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Next, create your academic terms and grade sets. When you're ready for report cards, the report card videos show how to map a template.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "05.2 Create academic terms and grade sets" and "12.1 Map a report card template".
