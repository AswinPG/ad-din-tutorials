# 05.4 Create class sections

## Goal
Add sections to a class, each with its own class teacher and capacity, and read a section's student list.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Sections
- Active and inactive students in a section
- why: split a class into groups, each with its own class teacher, timetable and attendance
- Search sections; More actions: Export and Import
- what the class teacher does (class attendance, pointer to 10.2); capacity is a guide, Over by
- naming tip, Cancel; adding a section sends no message
- Delete: only with no students and no alumni history; removes the section's timetable
- Search students, Medical and Note marks (pointer to 10.12); Remove students and Move to (pointer to 06.5)

## Demo data
- Demo madrasah "Maple Grove Madrasah"; the demo admin has Premium
- Class "Hifz Boys" (from 05.3) with no sections yet
- Teacher "Bilal Hussain" in the madrasah, to be the class teacher
- New section added on camera: "Hifz A", capacity 20
- Class "Maktab Level 1" with "Section A" (capacity 20, 15 students, one of them inactive, for example "Yahya Saleh") and "Section B" (capacity 20, 17 students, so it shows Nearly full)
- "Maktab Level 1" also has "Section C" (capacity 10, 12 students), so it shows "Over by 2"
- In Section A, one active student with a medical concern on file, so the list shows "Medical"
- Student contact details are demo data only

## Script

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** For madrasah admins: in this video you'll add sections to a class and look inside one. A section is a group of students in a class, with its own teacher and its own limit.
**Show:** Title card: "Create class sections".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your class is already created. Every class needs at least one section before students can join it.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

#### [1.2a] Why it matters
**Say:** Sections split a class into groups, for example a morning group and an evening group. Each section has its own class teacher, its own timetable and its own attendance.
**Show:** Back on the opening card; then a quick cut of the "Maktab Level 1" section cards.

### 2. Open Class Section
#### [2.1] Pick the class
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Admin Portal**, then **Class Section**. Under **Select Class**, choose your class.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Class Section. Pick "Hifz Boys" in Select Class.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** A new class has no sections yet. Tap **Add**.
**Show:** The message "No sections in this class yet". Click "Add".

### 3. Add a section
#### [3.1] Add Class Section
**Say:** In **Add Class Section**, the class is already filled in. Choose the **Class Teacher** who looks after this section.
**Show:** The "Add Class Section" window; "Hifz Boys" greyed out in Class. Pick "Bilal Hussain" in Class Teacher.

#### [3.1a] The class teacher
**Say:** The class teacher takes the section's daily class attendance, as video 10.2 shows. In the timetable, the class teacher can also be chosen for any of the section's subjects.
**Show:** Zoom on "Bilal Hussain" in Class Teacher.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Type a name under **Enter Class Section**, and set the **Student Capacity in this Section**. Every box is required.
**Show:** Type "Hifz A". Type 20 in the capacity box.

#### [3.2a] Good practice
**Say:** Use a name families will recognise, such as Morning or Hifz A. The capacity is a guide, not a hard limit: Ad-Din still lets you add more students, and then shows how many the section is over.
**Show:** Zoom on the name and capacity boxes.

#### [3.3]
**Say:** Tap **Save**. The new section appears as a card.
**Show:** Click "Save". A confirmation message; the "Hifz A" card shows "0 / 20" and "Open".

#### [3.3a]
**Say:** Adding a section doesn't send a message to anyone. If you open the window by mistake, tap **Cancel** or the cross to close it.
**Show:** Spotlight the "Hifz A" card. Click "Add" again; spotlight "Cancel" and the close cross; click "Cancel".

### 4. Read the section cards
#### [4.1] How full it is
**Say:** In a class with students, each card shows how many students a section has and how many it can hold. It says **Open** while there's room, and **Nearly full** when it's eighty percent full. If it's too full, it shows by how many.
**Show:** Switch Select Class to "Maktab Level 1". Spotlight "15 / 20 Open" on Section A, then "Nearly full" on Section B, then "Over by 2" on Section C.

#### [4.1a] Search and more
**Say:** In a class with many sections, type in **Search sections** to find one. The three dots beside **Add** hold **Export** and **Import**, to download your sections or bring them in from a file.
**Show:** Type "B" in Search sections; only Section B shows; clear it. Click the three dots: spotlight "Export" and "Import". Close the menu.

#### [4.2] Section options
**Say:** Tap the three dots on a card for more: **View students**, **Add students**, **Edit Class Section** to rename it or change the teacher or capacity, and **Delete**.
**Show:** Click the three dots on Section A. The options sheet; spotlight each option. Close it.

#### [4.2a] Deleting a section
**Say:** You can delete a section only when it has no students, and not if past students moved on from it. Deleting a section also deletes its timetable. Ad-Din asks you to confirm first.
**Show:** Click the three dots on Section A, then "Delete". The confirmation pop-up; click "No". Spotlight the student count on the card.
**Caption:** You can delete a section only when it has no students and no past students. Its timetable is deleted too.

### 5. Look inside a section
#### [5.1] The student list
**Say:** Tap a card to see its students, with each roll number and **Student ID**. Tap a student to see their contact details.
**Show:** Click "Section A"; the student list opens on the right. Click a student; the contact panel opens.

#### [5.1a] Find a student
**Say:** Type a name, roll number or ID in **Search students**. A student marked **Medical** or **Note** has a medical concern or a special note on file, so check it before class trips. Video 10.12 shows the contact details in full.
**Show:** Type "Zaid" in Search students, then clear it. Spotlight the "Medical" mark on the demo student.

#### [5.2] Active and inactive
**Say:** Students who are no longer active stay in the list, marked **Inactive**, so you can still see who was in the section. Contacts show for active students only.
**Show:** Scroll to "Yahya Saleh" with the "Inactive" badge. Click it: "Contacts and details show for active students only."

#### [5.3]
**Say:** To fill a section, use **Add students**. Video 06.5 shows how.
**Show:** Spotlight "Add students" at the top of the list.

#### [5.3a]
**Say:** **Remove students** lets you tap students, then **Remove** them from the section or **Move to** another section. Video 06.5 shows this too.
**Show:** Click "Remove students"; the hint "Tap students to select them, then choose Remove or Move below" shows. Click "Cancel".

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Done
**Say:** Your class now has its sections, each with a teacher and a capacity.
**Show:** Back to "Hifz Boys": the "Hifz A" card.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Next, video 05.5: Add subjects. To fill sections with students, see video 06.4: Add students and parents yourself, and video 06.5: Move students between sections.
**Show:** End card with three tiles: "05.5 Add subjects", "06.4 Add students and parents yourself" and "06.5 Move students between sections".
