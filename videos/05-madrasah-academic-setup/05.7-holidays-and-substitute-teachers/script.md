# 05.7 Holidays and substitute teachers

## Goal
Add the days your madrasah is closed, and let another teacher cover a teacher's subject or class attendance while they're away.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Holiday
- Delegations (subject-wise or class attendance)
- why: families know when you're closed; classes go on when a teacher is away
- holiday list: Edit, Delete, Export; good practice (one message per holiday)
- editing or deleting a holiday sends no new message (pointer to 07.3)
- a delegation works only between its dates, then moves to InActive; Edit, Delete and Export send no message
- parents and students also see delegations under Delegations

## Demo data
- Demo madrasah "Maple Grove Madrasah"; the demo admin has Premium
- One holiday already in the list; new holiday added on camera: "Eid al-Adha break", with a short description and a start and end date a few days apart
- Teachers "Bilal Hussain" (away) and "Fatima Siddiqui" (covering)
- Bilal Hussain teaches "Quran Hifz" in class "Hifz Boys", section "Hifz A"
- One older delegation already in the list, ended, so it shows under InActive
- For [6.1a]: a parent of a student in "Hifz A", signed in on a second browser
- New delegation added on camera: subject-wise (Quran Hifz, Hifz A) for one week, with the note "Covering while Ustadh Bilal is away"

## Script

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** This video is for madrasah admins. You'll mark the days your madrasah is closed, and give a teacher's work to another teacher while they're away.
**Show:** Title card: "Holidays and substitute teachers".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your classes, sections, subjects and teachers are already set up.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

#### [1.2a] Why it matters
**Say:** Families need to know when your madrasah is closed, and classes must go on when a teacher is ill or travelling. A holiday tells everyone at once. A delegation lets another teacher do the absent teacher's work in Ad-Din, for those dates only.
**Show:** Back on the opening card; then a quick cut of the Holiday list and the Delegation list.

### 2. Add a holiday
#### [2.1] The holiday list
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Admin Portal**, then **Holiday**. The list shows each holiday with its **Start Date**, **End Date** and **Description**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Holiday. The Holiday list with one holiday.

#### [2.1a]
**Say:** Select a holiday to **Edit** or **Delete** it. **Export** downloads the list.
**Show:** Select the existing holiday; spotlight "Edit" and "Delete". Clear the selection. Spotlight "Export".

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Tap **Add**. Enter a **Holiday Title** and a short **Description**, then pick the **Start Date** and **End Date**. For a single day, use the same date twice.
**Show:** Click "Add"; the "Add Holiday" window opens. Type "Eid al-Adha break" and a description. Pick the two dates.

#### [2.3]
**Say:** All four boxes are needed. Check the dates first: when you tap **Save**, Ad-Din sends an email and an app notification about the holiday to all your teachers, parents and students.
**Show:** Click "Save". A confirmation message; the holiday appears in the list.

#### [2.3a] Good practice
**Say:** Each holiday sends its own message, so add one when its dates are certain. If you edit or delete a holiday later, Ad-Din doesn't send another message. Tell families yourself, as video 07.3 shows.
**Show:** Select "Eid al-Adha break"; spotlight "Edit" and "Delete". Clear the selection.

#### [2.4]
**Say:** Teachers, parents and students see your holidays under **Holiday** in their own portals.
**Show:** Quick cut, signed in as a parent (side panel: "Signed in as: a parent"): Parent Portal → Holiday, with "Eid al-Adha break" in the list.

### 3. Start a delegation
#### [3.1] Delegations
**Say:** When a teacher is away, a substitute teacher can cover their work. In Ad-Din this is a delegation. In the **Admin Portal**, open **Delegations**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Delegations. The Delegation page with one older delegation.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Tap **Add**. Choose the teacher who is away under **From Teacher**, and the teacher covering for them under **To Teacher**. They must be two different people.
**Show:** Click "Add"; the "Add Delegation" window opens. Pick "Bilal Hussain" in From Teacher and "Fatima Siddiqui" in To Teacher.

### 4. Choose what they cover
#### [4.1] Scope
**Say:** Under **Scope**, pick one of two options. **Subject-wise** lets the covering teacher mark attendance for that subject, mark activities, and mark Quran Hifz assignments.
**Show:** Spotlight the two Scope options. Select "Subject-wise"; zoom on its description.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** **Class attendance** lets them take class attendance for the students in one section.
**Show:** Spotlight "Class attendance" and its description. Keep "Subject-wise" selected.

#### [4.3] Class, section and subject
**Say:** Choose the **Class** and **Class Section**. Only the absent teacher's classes are listed. For subject-wise cover, also choose the **Subject**.
**Show:** Pick "Hifz Boys", then "Hifz A", then "Quran Hifz".

### 5. Set the dates and save
#### [5.1]
**Say:** Pick the **Start Date** and **End Date** of the cover, and add a note under **Event Description** if you like. Tap **Save**. Ad-Din emails and notifies the students and parents in that section.
**Show:** Pick a one-week range. Type "Covering while Ustadh Bilal is away". Click "Save"; a confirmation message.

#### [5.1a] Only for those dates
**Say:** The covering teacher can do this work only between the start and end dates. After the end date, the delegation moves to the **InActive** list. If the teacher is away longer, edit the end date.
**Show:** Zoom on the Start Date and End Date of the new row.

#### [5.2]
**Say:** The delegation joins the list. Use the filter to show **Active** or **InActive** delegations, and **Edit** or **Delete** to change one.
**Show:** The new row: From Teacher, To Teacher, Class, Section, Subject, dates. Open the filter and pick "Active".

#### [5.2a]
**Say:** Editing or deleting a delegation doesn't send a message. **Export** downloads the list.
**Show:** Pick "InActive" in the filter: the older delegation shows. Pick "Active" again. Spotlight "Export".

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Done
**Say:** Your holidays are in place. Ad-Din doesn't send the covering teacher a message, so let them know. They see the delegation under **Delegations** in their **Teacher Portal**.
**Show:** Quick cut, signed in as Fatima Siddiqui (side panel: "Signed in as: Fatima Siddiqui, a teacher"): Teacher Portal → Delegations, showing the new delegation.

#### [6.1a]
**Say:** Parents and students in that section also see the delegation under **Delegations** in their own portals, so they know who is teaching.
**Show:** Quick cut, signed in as a parent (side panel: "Signed in as: a parent"): Parent Portal → Delegations, showing the new delegation.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Next, video 05.8: Your public program pages, to check what families see. To see how teachers take attendance, watch video 10.2: Take class attendance.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "05.8 Your public program pages" and "10.2 Take class attendance".
