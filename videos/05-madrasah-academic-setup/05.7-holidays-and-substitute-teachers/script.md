# 05.7 Holidays and substitute teachers

## Goal
Add the days your madrasah is closed, and let another teacher cover a teacher's subject or class attendance while they're away.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Holiday
- Delegations (subject-wise or class attendance)

## Demo data
- Demo madrasah "Al-Noor Madrasah"; the demo admin has Premium
- One holiday already in the list; new holiday added on camera: "Eid al-Adha break", with a short description and a start and end date a few days apart
- Teachers "Ibrahim Yusuf" (away) and "Amina Saleh" (covering)
- Ibrahim Yusuf teaches "Quran Hifz" in class "Hifz Boys", section "Hifz A"
- One older delegation already in the list
- New delegation added on camera: subject-wise (Quran Hifz, Hifz A) for one week, with the note "Covering while Ustadh Ibrahim is away"

## Script

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** This video covers two jobs: marking the days your madrasah is closed, and handing a teacher's work to another teacher while they're away.
**Show:** Title card: "Holidays and substitute teachers".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your classes, sections, subjects and teachers are already set up.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

### 2. Add a holiday
#### [2.1] The holiday list
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Admin Portal**, then **Holiday**. The list shows each holiday with its **Start Date**, **End Date** and **Description**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Holiday. The Holiday list with one holiday.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Tap **Add**. Enter a **Holiday Title** and a short **Description**, then pick the **Start Date** and **End Date**. For a single day, use the same date twice.
**Show:** Click "Add"; the "Add Holiday" window opens. Type "Eid al-Adha break" and a description. Pick the two dates.

#### [2.3]
**Say:** All four boxes are needed. If one is empty, Ad-Din asks you to fill in all the details. Tap **Save**.
**Show:** Click "Save". A confirmation message; the holiday appears in the list.

#### [2.4]
**Say:** Teachers, parents and students see your holidays under **Holiday** in their own portals.
**Show:** Quick cut: Parent Portal → Holiday, with "Eid al-Adha break" in the list.

### 3. Start a delegation
#### [3.1] Delegations
**Say:** When a teacher is away, give their work to someone else with a delegation. In the **Admin Portal**, open **Delegations**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Delegations. The Delegation page with one older delegation.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Tap **Add**. Choose the teacher who is away under **From Teacher**, and the teacher covering for them under **To Teacher**. They must be two different people.
**Show:** Click "Add"; the "Add Delegation" window opens. Pick "Ibrahim Yusuf" in From Teacher and "Amina Saleh" in To Teacher.

### 4. Choose what they cover
#### [4.1] Scope
**Say:** Under **Scope**, pick one of two options. **Subject-wise** lets the covering teacher mark attendance for that subject, mark activities, and mark Quran Hifz assignments.
**Show:** Spotlight the two Scope options. Select "Subject-wise"; zoom on its description.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** **Class attendance** lets them take class attendance for the students in one section.
**Show:** Spotlight "Class attendance" and its description. Keep "Subject-wise" selected.

#### [4.3] Class, section and subject
**Say:** Choose the **Class** and **Class Section**. Only the absent teacher's classes are listed. For subject-wise cover, also choose the **Subject**.
**Show:** Pick "Hifz Boys", then "Hifz A", then "Quran Hifz".

### 5. Set the dates and save
#### [5.1]
**Say:** Pick the **Start Date** and **End Date** of the cover, and add a note under **Event Description** if you like. Tap **Save**.
**Show:** Pick a one-week range. Type "Covering while Ustadh Ibrahim is away". Click "Save"; a confirmation message.

#### [5.2]
**Say:** The delegation joins the list. Use the filter to show **Active** or **InActive** delegations, and **Edit** or **Delete** to change one.
**Show:** The new row: From Teacher, To Teacher, Class, Section, Subject, dates. Open the filter and pick "Active".

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Done
**Say:** Your holidays are in place, and the covering teacher sees the delegation under **Delegations** in their **Teacher Portal**.
**Show:** Quick cut: Amina Saleh's Teacher Portal → Delegations, showing the new delegation.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Next, see how teachers take attendance. You can also check the program pages families see.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "10.2 Take class attendance" and "05.8 Your public program pages".
