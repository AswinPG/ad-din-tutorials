# 07.4 Class announcements

## Goal
Post photos and notes to your class as a teacher, and like, save and follow up on them as a parent.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- Class Announcements switched on for your madrasah (video 03.2)
- Teachers: a class section you teach
- Parents: a child in a class at the madrasah

## Covers
- Teachers post photo and note stories
- madrasah admins: Admin Portal → Class Announcements
- parents react
- Why use it; Change photo and Remove; Search students, Select all, Clear all
- The daily email and push to parents; what can't be edited; Delete and Keep it
- Parent filters (Class, Section, Teacher, Clear filters); full-screen photo with zoom and download

## Demo data
- Maple Grove Madrasah with Class Announcements switched on, and class "Hifz Class A" with section "Morning" and about six students; one student without media consent
- Demo teacher account "Bilal Hussain" (teacher of Hifz Class A · Morning) on the web
- Demo parent account: Yusuf Rahman with two children, Maryam in Hifz Class A and a second child, "Hamza Rahman", added to the world for this video (so the child tabs and All children show); shown in the app (phone frame)
- Admin shot: Yusuf Rahman as Admin
- Each account switch is labelled in the side panel: "Signed in as: teacher", "madrasah admin", "parent"
- A class photo on the recording machine (for example `hifz-circle.jpg`), with no recognisable children’s faces
- Two earlier announcements in the feed, one with a few likes and views
- The demo teacher teaches one subject in Hifz Class A ("Hifz"), so Message teacher ([6.3]) opens with the teacher and subject already picked
- Saving succeeds in the demo world

## Script

### 1. Moments from class
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** In this video for teachers and parents, you’ll post a photo or a note to your class with Class Announcements. Then you’ll see it as a parent: like it, save it and ask about it.
**Show:** Title card: "Class announcements".

#### [1.1a] Why use it
**Say:** Class Announcements let parents see what their children do in class, without group chats or sharing personal phone numbers. Only the section's students, their parents, and your teachers and admins see each post.
**Show:** Card: "Moments from class · No group chats · Only the class, parents and staff".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** A madrasah admin switches this feature on, on the **Services** tab of Organization Management, as video 03.2 shows. If it’s off, the page says **Class announcements are turned off**.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need. Cut-in: the Class Announcements switch on the Services tab.

### 2. Open Class Announcements
#### [2.1] Teacher
**Say:** As a teacher, open **Teacher Portal** in the sidebar, then **Class Announcements**. In the app, use the **Class Announcements** card on your home screen.
**Show:** Teacher account on the web (side panel "Signed in as: teacher"): Sidebar → Teacher Portal → Class Announcements. The feed with two earlier posts. Quick cut: phone frame, app home with the Class Announcements card ("View all", "New").

#### [2.2] Admin
**Say:** Madrasah admins open **Admin Portal**, then **Class Announcements**. They post in the same way, and can delete any post.
**Show:** Admin account (side panel "Signed in as: madrasah admin"): Sidebar → Admin Portal → Class Announcements; the same feed and the "New announcement" button.

### 3. Post a photo
#### [3.1] New announcement
**Say:** Tap **New announcement**. Under **Class & section**, choose the section you teach.
**Show:** Click "New announcement". In "Choose a section", pick "Hifz Class A · Morning".

#### [3.2] Photo
**Say:** Under **Type**, choose **Photo**, then tap **Choose a photo**. Pick a JPG or PNG, crop it, and tap **Save & Upload**.
**Show:** Type: Photo (selected). Click "Choose a photo" → "Choose Image" → `hifz-circle.jpg` → crop → "Save & Upload". The preview shows.

#### [3.3] Caption
**Say:** Add a **Caption** if you like, to say what’s happening.
**Show:** Type "Today’s Hifz circle: well done, everyone!" in Caption.

#### [3.3a]
**Say:** To use a different picture, tap **Change photo**, or **Remove** to clear it. If you tap **Post** without a photo, Ad-Din asks you to choose one.
**Show:** Spotlight "Change photo" and "Remove" under the preview; don't click.

### 4. Choose who sees it
#### [4.1] Share with
**Say:** Under **Share with**, **Whole class** shows it to every student in the section and their parents. **Tagged students only** shows it just to the students you tag, and their parents.
**Show:** Spotlight "Whole class" and "Tagged students only" with their hints.
**Caption:** Whole class: every student in the section and their parents. Tagged students only: just those students and their parents.

#### [4.2] Tag students
**Say:** To tag, choose **Tagged students only** and tick the students. A student without media consent can’t be tagged.
**Show:** Click "Tagged students only"; the student list appears. Tick two students; spotlight "No media consent — cannot be tagged" on one row.

#### [4.2a]
**Say:** Use **Search students** to find a name, or **Select all** and **Clear all** for the whole list. If you choose tagged students but tag nobody, Ad-Din asks you to tag at least one.
**Show:** Spotlight "Search students", "Select all", "Clear all" and the "2 selected" count.

#### [4.3] Post
**Say:** Tap **Post**. Your announcement appears at the top of the feed.
**Show:** Switch back to Whole class; click "Post". Message "Announcement posted."; the new card is first in the feed.

#### [4.3a] What parents get
**Say:** Parents aren't alerted for each post. Once a day, Ad-Din sends each parent one email and push notification listing the classes with announcements they haven't seen yet.
**Show:** Card: "Once a day: one email and push per parent, for posts they haven't seen".

### 5. Post a note, edit or delete
#### [5.1] Note
**Say:** For news without a picture, choose **Note** instead of Photo, and write your announcement.
**Show:** "New announcement" → section → Type: Note → type a short reminder about tomorrow’s revision test → "Post".

#### [5.2] Edit or delete
**Say:** To change your post, tap the three dots and choose **Edit**. You can change the caption and who it’s shared with. **Delete** removes it for everyone.
**Show:** Click "…" on the photo post: Edit, Delete. Click "Edit"; spotlight the locked section and the hint; click "Cancel".

#### [5.2a]
**Say:** The section, type, photo or text of a posted announcement can't be changed. To fix those, delete it and post again. **Delete** asks first, and you can tap **Keep it** to cancel.
**Show:** Click "…" → "Delete": the box "Delete this announcement?" Spotlight "Delete", then click "Keep it".

### 6. As a parent
#### [6.1] Find it
**Say:** Parents see announcements in the app, on the **Class Announcements** card for their child on the home screen, or under **Parent Portal**, **Class Announcements**. With more than one child, they can pick one or **All children**.
**Show:** Phone frame, parent account (side panel "Signed in as: parent"): app home, the child’s Class Announcements card → "View all". Spotlight the child tabs and "All children".

#### [6.2] React
**Say:** Tap the heart to like a post, and the bookmark to save it. To see only saved posts, open **Filters** and choose **Bookmarked only**.
**Show:** Tap the heart on the photo post (it fills); tap the bookmark. Open Filters; switch on Bookmarked only; tap "Done".

#### [6.2a] More filters and photos
**Say:** **Filters** can also show one **Class**, **Section** or **Teacher**; **Clear filters** shows everything again. Tap a photo to see it full screen, where you can zoom in or download it.
**Show:** Tap the "Maryam" child tab, then open Filters; spotlight Class, Section and Teacher; tap "Clear filters", then "Done". Tap "All children". Tap the photo: the full-screen view; spotlight the zoom and download buttons; close it.

#### [6.3] Ask the teacher
**Say:** To ask about a post, tap the three dots and choose **Message teacher**. A message to that teacher opens, ready to write.
**Show:** Tap "…" → "Message teacher". The new message window opens for the teacher. Close it.

### 7. What the teacher sees
#### [7.1]
**Say:** Back on the teacher’s side, each post counts its likes and views. Tap them to see who liked or saw it.
**Show:** Teacher account (side panel "Signed in as: teacher"): on the photo post, click "1 like"; the "Engagement" window opens on "Liked by". Click "Seen by" to switch lists, then close it.

### 8. Next
#### [8.1] Done
**Say:** Your class can now see what you share, and parents can respond. Next, video 07.3: Message parents, classes and teachers, and video 10.1: The teacher portal and the teacher app home.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "07.3 Message parents, classes and teachers" and "10.1 The teacher portal and the teacher app home".
