# 07.1 Your message inbox

## Goal
Find, read, filter and organise the messages and news your masjid, madrasah and Ad-Din send you, on the web or in the app.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- All, Unread, Read, Sent, Flagged, Archive
- the News tab
- Messages card on the app home: unread count, latest messages, Open inbox, New message
- Why one inbox; messages still arrive with email or push switched off (Preferences, 00.5)
- Reading the list (day groups, bold unread, Flagged tag); Nothing here yet
- Unflag and Mark Read; Archived keeps messages; Select all and the unread count on Mark all as read
- No reply button (pointer to 07.3); pictures, links and Search news on the News tab

## Demo data
- Demo parent account "Samir Karim" (parent of Zaid Karim at Maple Grove Madrasah, follows Maple Grove Masjid, no admin role), signed in on the web and in the app (phone frame); the side panel reads "Signed in as: a parent"
- About eight messages across both organisations: three unread, one flagged, one already archived, one Ad-Din notification (for example an attendance notice), and one from the masjid with an image, an attached link and a PDF attachment ("Family Quran Night this Friday", for the Family Quran Night event on Friday 13 November), besides the world's "Community iftar this Friday" and "Report cards are ready"
- The member's app home shows the Messages card (it appears for anyone with a role at their home madrasah: parent, student, teacher or admin), with "3 unread" and the three latest messages (New message is hidden for an account that is only a student)
- Four news items on the demo masjid, one of them pinned ("Winter prayer timetable", with a picture and a link)
- One message from a teacher (Bilal Hussain) for the [4.4] shot
- No real names or emails on screen

## Script

### 1. One inbox for everything
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** In this video, you’ll see where messages from your masjid, your madrasah, your child’s teachers and Ad-Din arrive, and how to read and organise them.
**Show:** Title card: "Your message inbox".

#### [1.1a] Why it matters
**Say:** Every message sent to you through Ad-Din lands in this inbox. That includes messages you also get by email or push, so nothing is lost if an email goes to spam.
**Show:** Card: "Email · Push notification · Inbox: every message is here".

#### [1.1b]
**Say:** Even if you switch email or push notifications off in **Preferences**, as video 00.5 shows, your messages still arrive here.
**Show:** Quick cut: Settings → Preferences, spotlight "Notification preferences" with "E-mail notification" and "Push notification". Don't change them.

#### [1.2] Open it
**Say:** On the web, tap **Message** in the sidebar. In the app, tap **Messages** on the home screen. The number on it shows how many messages are unread.
**Show:** Web: Sidebar → Message. Then phone frame: app home, spotlight the Messages tile and its unread badge.

#### [1.2a] The Messages card
**Say:** Parents, students, teachers and madrasah admins also have a **Messages** card on the app home. It shows how many are unread and your latest messages. Tap one to read it, or **Open inbox** to see them all. **New message** starts a new one.
**Show:** Phone frame: scroll to the Messages card. Spotlight the "3 unread" pill and the three latest messages, then the "Open inbox" and "New message" buttons. Tap "Open inbox"; the inbox opens.
**Caption:** Parents, students, teachers and madrasah admins also have a Messages card: tap a message, Open inbox or New message.

### 2. Find your way around
#### [2.1] The layout
**Say:** Your messages are listed on the left, newest first. Tap one to read it on the right. The number on the **Messages** tab shows how many are unread.
**Show:** Web inbox. Spotlight the list, the reading pane ("Nothing selected"), and the count on the Messages tab.

#### [2.1a] Reading the list
**Say:** Messages are grouped by day. Unread messages are in bold, and each shows its title, the sender and the start of the text. A message you flagged shows a **Flagged** tag.
**Show:** Spotlight a day heading, a bold unread row, then the "Flagged" tag on the flagged message.

#### [2.2] Organisation
**Say:** At the top, **Organisation** shows whose messages you see. **All organisations** shows everything, including Ad-Din’s own notifications. Pick one masjid or madrasah to see only theirs.
**Show:** Click the Organisation button; the "Select an Organization" list opens. Spotlight "All organisations", pick the demo masjid, then switch back to All organisations.
**Caption:** All organisations shows everything, including Ad-Din’s notifications. Pick one to see only theirs.

#### [2.3] Search
**Say:** To find a message, type a word in **Search messages**.
**Show:** Type "Quran" in Search messages; the list narrows. Clear it.

### 3. Filter your messages
#### [3.1] Filters
**Say:** The buttons under the search narrow the list: **All**, **Read**, **Unread**, **Flagged** and **Archived**.
**Show:** Click Read, Unread, Flagged and Archived in turn, then All. The list changes each time.

#### [3.1a]
**Say:** If a filter or search finds nothing, the list says **Nothing here yet**. Change the filter or clear the search to see your messages again.
**Show:** Type "zzz" in Search messages: "Nothing here yet" and "No messages match this view. Try a different search or filter." Clear the search.

#### [3.2] Sent
**Say:** If you can send messages, for example as a parent, teacher or admin, you’ll also see **Sent**, with the messages you’ve sent.
**Show:** Spotlight the Sent filter (this parent can write to teachers, so it shows).

### 4. Read and organise
#### [4.1] Read a message
**Say:** Tap a message to read it. You’ll see who sent it and when, and any picture, link or attachment. Tap the picture to see it full size, or an attachment to open it.
**Show:** Click "Family Quran Night this Friday". Spotlight the sender line, the image, "Attached Link" and the attachment card.

#### [4.2] Flag and archive
**Say:** Tap **Flag** to mark a message you want to find again later. **Mark Unread** keeps it bold for later. **Archive** moves it out of your list and into **Archived**.
**Show:** Click "Flag": the Flagged tag appears. Click "Mark Unread", then "Archive"; the message leaves the list.

#### [4.2a] Undo it
**Say:** Each button switches back. **Flag** becomes **Unflag**, and **Mark Unread** becomes **Mark Read**. Archived messages are not deleted; you can still find them under **Archived**.
**Show:** Click "Archived"; open the archived message; spotlight "Unflag" and "Mark Read" in its bar. Click "All".

#### [4.3] Several at once
**Say:** To organise several messages at once, tick their boxes and choose an action. Or tap **Mark all as read** to mark every unread message in the list as read.
**Show:** Tick three messages; the bar shows "3 selected" with Flag, Unflag, Mark read, Mark unread, Archive. Clear the selection, then click "Mark all as read".

#### [4.3a]
**Say:** **Select all** ticks every message in the list. The number on **Mark all as read** shows how many are still unread.
**Show:** Spotlight "Select all" and the count on "Mark all as read" (before clicking it, from the same take).

#### [4.4] Answering a message
**Say:** Messages have no reply button. To answer a teacher, send them a new message, as video 07.3 shows.
**Show:** Open a message from a teacher; spotlight the bar (Flag, Mark Read, Archive) with no reply option.

### 5. The News tab
#### [5.1] News
**Say:** Next to **Messages** is **News**. It shows the news your masjid or madrasah publishes, with **Pinned** news at the top.
**Show:** Click the "News" tab. The demo masjid’s news list; spotlight the Pinned tag on "Winter prayer timetable".

#### [5.2]
**Say:** Tap a news item to read it. News shows one organisation at a time, so use **Organisation** to switch between your masjid and madrasah.
**Show:** Open "Winter prayer timetable". Click Organisation and pick the demo madrasah; its news list shows.

#### [5.2a]
**Say:** News can have a picture, a link and attachments, just like a message. To find an older item, type a word in **Search news**.
**Show:** Back on the masjid's news: spotlight the picture and "Attached Link" on "Winter prayer timetable". Type "prayer" in Search news; clear it.

### 6. In the app
#### [6.1] Same inbox, phone layout
**Say:** The app shows the same inbox. Tap a message to open it. **Flag**, **Read** and **Archive** are at the bottom. Tap the back arrow to return to the list.
**Show:** Phone frame: the inbox list, tap a message; spotlight the bottom bar (Flag, Unread/Read, Archive); tap the back arrow.

### 7. Next
#### [7.1] Done
**Say:** Now you know where to find your messages and news, and how to keep your inbox in order. Next, video 07.2: Send a message to your community, and video 07.3: Message parents, classes and teachers.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "07.2 Send a message to your community" and "07.3 Message parents, classes and teachers".
