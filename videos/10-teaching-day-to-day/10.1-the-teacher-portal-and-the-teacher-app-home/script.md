# 10.1 The teacher portal and the teacher app home

## Goal
Find your teaching tools in the Teacher Portal on the web, and use the app home's quick actions, "Needs your attention" list and daily routine.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Portal tiles, “Needs your attention”, timetable, quick actions
- Why a teaching home helps; the Parent/Guardian Portal for teachers who are also parents
- Role tabs, your username and email (Tap to copy)
- Pin and unpin quick actions; the same pins on the web dashboard; the empty quick actions card
- What each "Needs your attention" row tells you and where it opens; "All caught up"
- My Routine rows (time, subject, class and section, breaks), "No classes on this day"
- Class Announcements: View all and New (video 07.4)

## Demo data
<!-- Records the demo organisation must have for this video (classes, students, events...). -->
- Demo madrasah "Maple Grove Madrasah" (Premium) with Academic Activity and Class Announcements switched on, current term "Autumn Term"
- Teacher account Bilal Hussain, who is also a parent of one enrolled child (so the app home shows the Teacher and Parent tabs and the Madrasah hub shows Teachers Portal)
- Class timetable for today with four periods for Bilal (for example Quran, Fiqh, a break, Arabic), so My Routine shows Now and Next tags
- Today: two of Bilal's class sections without attendance, three submitted Hifz entries waiting for review, five turned-in academic activities to grade (every "Needs your attention" row has a count)
- Quick actions already pinned: Attendance, Quran Hifz Assignments, Academic Activity
- One recent class announcement
- Bilal's account has a username and email, so tapping his name shows them
- No classes for Bilal on Saturday in the timetable, so My Routine shows "No classes on this day"
- A second, all-done state of the same home (no pending attendance, Hifz or grading, no pins) for the cut-in shots of "All caught up" and the empty Quick actions card
- Web: signed in as Bilal in the browser. App: the same account in a phone frame

### 1. Your teaching home
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** This video is for teachers. You'll see where to find your teaching tools on the web and in the Ad-Din app, and what the app shows you each day.
**Show:** Title card: "The teacher portal and the teacher app home".

#### [1.1a] Why it helps
**Say:** A teaching day is busy. Ad-Din keeps your registers, Hifz records and marking in one place, and the app home shows what still needs doing before your first class starts.
**Show:** Phone frame, app home on the Teacher tab. Slow scroll from Quick actions down to My Routine.

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your madrasah admin has already added you as a teacher and built the class timetable, so your classes are ready. The Teacher Portal needs Ad-Din Premium; your madrasah can give you one of its Premium seats.
**Show:** Opening card. Web dashboard on the left, app home in a phone frame on the right.

### 2. The Teacher Portal on the web
#### [2.1] Open the portal
**Say:** On the web, open the sidebar and expand **Teacher Portal**. Everything you need for your classes is listed here.
**Show:** Web. Sidebar → Teacher Portal, expanded. Slow pan down the list.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Day to day you'll mostly use **Attendance**, **Quran Hifz Assignments** and **Academic Activity**. Each one has its own video in this series.
**Show:** Spotlight Attendance, Quran Hifz Assignments and Academic Activity in turn.

#### [2.3]
**Say:** You'll also find **Class Section**, your class roster, which video 10.12 explains. Then **Class Routine**, **Parent Teacher Interview**, **Library** and **Recognition**.
**Show:** Spotlight Class Section, Class Routine, Parent Teacher Interview, Library, Recognition.

#### [2.3a]
**Say:** **Class**, **Subject**, **Holiday**, **Grade** and **Delegations** are there to read. **Academic Activity** and **Class Announcements** appear only when your madrasah switches them on.
**Show:** Spotlight Class, Subject, Holiday, Grade and Delegations as one group, then Academic Activity and Class Announcements.

#### [2.3b]
**Say:** Tap any item to open its page. For example, **Class Routine** opens on **My Routine**, your timetable for the week. **View By Class** and **View By Teacher** show the timetable of any class or teacher.
**Show:** Click "Class Routine": the "My Routine" tab shows Bilal's week. Spotlight the "View By Class" and "View By Teacher" tabs. Back to the sidebar.

### 3. Portal tiles in the app
#### [3.1] Madrasah tile
**Say:** In the Ad-Din app, the home screen has tiles. Tap **Madrasah**, then **Teachers Portal**.
**Show:** Phone frame, app home. Tap the "Madrasah" tile; the portals page shows Introduction, Parent/Guardian Portal and Teachers Portal. Tap "Teachers Portal".

#### [3.2]
**Say:** The same list as the web opens, one row per tool. Tap a row to open it. If you only teach, the **Madrasah** tile opens this list straight away.
**Show:** The Teacher Portal list. Tap "Attendance" to show it opens, then go back to the home screen.
**Caption:** The same list as the web opens. Tap a row to open it. If you only teach, Madrasah opens this list straight away.

#### [3.2a] If you're also a parent
**Say:** If you also have a child at the madrasah, the same page shows **Parent/Guardian Portal**. That is where you follow your own child, as any parent does. Video 14.1 explains the parent side.
**Show:** Back on the portals page, spotlight "Parent/Guardian Portal", then "Teachers Portal".

### 4. Quick actions
#### [4.1] The Teacher tab
**Say:** At the top of the home screen you see your name and how many classes you have today. If you're also a parent, tap the **Teacher** tab.
**Show:** Identity card with "Assalamu alaikum", Bilal's name and "4 classes today". Tap the "Teacher" tab.

#### [4.1a] Role tabs and your username
**Say:** The tabs only appear when you have more than one role at the madrasah, and each tab shows the home for that role. Tap your name to see your username and email, then tap either one to copy it, for example when support asks for it.
**Show:** Spotlight the "Teacher" and "Parent" tabs. Tap Bilal's name: his @username and email open with "Tap to copy". Tap the username. Tap the name again to close.

#### [4.2] Shortcuts
**Say:** **Quick actions** are shortcuts to the pages you use most. Tap one to jump straight in.
**Show:** Spotlight the Quick actions card with its chips: Attendance, Quran Hifz Assignments, Academic Activity.

#### [4.3] Change them
**Say:** To change them, tap the pen. In **Pin quick actions**, search or scroll, tap **Pin** next to a page, then tap **Done**.
**Show:** Tap the pen. The sheet opens; type "Library" in "Search modules", tap "+ Pin" so it shows "Pinned", tap "Done". The new chip appears.

#### [4.3a] Unpin, and the web
**Say:** To remove a shortcut, open the same sheet and tap **Pinned** again. Your pins are saved to your account, so your web dashboard shows the same quick actions.
**Show:** Tap the pen, type "Library" and tap "Pinned": it changes back to "+ Pin". Tap "Done"; the Library chip is gone. Cut to the web dashboard's quick actions with the same three pages.

#### [4.3b]
**Say:** With no pins, the card says **Tap the pen to pin the modules you use most**. A good start is the three pages you open every day.
**Show:** Cut-in from the all-done demo state: the Quick actions card with its "Tap the pen to pin the modules you use most" line. Then back to the card with Attendance, Quran Hifz Assignments and Academic Activity.

### 5. Needs your attention
#### [5.1] What's waiting
**Say:** **Needs your attention** lists what's waiting for you: **Attendance not taken**, **Hifz entries pending**, and **Academic activities to grade**.
**Show:** Scroll to the "Needs your attention" card. Spotlight each row and its count.

#### [5.1a] Read each row
**Say:** Under **Attendance not taken**, you see the class sections still waiting. The Hifz count is the entries your students submitted for your review. Under the grading row, you also see how many students haven't turned their work in yet.
**Show:** Zoom on each row's second line in turn: two class section names; "Submitted entries waiting for your review"; "2 missing".

#### [5.2]
**Say:** Each row shows a count. Tap a row, and Ad-Din opens the right page, ready to work.
**Show:** Tap "Attendance not taken": the Attendance page opens. Go back.

#### [5.2a]
**Say:** The Hifz row opens **Quran Hifz Assignments**, and the grading row opens **Academic Activity**. The grading row only appears when your madrasah uses Academic Activity.
**Show:** Tap "Hifz entries pending": Quran Hifz Assignments opens. Go back. Tap "Academic activities to grade": Academic Activity opens. Go back.

#### [5.3]
**Say:** A row only appears when work is waiting. Fewer rows means less to do.
**Show:** Zoom on the count circle of each row in turn.

#### [5.3a]
**Say:** When everything is done, the card says **All caught up — nothing needs you right now.**
**Show:** Cut-in from the all-done demo state: the card with no rows and the "All caught up" line.
**Caption:** When everything is done, the card says All caught up.

### 6. My Routine
#### [6.1] Today's timetable
**Say:** **My Routine** is your timetable for the day, taken from the class timetable. **Now** and **Next** show where you are.
**Show:** Scroll to "My Routine". Spotlight the "Now" and "Next" tags.

#### [6.1a] Each period
**Say:** Each row shows the start and end time, the subject, and the class and section. Breaks show in the list too, so you can see your whole day.
**Show:** Spotlight one period row (times, "Quran", "Maktab Level 1 · A"), then the break row.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Use the arrows to look at other days, and tap the day name to return to **Today**.
**Show:** Tap the right arrow twice (the day name changes), then tap the day name: it returns to "Today". Record on a Sunday to Thursday: the arrows stop at Saturday.

#### [6.2a]
**Say:** On a day without classes, it says **No classes on this day**. If a period is missing or wrong, ask your madrasah admin to change the class timetable.
**Show:** Tap the right arrow until Saturday: "No classes on this day". Tap the day name to return to Today.

#### [6.3]
**Say:** Below it, **Class Announcements** shows your latest class posts, when your madrasah uses them.
**Show:** Scroll to the Class Announcements card. Spotlight it briefly.

#### [6.3a]
**Say:** Tap **View all** to read every post, or **New** to write one for your class. Video 07.4 shows how.
**Show:** Spotlight the latest post, then "View all" and "New". Do not open them.

### 7. Next
#### [7.1] Your home is ready
**Say:** That's your teaching home: the Teacher Portal on the web, and in the app your quick actions, your to-do list and your routine.
**Show:** Split screen again: web Teacher Portal and app home.

#### [7.2] Close
**Say:** Next, video 10.2: Take class attendance, and video 10.4: Record daily Hifz progress.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "10.2 Take class attendance" and "10.4 Record daily Hifz progress".
