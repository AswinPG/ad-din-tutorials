# 10.4 Record daily Hifz progress

## Goal
Record what each Hifz student recited today, count the mistakes, add a note for the parents, and mark the task passed or failed.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Portion recited, mistakes, result
- Why record it daily; Reset filters; Create Assignment for chosen students
- Previous attempt (Passed, Failed, Not Marked) and how today's portion starts from it
- The marks per mistake and the most mistakes counted; No messages from parent
- Note templates (examples, the student's name filled in)
- Failed and the repeat count; what the student and parents receive (email and app notification)

### 1. Today's Hifz
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** This video shows teachers how to record each Hifz student's daily recitation: the portion recited, the number of mistakes, and the result.
**Show:** Title card: "Record daily Hifz progress".

#### [1.1a] Why
**Say:** A short record each day means the next lesson starts exactly where the last one ended, even with a different teacher. Parents see the same record in the app and can help at home.
**Show:** The marking panel of one student: Previous attempt at the top, Current Grading below.

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your admin has already set up the Hifz tasks for your class, so today's list is waiting for you.
**Show:** Opening card.

### 2. Open today's list
#### [2.1] Quran Hifz Assignments
**Say:** Open **Teacher Portal**, then **Quran Hifz Assignments**. **All Assignments** lists today's tasks, one row per student.
**Show:** Sidebar → Teacher Portal → Quran Hifz Assignments. Spotlight the All Assignments card.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Use **Search** to find a student, or tap the date to mark another day.
**Show:** Spotlight the Search box, then the date box.

#### [2.2a]
**Say:** The round arrow beside them resets the search and date. **Create Assignment** opens **Manage Assignments**, where you can add a task yourself for the students you choose.
**Show:** Spotlight the "Reset filters" button, then "+ Create Assignment". Click it: Manage Assignments opens, with "Students in this assignment" on the right. Go back without saving.

#### [2.3] Status
**Say:** **Status** shows where each one stands: **Not Submitted**, **Pending Review** when the family has sent it in, **Marked**, or **Failed**.
**Show:** Spotlight the Status column badges one by one.

### 3. The portion recited
#### [3.1] Pick a student
**Say:** Tap a student. Their task opens with the **Previous attempt** at the top, so you can see where they stopped last time.
**Show:** Click a "Pending Review" row. The panel opens on the right. Click "Previous attempt" to expand it: Start, End and the Teacher's Note.

#### [3.1a]
**Say:** The previous attempt says **Passed**, **Failed** or **Not Marked**. Today's portion starts where the last one ended. If the last attempt failed, Ad-Din fills in the same portion again, so the student repeats it.
**Show:** Zoom on "Previous attempt · Passed". Then click the Not Submitted student whose last attempt failed: "Previous attempt · Failed", and Start Ayah and End Ayah show the same range. Click back to the first student.

#### [3.2] Start and end
**Say:** Under **Current Grading**, set the **Start Ayah** and **End Ayah** of today's portion. Ad-Din shows the total.
**Show:** Change Start Ayah and End Ayah with the pickers. Zoom on the total at the top right of Current Grading.

#### [3.3]
**Say:** For tasks set up by Juz, you pick a **Start** and **End** in the Juz instead.
**Show:** Quick cut to a by-Juz task showing the Start and End pickers, then back.

### 4. Mistakes
#### [4.1] Number of Mistakes
**Say:** Under **Number of Mistakes**, tap a number from one to ten, or type a higher number in the last box.
**Show:** Click "2". Spotlight the "10+" box.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** For each mistake, Ad-Din subtracts a fixed number of marks, and **Marks:** shows the result.
**Show:** Zoom on the helper text "marks deducted per mistakes" and the "Marks:" box as it updates.

#### [4.2a]
**Say:** The helper text shows how many marks each mistake costs, and the most mistakes that count, set by your admin for the subject. If the number looks wrong, ask your admin to change it.
**Show:** Zoom on the full helper text, for example "(2 marks deducted per mistakes, max 10)".

### 5. Notes for the family
#### [5.1] From parent
**Say:** Open **Notes**. **From parent** shows anything the family sent with today's work.
**Show:** Click "Notes" to expand. Spotlight the From parent box.

#### [5.1a]
**Say:** When the family sent nothing, it says **No messages from parent.**
**Show:** Click the Not Submitted student's row, open Notes: "No messages from parent." Click back to the first student and open Notes again.

#### [5.2] Note for parent
**Say:** Write a **Note for parent**. Type a slash, or tap a chip, to add a ready-made note.
**Show:** Click in "Note for parent", type "/", pick "Excellent effort" from the menu. The note fills in with the student's name.

#### [5.2a]
**Say:** There are notes for good days, such as **Strong recitation**, and for harder ones, such as **Needs revision** or **Parent support needed**. You can change the text after it fills in, so it fits the student.
**Show:** Type "/" again and scroll the menu slowly; spotlight "Strong recitation", "Needs revision" and "Parent support needed". Press Escape. Click into the note and add " Well done." at the end.

#### [5.3]
**Say:** If you track how long the student spent, set **Time Spend** too.
**Show:** Click the Time Spend pill, pick 00:20, click "Done".

### 6. The result
#### [6.1] Pass or Failed
**Say:** Finish with **Pass**. If the student needs to repeat the portion, tap **Failed** instead.
**Show:** Spotlight both buttons. Click "Pass".

#### [6.1a]
**Say:** When you tap **Failed**, the row shows how many times the student has failed this task, for example, Failed (1). Next time, the same portion comes back for them to repeat.
**Show:** Spotlight the row already showing "Failed (1)" in the Status column.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** A message confirms it, and the row now shows **Marked**. Move on to the next student.
**Show:** Green confirmation message. The row's Status changes to "Marked". Click the next row.

#### [6.2a] What the family receives
**Say:** The student and their parents get an email and an app notification with the result, the mistakes, the marks and your note. So check your note before you tap **Pass** or **Failed**.
**Show:** Click back to the student just marked. Spotlight the Number of Mistakes, the Marks: box and the Note for parent text in turn, then the Pass and Failed buttons.

### 7. In the app
#### [7.1]
**Say:** In the app it works the same way. Tap **Back to Listing** to return to your list.
**Show:** Phone frame. The same marking screen; tap "Back to Listing" at the bottom.

#### [7.2] Close
**Say:** Today's Hifz is recorded, and parents see the result and your note in their app. Next, video 14.3: Follow Hifz progress and classwork, and video 10.2: Take class attendance.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "14.3 Follow Hifz progress and classwork" and "10.2 Take class attendance".
