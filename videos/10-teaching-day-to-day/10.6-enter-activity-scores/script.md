# 10.6 Enter activity scores

## Goal
Enter scores for an academic activity, open a student's submitted work, return it if needed, and message the student about it.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Activity Score Management, view a student, message a student
- Why mark in Ad-Din; the activity details at the top of Add Marks
- Score range, the Status column (Assigned, Turned In, Graded, Missing, Returned); changing a grade
- Non-scored activities: the round mark (Not marked, Completed, Not completed)
- Who is told about a grade (student and parents, email and app notification, only when the status changes); Return Submission and message notifications
- Export marks and Import marks (video 10.7)

### 1. Marking made simple
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** In this video for teachers, you'll enter each student's score for an activity, look at their work, and message them about it.
**Show:** Title card: "Enter activity scores".

#### [1.1a] Why
**Say:** Scores you enter here go into each student's term grade and report card. Families can follow every grade in the app, so they know how their child is doing long before the report arrives.
**Show:** The Activity Score Management list for Maktab Level 1 A, Fiqh, with its Graded and Missing counts.

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your activities are already created for this term.
**Show:** Opening card.

### 2. Find the activity
#### [2.1] Activity Score Management
**Say:** Open **Teacher Portal**, then **Academic Activity**, and tap **Activity Score Management**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Teacher Portal → Academic Activity. Click "Activity Score Management".

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Choose the **Class**, **Class section** and **Subject**. Use **Marking Status** to show only the activities still **Pending**.
**Show:** Pick "Maktab Level 1", "A", "Fiqh". Then set Marking Status to "Pending".

#### [2.3]
**Say:** Names in green are marked; red ones still need marks. The **Completed** and **Pending** totals sit at the top.
**Show:** Clear the Marking Status filter. Spotlight a green and a red name, then the totals.

### 3. Enter scores
#### [3.1] Add Mark
**Say:** Tap **Add Mark** next to the activity. The **Add Marks** page lists every student in the section.
**Show:** Click "Add Mark" on "Wudu steps worksheet". The Add Marks page opens.

#### [3.1a] The activity at a glance
**Say:** The top of the page shows the activity name and due date, then its **Class**, **Category**, **Weight**, any **URL** and files, and whether it is **Ignored in Calculation**.
**Show:** Spotlight the title and "Due :" line, then pan across the detail fields and the attached file.

#### [3.2] Notify families
**Say:** To tell families about each grade, first turn on **Notify to Parent/Guardian** under the list. It starts off, and families aren't told about grades you saved before.
**Show:** Spotlight the switch under the list; turn it on.

#### [3.2a] Who is told
**Say:** With the switch on, the student and their parents get an email and an app notification when you grade them. If you change a grade later, no second message is sent.
**Show:** Zoom on the "Notify to Parent/Guardian" switch, now on.

#### [3.3] Add Grade
**Say:** Tap a student's **Add Grade** box and type the score. Grades are saved automatically as you enter them.
**Show:** Click the first Add Grade cell, type 9, press Enter. The Status changes to "Graded". Fill in the rest of the class (sped up).

#### [3.3a] Scores and status
**Say:** A score can be from zero up to the **Score Out Of** shown in the heading, here 10. The **Status** column shows **Assigned**, **Turned In**, **Graded**, **Missing** or **Returned**.
**Show:** Zoom on the heading "Marks / 10". Spotlight the Status badges in turn: Graded, Turned In and Missing.

#### [3.3b]
**Say:** To correct a grade, tap the box again and type the new score. It saves straight away.
**Show:** Click the 9 in the first row, type 8, press Enter. The row stays "Graded".

#### [3.3c] Activities without a score
**Say:** For a non-scored activity, each student has a round mark instead. Tap it to move between **Not marked**, **Completed** and **Not completed**. Each tap is saved.
**Show:** Click "Back To List", then "Add Mark" on "Bring your Quran to class". Spotlight the guide under the heading. Tap one student's grey mark: green tick, then red cross, then grey dash. Tap once more for green. Go back to "Wudu steps worksheet".

#### [3.3d] Marks from Excel
**Say:** **Export marks** downloads the students and their marks as an Excel sheet. **Import marks** grades the class from a sheet, which video 10.7 shows.
**Show:** Spotlight "Export marks" and "Import marks" above the list. Do not click them.

### 4. View a student
#### [4.1] Their work
**Say:** Tap a student's name to open their work. **Student Submission** lists anything they uploaded.
**Show:** Click a student with turned-in work. The side panel opens; click the download icon on their file.

#### [4.2] Attachments and returns
**Say:** **Add Attachments** sends back a marked file. **Return Submission** hands the work back so they can try again.
**Show:** Spotlight "Add Attachments". Click "Return Submission"; the question "Are you sure you want to return the assigment ?" shows. Click "Yes".

#### [4.2a]
**Say:** The window says the update is shared by app notification and email. The student's status changes to **Returned**, so they can work on it and turn it in again.
**Show:** Replay the question window with its line "Updates will be shared via push notification and email." Then the row's Status: "Returned".

### 5. Message a student
#### [5.1] Message Student
**Say:** Under **Message Student**, type in **Type your message** and tap the send arrow.
**Show:** Scroll the panel to Message Student. Type "Well done, check step 3 again." and click the send arrow. The message appears in the chat.

#### [5.2]
**Say:** The student sees your message with this activity, and can reply here.
**Show:** Spotlight the earlier student message and the new reply in the chat.

#### [5.2a]
**Say:** A few minutes after you write, the student and their parents get an email and an app notification. Messages you send close together arrive as one notification, so a short conversation doesn't flood their phones.
**Show:** Type a second line "Bring it on Thursday." and send. Spotlight both messages in the chat.

### 6. In the app
#### [6.1]
**Say:** On a phone, the **Marks** tab shows the list. Pick a student, then tap their name at the top to see their work and the chat.
**Show:** Phone frame, same page. Tap a student row, then the second tab (the student's name): Student Submission and Message Student show.

### 7. Next
#### [7.1] Done
**Say:** Your scores are saved. When you're finished, tap **Back To List**. The activity now shows in green: it's marked.
**Show:** Web. Click "Back To List": "Wudu steps worksheet" is now green.

#### [7.2] Close
**Say:** Next, video 10.7: Import activities and marks from Excel, and video 12.2: Write comments and mark reports ready.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "10.7 Import activities and marks from Excel" and "12.2 Write comments and mark reports ready".
