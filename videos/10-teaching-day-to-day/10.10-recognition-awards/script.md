# 10.10 Recognition awards

## Goal
Give a student or staff member an award, download its certificate, and send it so they and their family are told.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Student and staff awards
- Why give awards in Ad-Din
- To Student with Class and Class Section, or To Guest
- Every award type; Custom (type your own) with a Certificate Title
- Edit (nobody is told again, the certificate updates), Delete, Export

### 1. Celebrate good work
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Recognition lets you celebrate students and staff with an award and a certificate, such as Student of the Month.
**Show:** Title card: "Recognition awards".

#### [1.1a] Why
**Say:** Praise in front of the class means more when the family hears it too. An award in Ad-Din reaches the student and their parents the same day, and the certificate is something they can keep and print.
**Show:** A sample Student of the Month certificate, then the Student list of awards.

#### [1.2]
**Say:** This is for madrasah admins. Your students and teachers are already in Ad-Din.
**Show:** Opening card.

### 2. Open Recognition
#### [2.1]
**Say:** In the **Admin Portal**, open **Recognition**. There are two tabs: **Student** and **Staff**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal → Recognition. Spotlight the two tabs.

### 3. A student award
#### [3.1] From
**Say:** On **Student**, tap **Add**. Under **From**, choose **From Teacher** and pick the teacher, or **From Guest** and type a name.
**Show:** Click "Add". The Recognition window opens. "From Teacher" is selected; pick "Bilal Hussain". Tap "From Guest" to show the text box, then back to "From Teacher".

#### [3.2] To
**Say:** Under **To**, choose **To Student**. Pick the **Class** and **Class Section**, then the student. For someone outside the madrasah, choose **To Guest** and type their name.
**Show:** "To Student" selected; pick "Maktab Level 1", section "A", then "Zaid Karim". Spotlight "To Guest" without clicking it.

#### [3.3] Type and date
**Say:** Choose the **Type**, such as **Student of the Month** or **Perfect Attendance Award**. Set the **Date** and tap **Save**. The student, their parents and the teacher are told straight away, by email and in the app.
**Show:** Open Type; spotlight the options, pick "Student of the Month". Pick today's date. Click "Save". The award appears in the list.

#### [3.3a] Every type
**Say:** Other types are **Student of the Year**, **Most Improved Student of the Month**, **Most Improved Student of the Year**, **Years of Service Award** and **Extracurricular Award**.
**Show:** Click "Add" again and open Type; move slowly down the list.

#### [3.3b] Your own title
**Say:** If none of them fits, choose **Custom (type your own)** and type a **Certificate Title**. It's printed on the certificate exactly as you type it, up to 60 characters, so check the spelling.
**Show:** Pick "Custom (type your own)": the Certificate Title box appears with its hint. Type "Juz Amma completed". From Teacher "Bilal Hussain", To Student "Maktab Level 1", "A", "Hana Mirza", today's date. Click "Save".

### 4. Certificates
#### [4.1] View Certificate
**Say:** Each award has a certificate. Tap **View Certificate** to download it as a PDF.
**Show:** Click "View Certificate" on the new row. The sample certificate PDF opens.

#### [4.2] Send Certificate
**Say:** To send the award again later, select it and tap **Send Certificate**. The same people are told again.
**Show:** Select the row, click "Send Certificate". The confirmation message shows.

#### [4.3] Edit or delete
**Say:** To correct an award, select it and tap **Edit**. Nobody is told about an edit, but **View Certificate** always gives you the updated certificate, and **Send Certificate** shares it. **Delete** asks you to confirm first.
**Show:** Select the Custom award, click "Edit", change the title to "Juz Amma memorised", click "Save". Click "View Certificate": the new title shows. Spotlight "Delete".

#### [4.4]
**Say:** **Export** downloads your list of awards as a CSV file, which opens in a spreadsheet, for example for the end-of-year assembly.
**Show:** Spotlight "Export" above the list.

### 5. A staff award
#### [5.1] Staff Recognition
**Say:** Staff awards work the same way. On **Staff**, tap **Add**, then choose who it's from, and **To Teacher** or **To Guest**.
**Show:** Click the "Staff" tab, then "Add". The "Staff Recognition" window opens. Choose "From Guest", type "Masjid committee". Under To, pick "Fatima Siddiqui".

#### [5.2]
**Say:** Staff types include **Teacher of the Month**, **Years of Service Award** and **Certificates of Appreciation**. Pick one, set the date, and tap **Save**. The teacher is told straight away.
**Show:** Open Type and spotlight those options. Pick "Years of Service Award", set the date, click "Save".

#### [5.2a]
**Say:** There are also **Teacher of the Year**, **Certificates of Acknowledgement** and the same **Custom (type your own)** choice, for example to thank a volunteer.
**Show:** Open Type on a new Staff Recognition window; spotlight those three options. Click Cancel.

#### [5.3]
**Say:** Each staff award has its own **View Certificate** and **Send Certificate** buttons.
**Show:** Spotlight the two buttons on the new row. Click "Send Certificate"; the confirmation shows.

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Who sees them
**Say:** Use the **Type** and date filters to find past awards. Teachers see them under **Recognition** in the Teacher Portal, and families in the Parent Portal.
**Show:** Open Filters, pick a type. Quick cut to a teacher (side panel: "Signed in as: Bilal Hussain, a teacher"): Teacher Portal → Recognition.

#### [6.2] Close
**Say:** Your awards are given and shared. Next, video 10.2: Take class attendance, and video 10.9: Library.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "10.2 Take class attendance" and "10.9 Library".
