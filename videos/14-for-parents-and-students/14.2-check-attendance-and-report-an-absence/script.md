# 14.2 Check attendance and report an absence

## Goal
Check your child's attendance for today, by subject and for the term, and send the madrasah an excuse before an absence or late arrival.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- The Ad-Din app, signed in with your parent account
- A child enrolled in a class

## Covers
- Today, by subject, term summary
- Attendance excuse
- Why tell the madrasah ahead; what parents receive when the teacher saves attendance (app notification; email when not present)
- Empty states (No classes today, No academic term is set up yet)
- The View tab (one day's status and remarks); Help and View status legend
- Filter by date for past excuses; Description is optional; Cancel
- Deleting an excuse (Delete excuse?, Proceed); the teacher sees the excuse in the register, with no separate message

## Demo data
- Parent "Yusuf Rahman" (the demo world's account) in the app, Maple Grove Madrasah; child "Maryam Rahman" in "Maktab Level 1", section "A"
- Today's attendance marked for Maryam: one class Present, one Late; subject-wise attendance on, with two subject periods today ("Quran" Present, "Arabic" Late)
- Academic terms "Term 1" (current) and "Term 2"; Term 1 summary: 40 days (35 present, 2 absent, 1 sick, 2 late)
- One past excuse for Maryam: Sick, last week, attendance already marked
- New excuse typed on camera: next Monday, Late, 10:30, "Dentist appointment, back by 10:30."
- Yesterday's attendance for Maryam with a teacher remark ("Arrived 10 minutes late") for the View tab shot
- Attendance notifications are mocked; a demo notification for Maryam's Late status today
- Phone frame for every shot

### 1. Attendance in the app
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** In this video for parents, you'll check your child's attendance in the Ad-Din app, and tell the madrasah ahead of time when they'll be away or late.
**Show:** Title card: "Check attendance and report an absence". Phone frame on the app home.

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your child is already enrolled, and teachers take attendance in class. You'll find it on your app home.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

#### [1.2a] Why it helps
**Say:** When you tell the madrasah ahead of time, the teacher knows why your child is missing before the class starts. Checking attendance also shows you early if your child is often late.
**Show:** Phone frame: the Attendance card with Today's statuses, then the Past excuses list.

### 2. Today
#### [2.1] Find the card
**Say:** Pick your child at the top of the home screen, then scroll down to the **Attendance** card.
**Show:** Tap "Maryam" next to Viewing. Scroll to the Attendance card.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** **Today** lists each of today's classes with its teacher and a status, such as **Present**, **Late**, **Absent** or **Sick**. **Not taken** means the teacher hasn't marked it yet.
**Show:** The Today tab is selected. Spotlight each row and its coloured status pill.
**Caption:** Today: each class with its status. Not taken means it isn't marked yet.

#### [2.3] When the teacher saves
**Say:** When the teacher saves attendance and tells parents, you get an app notification. If your child is absent, sick, late or picked up early, you get an email too.
**Show:** A notification banner about Maryam's Late status slides down over the home. Tap it away.

### 3. Subject by subject
#### [3.1] Subject Wise
**Say:** If your madrasah takes attendance for each subject, you'll also see **Subject Wise**. It shows each of today's lessons on its own.
**Show:** Tap "Subject Wise": rows "Quran" (Present) and "Arabic" (Late), each with the class and teacher.

### 4. The whole term
#### [4.1] Term dashboard
**Say:** **Term dashboard** sums up the term. The ring counts the school days, coloured by status, with a total for each.
**Show:** Tap "Term dashboard": the ring reads "40 days". Spotlight the legend: Present 35, Absent 2, Sick 1, Late 2.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Underneath is how much of the term your child was present. Pick another term from the box to look back.
**Show:** Spotlight the "present this term" line. Open the term box: "Term 1", "Term 2". Close it on Term 1.

#### [4.2a]
**Say:** If the madrasah hasn't set up its terms yet, the card says **No academic term is set up yet**. Ask the madrasah office if you see it.
**Show:** Cutaway: the Term dashboard tab reading "No academic term is set up yet".

### 5. Report an absence
#### [5.1] Open the form
**Say:** If your child will miss a day or arrive late, go back to **Today** and tap **Report an absence**.
**Show:** Tap "Today", then "Report an absence". The Attendance page opens on the "Submit Excuse" tab, with Class Details open.

#### [5.1a] One day in detail
**Say:** The page also has a **View** tab. Choose the class, section and child, pick a date, and tap **View** to see that day's status and any remarks from the teacher.
**Show:** Tap the "View" tab. Pick Maktab Level 1, A, Maryam Rahman; pick yesterday in the date; tap "View": the row shows "Late" and the remark "Arrived 10 minutes late". Tap "Submit Excuse" again.

#### [5.1b] Help and the legend
**Say:** The menu at the top has **Help** and **View status legend**, which explains each status. **Excuse** means an absence you told the madrasah about in advance.
**Show:** Tap the ⋮ menu; tap "View status legend": the "Status legend" sheet lists Present, Absent, Sick, Late, Early Pickup, Excuse ("Pre-approved absence") and Not Marked. Tap "Close".

#### [5.2] Choose your child
**Say:** Under **Class Details**, choose the **Class Name** and **Class Section**, then your **Child**.
**Show:** Pick Class Name "Maktab Level 1", Class Section "A", Child "Maryam Rahman". The panel folds away and "Past excuses" loads.

#### [5.3]
**Say:** **Past excuses** lists the ones you've already sent. To add one, tap **Submit new excuse**.
**Show:** Spotlight last week's "Sick" excuse. Tap "Submit new excuse": the "Attendance excuse" sheet slides up.

#### [5.3a]
**Say:** To find an older excuse, use **Filter by date**: pick a **From Date** and a **To Date**, and tap **Apply**. **Clear** shows them all again.
**Show:** Spotlight "Filter by date"; pick the first of last month and today; tap "Apply": the list narrows. Tap "Clear".

### 6. Fill in the excuse
#### [6.1] Dates
**Say:** Pick the **Start Date** and the **End Date**. For a single day, choose the same date in both.
**Show:** Pick next Monday in Start Date and again in End Date.

#### [6.2] Reason
**Say:** Choose a **Reason**: **Absent**, **Late**, **Sick** or **Early Pickup**. For late or early pickup, also set the **Time**.
**Show:** Open Reason and pick "Late": a Time field appears. Set it to 10:30.

#### [6.3] Save
**Say:** Add a short **Description** for the teacher, then tap **Save excuse**.
**Show:** Type "Dentist appointment, back by 10:30." in Description. Tap "Save excuse": toast "Successfully added the reason". The new excuse appears at the top of Past excuses.

#### [6.3a]
**Say:** The description is optional, but it helps the teacher. **Cancel** closes the sheet without saving anything.
**Show:** Spotlight the placeholder "Add details (optional)" (from the start of the take) and the "Cancel" button.

### 7. Edit or delete an excuse
#### [7.1]
**Say:** Until the teacher marks attendance for that day, you can edit or delete an excuse with the buttons on its card. After that, it shows **Attendance marked** and can't be changed.
**Show:** Spotlight the edit and delete buttons on the new excuse, then "Attendance marked" on last week's excuse.
**Caption:** Edit or delete an excuse until attendance is marked for that day.

#### [7.2] Delete one
**Say:** If plans change, tap the delete button on the excuse. Ad-Din asks **Delete excuse?**, because this can't be undone. Tap **Proceed** to delete it.
**Show:** Tap delete on a second test excuse (not the new one): the "Delete excuse?" box with "This action can't be reversed…". Tap "Proceed": the excuse leaves the list.

### 8. Next
#### [8.1] Recap
**Say:** Your excuse is now saved with your child's attendance. The teacher sees it in the class register, so the madrasah knows before the day comes.
**Show:** Past excuses with the new Late excuse at the top.

#### [8.1a]
**Say:** Ad-Din doesn't send the teacher a separate message about it. So send the excuse before the day, and the teacher sees it when they open the register.
**Show:** Cutaway: the teacher's register for next Monday with the excuse note in Maryam's row (from the 10.2 recording).

#### [8.2] Close
**Say:** Next, video 14.3: Follow Hifz progress and classwork. Or video 14.4: Report cards and interview bookings.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "14.3 Follow Hifz progress and classwork" and "14.4 Report cards and interview bookings".
