# 14.1 Your parent home in the app

## Goal
Find your way around the parent home in the Ad-Din app: a panel for each child with their routine and class announcements, enrollments waiting for approval, and the shortcuts below.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- The Ad-Din app, signed in with your parent account
- At least one child enrolled at your madrasah

## Covers
- A panel per child, My Routine, announcements, Enroll Now, Pending Approval
- Parent Portal pages from the Madrasah tile, including the read-only lists (Class, Subject, Holiday, Grade, Delegations)
- The same on the web: parent Dashboard and the Parent Portal in the sidebar

## Demo data
- Parent "Yusuf Rahman" (username `yusuf_rahman`, the demo world's account), set to the Parent role only for this video; home masjid "Maple Grove Masjid", home madrasah "Maple Grove Madrasah" (Premium, Class Announcements switched on)
- Children linked to Yusuf: "Maryam Rahman" (Maktab Level 1, section A, username `maryam_rahman`) and "Hamza Rahman" (Hifz Class A, added to the world as in 07.4), both in a class; "Zainab Rahman" (added to the world for this video) linked, with an enrollment request for "Maktab Level 1" in status Pending. Only Zainab has an enrollment request on record (the My enrollments card lists every request of each linked child, approved ones included)
- For the cards in [4.4]: Maryam is also in a class marked as a Quran Hifz class, with a Hifz record today (the Hifz progress card only shows for a child in a Hifz class); Maple Grove Madrasah has Academic Activity switched on (My Classwork only shows then)
- Today's routine for Maryam: three classes, one of them happening now
- Two class announcements for Maryam's class (one note, one photo) from her teacher
- Iqamah times for Maple Grove Masjid, two unread messages, one masjid event today
- Teacher "Fatima Siddiqui", given a Parent role and a linked child for the role tabs shot, labelled "Signed in as: teacher and parent Fatima Siddiqui"
- Parent "Tariq Saleh" with one linked child "Yahya Saleh" (inactive in the world), not in a class and no enrollment request, for the No Active Enrollments shot, labelled "Signed in as: parent Tariq Saleh"
- Yusuf has his own Premium (or a seat), so the Madrasah tile opens his Parent Portal pages instead of Go Premium; Maple Grove Madrasah has a holiday, a grade set and one teacher delegation for Maryam's class on record
- Phone frame for every shot, except a browser frame for the web shot in [6.4] (Yusuf signed in on the web, Parent role selected on the Dashboard)

### 1. Your home in the app
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** When you open the Ad-Din app as a parent, your home screen shows each child's day. In this video for parents, you'll see what each part of it does.
**Show:** Title card: "Your parent home in the app". Phone frame on Yusuf's app home.

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your children are already enrolled at your madrasah. Everything here comes straight from the madrasah, so there's nothing to set up.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

### 2. The top of the screen
#### [2.1] Header
**Say:** At the top is your organisation's name. The menu button on the left opens every page, and the settings button on the right opens your profile.
**Show:** Spotlight the header bar: the name, then the ☰ button, then the gear button. Don't tap them.

#### [2.2] Prayer times
**Say:** Below it are today's **Iqamah times** from your masjid, with a countdown to the next prayer. Tap the card to open the full prayer times.
**Show:** Spotlight the prayer card: "Iqamah times", the five prayers, the "in … min" countdown. Don't tap.

### 3. One panel per child
#### [3.1] Your card
**Say:** Your card greets you with **Assalamu alaikum** and your name. Underneath, it says how many of your linked children are in a class.
**Show:** Spotlight the identity card: "Assalamu alaikum", "Yusuf Rahman", and the line "2 of 3 linked children in a class here".

#### [3.2] Pick a child
**Say:** Next to **Viewing** there's a button for each child in a class. Tap a name, and everything below switches to that child.
**Show:** Tap "Hamza": the cards below change. Tap "Maryam" again.

#### [3.3]
**Say:** Tap the selected child again to see their class and username. Tap the username to copy it, in case the madrasah asks for it.
**Show:** Tap the "Maryam" button: the details open with Class "Maktab Level 1 · A" and Username "@maryam_rahman". Tap the username: toast "Copied to clipboard".

#### [3.4] More than one role
**Say:** If you're also a teacher at the madrasah, tabs at the top of your card switch between **Parent** and **Teacher**.
**Show:** Cut to Fatima Siddiqui, with the label "Signed in as: teacher and parent Fatima Siddiqui" in the side panel: tabs "Parent" and "Teacher" at the top of her card. Tap "Teacher", then "Parent". Cut back to Yusuf ("Signed in as: parent Yusuf Rahman").

### 4. Your child's day
#### [4.1] Routine
**Say:** First comes your child's routine, here **Maryam's Routine**. It lists today's classes with their times, and marks the one on **Now** and the **Next** one.
**Show:** Spotlight the routine card titled "Maryam's Routine"; zoom on the "Now" and "Next" tags.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Use the arrows to look at the other days of the week. Tap the day in the middle to jump back to **Today**.
**Show:** Tap the right arrow twice: the day label changes. Tap the label: it reads "Today" again. (The week runs Sunday to Saturday and the arrows stop at its ends, so record Sunday to Thursday.)

#### [4.3] Announcements
**Say:** **Class Announcements** shows the latest posts from your child's teachers, such as notes and photos. Tap **View all** to see every post.
**Show:** Spotlight the Class Announcements card with the two posts. Tap "View all": the Class Announcements page opens. Go back.

#### [4.4]
**Say:** Further down are attendance, Hifz progress and classwork. Videos 14.2 and 14.3 show them.
**Show:** Scroll slowly past the Attendance, Hifz progress and My Classwork cards, spotlighting each heading.

### 5. Enrollments waiting for approval
#### [5.1] My enrollments
**Say:** If you've applied to enroll a child, **My enrollments** shows the request and its status. **Pending** means the madrasah hasn't decided yet.
**Show:** Scroll to "My enrollments": row "Zainab Rahman", "Maktab Level 1", pill "Pending". Spotlight the hint "A child appears here once the madrasah approves the enrollment."

#### [5.2]
**Say:** Once the madrasah approves it, your child gets their own button and panel at the top. Tap a request to open your enrollments page.
**Show:** Tap the row: the enrollment page opens with Zainab's request. Go back.

#### [5.3] Enroll Now
**Say:** If none of your children is in a class yet, you'll see **No Active Enrollments** instead. Tap **Enroll Now** to apply for a program.
**Show:** Cut to parent Tariq Saleh, with the label "Signed in as: parent Tariq Saleh" in the side panel: card "No Active Enrollments", "Yahya is linked but not enrolled in a class." Spotlight "Enroll Now". Don't tap. Cut back to Yusuf ("Signed in as: parent Yusuf Rahman").

### 6. Shortcuts, messages and events
#### [6.1] Tiles
**Say:** Below your children are shortcut tiles, such as **Prayer Times**, **Quran**, **Events** and **Messages**.
**Show:** Scroll to the tile grid. Spotlight Prayer Times, Quran, Events, Messages in turn.

#### [6.1a] All your pages
**Say:** Tap **Madrasah** for every page of your **Parent Portal**. Besides attendance and Hifz, it lists your child's **Class**, **Subject**, **Holiday** dates, **Grade** scale, and **Delegations**, which show when another teacher covers a class.
**Show:** Tap the "Madrasah" tile: the Parent Portal list opens straight away (Yusuf has only one portal). Scroll slowly; spotlight Class, Subject, Holiday, Grade, Delegations. Tap "Delegations": one row with From Teacher, To Teacher and the dates. Go back twice to the home.
**Caption:** Tap Madrasah for every Parent Portal page: Class, Subject, Holiday, Grade, Delegations and more.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** The **Messages** card counts your unread messages, and **Today's events** lists what's on at your masjid.
**Show:** Spotlight the Messages card with "2 unread", then the Today's events card.

#### [6.3] Refresh
**Say:** Pull down from the top at any time to refresh your home.
**Show:** Scroll to the top and pull down: the Ad-Din logo spins, then the cards reload.

#### [6.4] On the web
**Say:** On a computer, sign in on the Ad-Din website. Your **Dashboard** sums up the same things for the child you pick, and **Parent Portal** in the sidebar has the same pages.
**Show:** Browser frame: Yusuf's Dashboard with the Parent role. Pick Maryam under "Select Child to view KPI :"; spotlight the Quran Hifz Assignments and attendance boxes. Then Sidebar → Parent Portal, expanded.

### 7. Next
#### [7.1] Recap
**Say:** That's your parent home: a panel for each child, their routine and announcements, any enrollment waiting for approval, and every other page one tap away.
**Show:** Yusuf's home, top of the screen, Maryam selected.

#### [7.2] Close
**Say:** Next, video 14.2: Check attendance and report an absence. Or video 14.3: Follow Hifz progress and classwork.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "14.2 Check attendance and report an absence" and "14.3 Follow Hifz progress and classwork".
