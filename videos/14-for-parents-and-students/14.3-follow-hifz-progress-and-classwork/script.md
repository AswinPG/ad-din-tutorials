# 14.3 Follow Hifz progress and classwork

## Goal
Follow your child's daily Hifz recitation and their classwork, with scores, from the parent home in the Ad-Din app.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- The Ad-Din app, signed in with your parent account
- A child in a Hifz class, and in a class that uses Academic Activity for classwork

## Covers
- Hifz progress card, My Classwork, scores
- Parent Library and Recognition
- Why follow it day by day; empty states (No Hifz entries for this date; all caught up)
- What parents receive: the Hifz result by email and app notification (10.4); grades only when the teacher turns on Notify to Parent/Guardian (10.6)
- The activity page: Teacher's notes, Resources, Marked out of, Counts toward grade; Your work, Add files and Turn in for your child; Comments with the teacher

## Demo data
- Parent "Yusuf Rahman" (the demo world's account) in the app; child "Hamza Rahman" (added to the world as in 07.4) in "Hifz Class A" (marked as a Quran Hifz class, which the Hifz progress card needs) and "Maktab Level 1"; Maple Grove Madrasah has Academic Activity switched on
- Today's Hifz records for Hamza from teacher "Bilal Hussain": a new-lesson task marked (mark 8, 2 mistakes, one failed attempt then a pass, 30 lines, 2 pages, a teacher note), and a revision task the student has submitted but the teacher hasn't marked
- Yesterday: one marked Hifz record, for the change-date shot
- Classwork for Hamza across both classes, all in the current term (the card shows the current term only): "Surah Al-Mulk test" graded 18/20 (weight 2), "Wudu poster" graded, not scored, "Seerah worksheet" due later this week, "Arabic vocabulary quiz" missing (two days late)
- Library: Hamza has one borrowed book on record ("Stories of the Prophets"); Recognition: one "Student of the Month" award for Hamza from Bilal Hussain
- Exam Marks stays off for Maple Grove Madrasah (the default), so Exams and Exam Marks are not in the menu
- "Seerah worksheet" has a teacher's note and a link under Resources; no files attached yet
- A day last week with no Hifz record, for the empty-day shot
- Hifz and grade notifications mocked; a demo notification for today's Hifz result
- Phone frame for every shot

### 1. Hifz and classwork at home
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** In this video for parents, you'll follow your child's Quran Hifz progress and their classwork, with scores, right from your Ad-Din app home.
**Show:** Title card: "Follow Hifz progress and classwork". Phone frame on the app home.

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your child is already in their classes, and the teachers record progress in Ad-Din. Pick your child at the top of the home screen to begin.
**Show:** Tap "Hamza" next to Viewing.

#### [1.2a] Why it helps
**Say:** Hifz grows a little every day, so a quick look tells you what to listen to at home tonight. Classwork shows you what's due before it becomes late.
**Show:** The Hifz progress card, then the My Classwork card with its Missing and Due soon counts.

### 2. Hifz progress
#### [2.1] The card
**Say:** Scroll down to **Hifz progress**. It shows what your child recited on one day, starting with today.
**Show:** Scroll to the Hifz progress card. Spotlight the date chip showing today.

#### [2.2] Tasks
**Say:** Each tab is one Hifz task for that day, such as the new lesson or revision. Tap a tab to see it.
**Show:** Tap the second tab (the revision task), then the first tab again.

#### [2.3] What was recited
**Say:** **Start** and **End** show where the recitation began and finished. **Total** shows how much that was, in lines and pages, or in Juz'.
**Show:** Spotlight Start and End, then "Total 30 lines · 2 pages".

#### [2.4] The result
**Say:** The badge shows the result. A mark with the number of mistakes means the teacher has marked it. **Submitted** means it's waiting for the teacher, and **Not marked** means it hasn't been submitted or marked yet.
**Show:** Zoom on the badge "8 · 2 mistakes". Tap the second tab: badge "Submitted". Tap the first tab again.
**Caption:** Mark and mistakes: marked. Submitted: waiting for the teacher. Not marked: not submitted or marked yet.

#### [2.5] Attempts and notes
**Say:** **Attempts** counts the tries: a cross for each failed one and a tick for the pass. Tap the teacher's note to read it in full.
**Show:** Spotlight "Attempts 2" with a cross and a tick. Tap the note: it expands. Spotlight "Teacher: Bilal Hussain" at the bottom.

#### [2.6] Another day
**Say:** To look back, tap the date and pick another day.
**Show:** Tap the date chip, pick yesterday. The card shows yesterday's record.

#### [2.6a]
**Say:** On a day without a lesson, the card says **No Hifz entries for this date**.
**Show:** Tap the date chip and pick the empty day last week: "No Hifz entries for this date". Pick today again.

#### [2.6b] When it's marked
**Say:** When the teacher marks a task, you and your child get an email and an app notification with the result, the mistakes and the teacher's note.
**Show:** A notification banner with Hamza's Hifz result slides down over the home. Tap it away.

### 3. My Classwork
#### [3.1] The card
**Say:** Next is **My Classwork**: the activities your child's teachers set, such as tests, worksheets and projects.
**Show:** Scroll to the My Classwork card. Spotlight its heading and the list.

#### [3.2] Filters
**Say:** The filters count each kind: **All**, **Missing**, **Due soon** and **Graded**. Tap one to see only those.
**Show:** Tap "Missing": "Arabic vocabulary quiz" with pill "Missing" and "2 days late". Tap "Due soon": "Seerah worksheet" with its due day.

#### [3.2a]
**Say:** The number on each filter shows how many activities it holds. If a filter is empty, the card says **Nothing here — all caught up, mashaAllah.**
**Show:** Zoom on the counts on All, Missing, Due soon and Graded. Cutaway: the empty message on a filter with nothing in it.

#### [3.3] One class
**Say:** If your child is in more than one class, use the class box to show just one of them.
**Show:** Open the class box: "All classes", "Hifz Class A", "Maktab Level 1". Pick "Maktab Level 1", then "All classes" again.

### 4. Scores, library books and awards
#### [4.1] Graded work
**Say:** Tap **Graded** to see the scores. Each one shows the mark out of the total, and its weight, which is how much it counts.
**Show:** Tap "Graded". Zoom on "Surah Al-Mulk test": "18/20", "weight 2".

#### [4.1a]
**Say:** Teachers can also tell you about each grade as they save it, but only if they turn on **Notify to Parent/Guardian**. So check this card too.
**Show:** Spotlight the new "18/20" on Surah Al-Mulk test.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Work that isn't scored shows **Completed** instead of a mark.
**Show:** Spotlight "Wudu poster" with the "Completed" pill.

#### [4.3] More detail
**Say:** Tap any activity to open its details. Tap **All activities** for the full list, which you can filter by class, subject and term.
**Show:** Tap "Surah Al-Mulk test": the Activity Score Management page opens on it. Go back. Spotlight "All activities" at the bottom of the card.

#### [4.3a] The activity page
**Say:** The activity page shows who set it, what it's **Marked out of**, whether it **Counts toward grade**, and any **Teacher's notes** and **Resources**, such as a link.
**Show:** Tap "Seerah worksheet": its page opens. Spotlight Added by, Counts toward grade, Teacher's notes and the link under Resources.

#### [4.3b] Hand in work
**Say:** Under **Your work**, you can tap **Add files** to attach your child's work, and **Turn in** to send it. After that, the files can't be changed.
**Show:** Spotlight "No files yet. Add your work below.", "Add files" and "Turn in". Don't tap Turn in.

#### [4.3c] Comments
**Say:** Under **Comments**, you can write to the teacher about this activity. Type your message and tap send.
**Show:** Spotlight the Comments box ("Type your message here…") and the send button. Tap "Cancel" to close the page.

#### [4.4] Library and Recognition
**Say:** There's more in the menu, under **Parent Portal**. **Library** lists the books your child has borrowed, and **Recognition** shows the awards their teachers have given.
**Show:** ☰ → Parent Portal → Library: the "Circulations" tab with "Stories of the Prophets" and its Issue Date. Go back. Parent Portal → Recognition: the "Student" tab with "Student of the Month" for Hamza. Go back to the home.

### 5. Next
#### [5.1] Recap
**Say:** Now you can follow your child's Hifz and classwork day by day, see each score as soon as it's marked, and find their library books and awards.
**Show:** The home with the Hifz progress and My Classwork cards in view.

#### [5.2] Close
**Say:** Next, video 14.4: Report cards and interview bookings. Or video 14.2: Check attendance and report an absence.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "14.4 Report cards and interview bookings" and "14.2 Check attendance and report an absence".
