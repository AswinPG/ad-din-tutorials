# 00.1 What Ad-Din does for your community

## Goal
Know what Ad-Din does for a masjid, a madrasah and the people around them, and which video to watch next.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Masjid and madrasah sides, the four portals, web and app, free vs Premium
- Who Ad-Din is for, with a Maple Grove example family
- Masjid pages (About Masjid, Staff List, Facility Details, Gallery, Forms, Volunteering) and Monthly Prayer Times
- How news reaches people: followers, the Message inbox, email and push
- Madrasah extras: online enrolment, Class Announcements, Staff Attendance, Reports
- App-only tools (Quran, Qiblah, Tasbeeh) and add-on services

## Demo data
- Demo masjid and madrasah (the shared demo organisation) with prayer times, two upcoming events, a recent announcement
- One demo account with Premium that is Masjid Admin and Madrasah Admin, and also has the Teacher, Parent (with an enrolled child) and Student roles, so Prayer Times, Donation and all four portals show in the sidebar (each portal needs its own role)
- Academic Activity switched on for the demo madrasah (Admin Portal → Academic Activity only shows then)
- One published report card for the demo child, for the quick cut in [3.2]
- The same account signed in on the app (phone frame) for the closing shot
- The masjid message "Community iftar this Friday" in the Message inbox for [2.3a]
- November's Monthly Prayer Times for Maple Grove Masjid for [2.1a]
- Staff Attendance switched on for the demo madrasah, so Admin Portal → Staff Attendance shows in [3.2b] (the demo world has it off by default)
- Maryam Rahman's Hifz entries (Parent Portal → Quran Hifz Assignments) and one class's attendance (Teacher Portal → Attendance) for [4.3a]

## Script

### 1. One place for your community
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Welcome to **Ad-Din**. It's one place where a masjid, a madrasah, and everyone around them can stay organised and connected.
**Show:** Login page with the slogan "Masjid, Madrasah & School Management Made Easy." Slow zoom on the Ad-Din logo.

#### [1.2]
**Say:** In this video you'll see what Ad-Din can do, who it's for, and which video to watch next.
**Show:** Title card: "What Ad-Din does for your community".

#### [1.2a] Who it's for
**Say:** Ad-Din is for the people who run a masjid or madrasah, for teachers, for parents and students, and for anyone who prays at a masjid. The examples in this video come from one community: Maple Grove Masjid and Maple Grove Madrasah.
**Show:** Card with five words in a row: Admins, Teachers, Parents, Students, Community. Then the web dashboard of the demo account, spotlight "Maple Grove Masjid" in the header.
**Caption:** Ad-Din is for admins, teachers, parents, students and anyone who prays at a masjid. The examples come from Maple Grove Masjid and Madrasah.

### 2. For the masjid
#### [2.1] Masjid tools
**Say:** For a masjid, Ad-Din shares your **Prayer Times**, so everyone who follows your masjid sees the right Adhan and Iqamah times.
**Show:** Signed in. Sidebar → Masjid → Prayer Times. Spotlight today's row.

#### [2.1a] Monthly timetable
**Say:** Members can also open **Monthly Prayer Times** to see the whole month, so a family can plan around Fajr and Isha without asking the office.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid → Monthly Prayer Times. The November table for Maple Grove Masjid; slow pan down the rows.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** You can share **Events**, collect a **Donation**, send news through **Message**, and stream live from the masjid with **Live Streaming**.
**Show:** Hover the sidebar items in turn: Events, Donation, Message, Live Streaming.

#### [2.2a] Your masjid's pages
**Say:** Your masjid's own pages tell people about it: **About Masjid**, the **Staff List**, **Facility Details**, a **Gallery**, and **Forms** people can download. With **Volunteering**, members can offer to help.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid, expanded. Spotlight About Masjid, Staff List, Facility Details, Gallery, Forms and Volunteering in turn.

#### [2.3]
**Say:** Members of the community find you with the **Masjid Finder**, follow you, and get your news on their phone.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid → Masjid Finder. The map with the demo masjid pinned.

#### [2.3a] How news reaches people
**Say:** For example, when Maple Grove Masjid sends a message about a community iftar, it lands in each follower's **Message** inbox. People who allow it also get it by email or as a phone notification.
**Show:** Top bar envelope → the Message inbox. Spotlight "Community iftar this Friday" at the top and open it.

### 3. For the madrasah
#### [3.1] Madrasah tools
**Say:** For a madrasah or school, Ad-Din runs the academic year: terms, classes, subjects, timetables, enrolment and attendance.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal, expanded. Spotlight Class, Subject, Class Routine, Attendance, Terms Management.

#### [3.1a] Enrolment
**Say:** Families can apply online. Under **Madrasah / School**, **Signup for a Program** opens the application form, and the admin approves each application before the child joins a class.
**Show:** Sidebar → Madrasah / School, expanded. Click "Signup for a Program": the online enrolment form opens for Maple Grove Madrasah. Don't fill it in.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Teachers track Quran Hifz progress, set Academic Activity, record marks, and share report cards with families.
**Show:** Admin Portal → Quran Hifz Assignments, then Academic Activity. Quick cut to a report card preview.

#### [3.2a] Keeping families informed
**Say:** Teachers post **Class Announcements** with notes and photos, and parents can report an absence from the app instead of phoning the office.
**Show:** Admin Portal → Class Announcements: the demo list. Cut to the app (phone frame), Parent tab: spotlight "Report an absence" on the attendance card.

#### [3.2b] Staff and reports
**Say:** With **Staff Attendance**, staff check in and out on their phone, and admins review the hours. **Reports** gives admins and teachers ready-made reports and a **Report Builder**.
**Show:** Admin Portal → Staff Attendance: its first page. Then Sidebar → Reports, expanded; spotlight Report Builder.

### 4. Four portals, one account
#### [4.1] The portals
**Say:** Everyone signs in with the same kind of account. Your role decides which of the four portals you see.
**Show:** Collapse the sidebar to its top level. Spotlight the four portals together.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** The **Admin Portal** runs the organisation. The **Teacher Portal** is for classes and marking. The **Parent Portal** and **Student Portal** show each child's classes, homework, Hifz and results.
**Show:** Spotlight each portal as it is named: Admin Portal, Teacher Portal, Parent Portal, Student Portal.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** One person can have more than one role. A teacher who is also a parent simply sees both portals.
**Show:** Spotlight Teacher Portal and Parent Portal together.

#### [4.3a] One family's day
**Say:** For example, Yusuf Rahman teaches at Maple Grove Madrasah, and his daughter Maryam studies there. He marks his class's attendance in the **Teacher Portal**, then checks Maryam's Hifz in the **Parent Portal**, with the same account.
**Show:** Teacher Portal → Attendance: the demo class list. Then Parent Portal → Quran Hifz Assignments: Maryam Rahman's entries.

#### [4.3b] Who gives you a role
**Say:** You don't choose a role yourself. A new account belongs to a member of the community. Your masjid or madrasah gives you a role, for example when an admin adds you as a teacher, or approves your child's enrolment.
**Show:** Card: "New account = community member. Your masjid or madrasah gives you a role." Then the sidebar of a member with no role: Masjid, Madrasah / School, Message and Events, and no portals.
**Caption:** You don't choose a role yourself. A new account is a community member; your masjid or madrasah gives you a role.

### 5. Web and app, free and Premium
#### [5.1] Web and app
**Say:** Ad-Din works in your web browser and in the Ad-Din app. Admins usually set things up on the web; families mostly use the app.
**Show:** Split screen: the web dashboard on the left, the app home screen in a phone frame on the right.

#### [5.1a] In the app
**Say:** The app also has tools for everyone: the **Quran**, the **Qiblah** direction, a **Tasbeeh** counter, and notifications on your phone when your masjid or madrasah sends news.
**Show:** Phone frame: the app home screen. Spotlight the Quran, Qiblah and Tasbeeh tiles in turn.

#### [5.2] Plans
**Say:** Many masjid features are free. Madrasah tools, the portals, events and donations come with **Ad-Din Premium**, and the website is an add-on.
**Show:** Card with three rows: Free, Premium, Add-ons. No prices on screen.
**Caption:** Many masjid features are free. Madrasah tools, portals, events and donations need Ad-Din Premium; the website is an add-on.

#### [5.2a] Who pays
**Say:** You can get Premium for yourself, or your masjid or madrasah can give you one of its Premium seats. Organisations can also add extra services, such as a public website or Prayer Times Premium for a masjid's TV screens.
**Show:** The same card: highlight the Premium row ("for yourself, or a seat from your organisation"), then the Add-ons row ("website, Prayer Times Premium"). No prices on screen.
**Caption:** Get Premium for yourself, or get one of your organisation's Premium seats. Organisations can add a website or Prayer Times Premium.

### 6. Where to go next
#### [6.1] Next steps
**Say:** If you're new to Ad-Din, watch video 00.2, Create your Ad-Din account. If you're setting up a masjid or madrasah, watch video 01.1, Register a masjid, madrasah, or both.
**Show:** End card with three tiles: "00.2 Create your Ad-Din account", "01.1 Register a masjid, madrasah, or both" and "02.1 How Ad-Din plans work". Spotlight the first two as they are named.

#### [6.1a]
**Say:** To find out which plan you need, watch video 02.1, How Ad-Din plans work.
**Show:** Same end card; spotlight the "02.1 How Ad-Din plans work" tile.

#### [6.2] Close
**Say:** Every video in this series stands on its own, so pick the one you need. Welcome to Ad-Din.
**Show:** Ad-Din logo end card.
