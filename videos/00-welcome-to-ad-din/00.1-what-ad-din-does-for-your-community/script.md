# 00.1 What Ad-Din does for your community

## Goal
Know what Ad-Din does for a masjid, a madrasah and the people around them, and which video to watch next.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Masjid and madrasah sides, the four portals, web and app, free vs Premium

## Demo data
- Demo masjid and madrasah (the shared demo organisation) with prayer times, two upcoming events, a recent announcement
- One demo account with Premium that is Masjid Admin and Madrasah Admin, and also has the Teacher, Parent (with an enrolled child) and Student roles, so Prayer Times, Donation and all four portals show in the sidebar (each portal needs its own role)
- Academic Activity switched on for the demo madrasah (Admin Portal → Academic Activity only shows then)
- One published report card for the demo child, for the quick cut in [3.2]
- The same account signed in on the app (phone frame) for the closing shot

## Script

### 1. One place for your community
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Welcome to **Ad-Din**. It's one place where a masjid, a madrasah, and everyone around them can stay organised and connected.
**Show:** Login page with the slogan "Masjid, Madrasah & School Management Made Easy." Slow zoom on the Ad-Din logo.

#### [1.2]
**Say:** In the next few minutes you'll see what Ad-Din can do, who it's for, and where to go next in this series.
**Show:** Title card: "What Ad-Din does for your community".

### 2. For the masjid
#### [2.1] Masjid tools
**Say:** For a masjid, Ad-Din keeps **Prayer Times** accurate, so everyone who follows your masjid sees the right Adhan and Iqamah times.
**Show:** Signed in. Sidebar → Masjid → Prayer Times. Spotlight today's row.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** You can share **Events**, collect a **Donation**, post updates through **Message**, and even stream live from the masjid.
**Show:** Hover the sidebar items in turn: Events, Donation, Message, Live Streaming.

#### [2.3]
**Say:** Members of the community find you with the **Masjid Finder**, follow you, and get your news on their phone.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid → Masjid Finder. The map with the demo masjid pinned.

### 3. For the madrasah
#### [3.1] Madrasah tools
**Say:** For a madrasah or school, Ad-Din runs the academic year: terms, classes, subjects, timetables, enrolment and attendance.
**Show:** Sidebar → Admin Portal, expanded. Spotlight Class, Subject, Class Routine, Attendance, Terms Management.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Teachers track Quran Hifz progress, set Academic Activity, record marks, and share report cards with families.
**Show:** Admin Portal → Quran Hifz Assignments, then Academic Activity. Quick cut to a report card preview.

### 4. Four portals, one account
#### [4.1] The portals
**Say:** Everyone signs in with the same kind of account. What you see depends on your role, through four portals.
**Show:** Collapse the sidebar to its top level. Spotlight the four portals together.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** The **Admin Portal** runs the organisation. The **Teacher Portal** is for classes and marking. The **Parent Portal** and **Student Portal** show each child's classes, homework, Hifz and results.
**Show:** Spotlight each portal as it is named: Admin Portal, Teacher Portal, Parent Portal, Student Portal.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** One person can have more than one role. A teacher who is also a parent simply sees both portals.
**Show:** Spotlight Teacher Portal and Parent Portal together.

### 5. Web and app, free and Premium
#### [5.1] Web and app
**Say:** Ad-Din works in your web browser and in the Ad-Din app. Admins usually set things up on the web; families mostly use the app.
**Show:** Split screen: the web dashboard on the left, the app home screen in a phone frame on the right.

#### [5.2] Plans
**Say:** Many masjid features are free. Madrasah tools, the portals, events and donations come with **Ad-Din Premium**, and the website is an add-on.
**Show:** Card with three rows: Free, Premium, Add-ons. No prices on screen.
**Caption:** Many masjid features are free. Madrasah tools, portals, events and donations need Ad-Din Premium; the website is an add-on.

### 6. Where to go next
#### [6.1] Next steps
**Say:** New to Ad-Din? Start by creating your account in the next video. Setting up a masjid or madrasah? Jump to registering your organisation.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "00.2 Create your Ad-Din account" and "01.1 Register a masjid, madrasah or both".

#### [6.2] Close
**Say:** Every video in this series is short and stands on its own, so pick the one you need. Welcome to Ad-Din.
**Show:** Ad-Din logo end card.
