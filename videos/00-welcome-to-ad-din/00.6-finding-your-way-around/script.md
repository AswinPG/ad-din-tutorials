# 00.6 Finding your way around

## Goal
Find your way around Ad-Din: the sidebar, the dashboard, the help menu on the web, and the home screen and portal tiles in the app.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Sidebar, portal tiles, dashboard, help menu (Knowledge Base, Training Courses), the app’s home screen
- Rest of the help menu: Contact Us, Release Notes, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Key Terms, FAQ
- Pinning quick actions in the app (Admin and Teacher tabs; the pins are shared with the web dashboard)

## Demo data
<!-- Records the demo organisation must have for this video (classes, students, events...). -->
- The shared demo organisation (masjid and madrasah, both approved) with prayer times, two upcoming events, recent messages
- One demo account that is Madrasah Admin, Masjid Admin and a parent of one enrolled child, so the sidebar shows Parent Portal and Admin Portal and the dashboard role menu offers Admin and Parent; the account has Ad-Din Premium, so the portals open
- Dashboard tiles with demo numbers; two pinned Quick Actions (for example Class and Message; not Attendance, which [3.3] pins on camera)
- The same account signed in on the app (phone frame): prayer card, identity card with Parent and Admin tabs, the tile grid, messages and events; the Admin tab's Quick actions card shows the web pins (Class, Message, and Attendance after [3.3])
- Knowledge Base and Training Courses open external sites in a new tab: show the landing pages only, never sign in on camera

### 1. A quick tour
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** In this video you'll see where to find everything in Ad-Din, on the web and in the app.
**Show:** Title card: "Finding your way around".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** What you see depends on your roles. This account runs a masjid and madrasah, and is also a parent, so it sees most sections.
**Show:** Signed in on the web; the dashboard with the sidebar open.

### 2. The sidebar
#### [2.1] Sections
**Say:** On the web, the sidebar on the left lists every section you can use: **Dashboard**, **Masjid**, **Madrasah / School**, **Message**, **Events**, and more.
**Show:** Slow pan down the sidebar, spotlighting each top-level item as it's named.

#### [2.2] Portals
**Say:** Further down are your portals, one for each of your roles. This account has the **Parent Portal** and the **Admin Portal**; tap one to open its pages. Portal pages need Ad-Din Premium, as video 02.1 explains.
**Show:** Click "Admin Portal": it expands to its pages (User Management, Class, Attendance...). Collapse it again.

#### [2.3] Close or pin it
**Say:** The menu button at the top makes the sidebar narrow, with icons only; point at an icon to see its pages. Tap the button again to open the sidebar fully. The pin keeps it open while you work.
**Show:** Click the menu icon: the sidebar collapses to icons. Hover an icon to show its pages. Reopen it, then click the pin icon.

#### [2.4]
**Say:** Your organisation's logo at the top always brings you back to the dashboard. **Logout** is at the bottom.
**Show:** Spotlight the logo at the top of the sidebar, then "Logout" at the bottom.

### 3. The dashboard
#### [3.1] At a glance
**Say:** The **Dashboard** greets you and shows your home organisation with its ID. Switch between the **Madressah/School Dashboard**, for your madrasah, and the **Masjid Dashboard** at the top.
**Show:** Spotlight the greeting and the ID badge. Click "Masjid Dashboard", then back to "Madressah/School Dashboard".

#### [3.2] Roles
**Say:** If you have more than one role, the role menu on the right changes the view, for example from **Admin** to **Parent**.
**Show:** On the Madressah/School Dashboard (the masjid view has no Parent role), open the role dropdown on the right; choose Parent, then back to Admin.

#### [3.3] Quick Actions
**Say:** Under **Quick Actions**, pin the pages you use most. Tap the search bar, type the page name under **Add Quick Actions**, and tap the pin next to it.
**Show:** Click "Search module, Select to favorite"; the Add Quick Actions window opens. Type "Attendance", pin it, close the window. The new card appears.

#### [3.4]
**Say:** On the right you'll also find prayer times, a calendar and your latest messages. **Manage Dashboard Tiles** hides the boxes you don't need.
**Show:** Pan the right column. Click "Manage Dashboard Tiles": the list of switches opens. Close it.

### 4. The top bar and help
#### [4.1] Top bar
**Say:** In the top bar, the envelope opens your messages.
**Show:** Spotlight the envelope icon.

#### [4.2] Help menu
**Say:** Tap **Profile** to open a menu with your **User Profile** and the help pages. **Knowledge Base** has step-by-step articles, and **Training Courses** has online lessons.
**Show:** Click "Profile": the menu drops down. Spotlight "User Profile", "Knowledge Base" and "Training Courses". Click "Knowledge Base": its landing page opens in a new tab. Close the tab and return.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** **Contact Us** is for support. The same menu also has **Release Notes**, **Privacy Policy**, **Terms of Service**, **Key Terms** and **FAQ**. Each one opens in a new browser tab.
**Show:** Open the Profile menu again. Spotlight the block from Contact Us to FAQ as one group, then hover Contact Us.

### 5. The app's home screen
#### [5.1] Home
**Say:** In the Ad-Din app, you start on the home screen. Your organisation's name is at the top, with the menu button on the left and settings on the right.
**Show:** Phone frame: the app home screen. Spotlight the title, the menu icon and the gear icon.

#### [5.2]
**Say:** The menu button opens the same sidebar you saw on the web. The prayer card shows today's Iqamah times.
**Show:** Tap the menu icon: the drawer slides in; tap outside to close it. Spotlight the prayer card.

#### [5.3] Your card
**Say:** Your card shows your name, and a tab for each role, such as **Parent** and **Admin**. Tap a tab to switch.
**Show:** Spotlight the identity card; tap "Parent", then "Admin".

#### [5.3a] Quick actions
**Say:** On the **Admin** and **Teacher** tabs, **Quick actions** shows the pages you pinned on the dashboard.
**Show:** On the Admin tab, spotlight the Quick actions chips, including Attendance from [3.3].

#### [5.3b]
**Say:** To change them, tap the pen, tap **Pin** next to a page, then tap **Done**.
**Show:** Tap the pen: the "Pin quick actions" sheet opens. Type "Subject" in "Search modules", tap "+ Pin" so it shows "Pinned", tap "Done". The new chip appears.

#### [5.4] Tiles
**Say:** Each tile opens a page with one tap: **Prayer Times**, **Quran**, **Messages**, **Events**, **Qiblah**, **Donation** and more.
**Show:** Spotlight the tile grid, then the Messages and Events cards below it.

#### [5.5] Portal tiles
**Say:** Tap **Madrasah** to see a tile for each portal you have, like the **Parent/Guardian Portal** and the **Admin Portal**. The **Masjid** tile works the same way.
**Show:** Tap the "Madrasah" tile: the portal tiles open. Spotlight both. Go back, tap "Masjid": the masjid pages list.

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Close
**Say:** Now you know your way around. Next, set up notifications and location on your phone in video 00.7. If you run a masjid or madrasah, register it in video 01.1.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "00.7 Ad-Din on your phone: notifications and location" and "01.1 Register a masjid, madrasah, or both".
