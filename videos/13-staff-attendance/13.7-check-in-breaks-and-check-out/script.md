# 13.7 Check in, breaks and check out

## Goal
Record your working day in the Ad-Din app: check in, take a break, check out, give a reason when asked, and answer a card about an earlier day.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- The Ad-Din app, signed in with your own account
- Your organisation has set you up for Staff Attendance
- Ad-Din Premium; your organisation usually gives you a seat
- Location allowed for the Ad-Din app (video 00.7)

## Covers
- Welcome notice, location dot, reason sheet, “Needs your attention”
- Why and good practice (location only at the tap)
- When the Check in button appears (the check-in window), Shifts link with today's times, Check-in window closed
- Location off: Open settings or Record without location; Locating… and Cancel
- Break auto-close warning; no Break button when breaks aren't tracked
- Leaving early or staying late (reason sheet, Overtime); See today's record
- Reason needed chip; phone reminders (No check-in recorded, No check-out recorded, Break closed on your record, Shift ending soon)
- Got it on a card; who decides and how the card clears; On approved leave

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Staff Attendance lets you record your working day from your phone. In this video for staff and teachers, you'll check in, take a break, and check out.
**Show:** Title card: "Check in, breaks and check out".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your organisation has already set you up. All you need is the Ad-Din app, signed in with your own account, with location allowed. Staff Attendance needs Ad-Din Premium; your organisation usually gives you a seat.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

#### [1.2a] Why
**Say:** It replaces the paper sign-in sheet, and it's your own record of the hours you work. You can see every tap you make, and any change your admin makes to your day.
**Show:** Phone frame (side panel: "Signed in as: Khadija Usman, office staff"): the Today screen with the clock card; then tap "My attendance" and show the day's taps. Go back.

### 2. Find it
#### [2.1] Your clock card
**Say:** Open the Ad-Din app. If your organisation uses Staff Attendance, your clock card is on your home screen. You can also open **My Attendance** from the menu.
**Show:** Phone frame, 8:20 (side panel: "Signed in as: Khadija Usman, office staff"). App home screen; zoom on the clock card. Then open the menu and spotlight "My Attendance".

### 3. The first time
#### [3.1] What's recorded
**Say:** The first time, tap **Read it now** on the clock card. Ad-Din shows **What's recorded when you tap**.
**Show:** On the clock card, spotlight "Before your first tap, read what Staff Attendance records about you." Tap "Read it now". The notice opens; zoom on its title.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Your location is read only when you tap **Check in** or **Check out**. It's never tracked in the background. When you've read it, tap **Got it**, and **Got it** again to continue.
**Show:** Scroll slowly through the statements. Tap "Got it", then "Got it" again to continue.

### 4. Check in
#### [4.1] Your Today screen
**Say:** This is your **Today** screen. Before your shift, the card counts down: **Shift starts in**. Below it are today's shift and break times.
**Show:** Today screen at 8:24. Zoom on the card, then spotlight the shift and break lines below it.

#### [4.1a] When you can check in
**Say:** The **Check in** button appears a few minutes before your shift starts; your organisation sets how many. You can only check in during a shift that's scheduled for you. Tap **Shifts** at the bottom of the card to see today's times.
**Show:** Still at 8:24, spotlight the "Check in" button under the countdown (it appeared at 8:20). Tap "Shifts" in the card footer; the popup lists "Shift …" and "Break …". Tap away.

#### [4.2] Check in
**Say:** When you arrive, tap **Check in**. It's eight thirty-three. Your organisation allows ten minutes of grace, so your day counts from eight thirty.
**Show:** Clock moves to 8:33. Tap "Check in". The card shows "Locating…", then turns to Checked in. Zoom on "Checked in at 8:33" and "Recorded 8:30".

#### [4.2a] If location is off
**Say:** If location is off for the app, Ad-Din tells you before it sends the tap. Tap **Open settings** to allow it, or **Record without location**. Either way, your tap keeps the time you pressed.
**Show:** Another morning, 8:31, location off for the app. Tap "Check in": the sheet "Location is off for this app" opens. Spotlight "Open settings", then tap "Record without location".

#### [4.3] The location dot
**Say:** Tap the location dot to see the position saved with your tap, and whether it was **In the zone**.
**Show:** Tap the dot on the card; the popup shows "In the zone", the time it was read, and the position. Tap away to close it.

### 5. Breaks
#### [5.1] Start a break
**Say:** At lunch, tap **Break**. The card times your break and shows when you're due back. If you forget to end it, Ad-Din closes it for you. When you're back, tap **End break**.
**Show:** Clock moves to 12:01. Tap "Break". Zoom on the card: On break, "Back by 12:30". Clock moves to 12:28. Tap "End break".

#### [5.1a]
**Say:** While you're on break, the card tells you when your record will close it if you don't end it. If your organisation doesn't track breaks, there's no **Break** button: unpaid breaks are taken off your day automatically.
**Show:** Back at 12:05 on break; zoom on the line "Counts as Lunch 12:00 – 12:30 · if you don't end it, your record closes it at …".

### 6. Check out
#### [6.1] Done for today
**Say:** At the end of your shift, tap **Check out**. The card says **Done for today**, with the hours you recorded.
**Show:** Clock moves to 4:33. Tap "Check out". Zoom on "Done for today" and the recorded hours.

#### [6.1a] See today's record
**Say:** Tap **See today's record** to check your taps for the day, as in video 13.8.
**Show:** Spotlight "See today's record" in place of the button.

#### [6.1b] Staying late
**Say:** If you check out well after your shift ends, or well before, Ad-Din asks why. Pick a reason, such as **Overtime**, and tap **Send**. Your admin decides whether the extra time is paid.
**Show:** Thursday, clock at 5:10. Tap "Check out". The sheet "You're checking out after your shift ended — why?" opens. Tap "Overtime", then "Send".

### 7. When Ad-Din asks why
#### [7.1] Outside the zone
**Say:** Some days you might tap away from your usual place, for example at an off-site event. Your tap is already saved. Ad-Din just asks: **You were outside the zone — why?**
**Show:** Another morning, 8:31. Tap "Check in". The sheet opens; zoom on its title.

#### [7.2]
**Say:** Pick the reason that fits, such as **Work activity at another location**, add a note if you like, and tap **Send**.
**Show:** Tap "Work activity at another location". Tap "Send". The sheet closes.

#### [7.2a] Reason needed
**Say:** You can close the sheet without answering. Your tap stays recorded, and a **Reason needed** chip stays on your card. Tap it to answer later.
**Show:** On a second out-of-zone tap, close the sheet. Zoom on the "Reason needed" chip; tap it and the sheet opens again.

#### [7.3] Forgot to check in
**Say:** If you forgot to check in this morning, tap **Record a check-in**, choose why, and tap **Record it**. Until your admin reviews it, your day starts at your shift's start time.
**Show:** Another morning, 9:40. Tap "Record a check-in at 8:30". Tap "I was there but forgot to check in", then "Record it".

#### [7.3a] Reminders on your phone
**Say:** If no check-in is recorded after your shift starts, your phone shows **No check-in recorded**. You can still record it until your shift ends. After that, the card says **Check-in window closed**, and your admin corrects the day.
**Show:** Phone lock screen with the mocked notification "No check-in recorded". Then 6:00 PM on another day: zoom on "Check-in window closed" and the sentence under it.

#### [7.3b]
**Say:** Other reminders are **No check-out recorded** and **Break closed on your record**, and, if your organisation switches it on, **Shift ending soon**. Apart from that one, a normal day brings no notification at all.
**Show:** Show the three notification titles one after another on the lock screen (mocked).

### 8. Needs your attention
#### [8.1] A card about an earlier day
**Say:** If an earlier day needs a look, it shows under **Needs your attention**. Here, you forgot to check out on Tuesday, so the system closed your day at the scheduled end.
**Show:** Today screen. Scroll to "Needs your attention · 1"; zoom on the card "… your day was closed for you".

#### [8.2]
**Say:** Tap **Tell us what happened**, pick the closest answer, and tap **Send**. Your admin reviews it, and you'll see their decision on the same card.
**Show:** Tap "Tell us what happened". In "What happened?", tap "I was there, forgot to check out", then "Send". The card shows "Sent · awaiting admin".

#### [8.2a] Got it
**Say:** If the record is right, tap **Got it** instead. A card stays until your admin decides; then it shows who reviewed it, and the outcome.
**Show:** Spotlight "Got it" on another card. Then zoom on a settled card from an earlier week: "Yusuf Rahman reviewed it and marked it approved."

#### [8.3] On leave
**Say:** On a day of approved time off, the card says **On approved leave**, and there's nothing to tap.
**Show:** Switch the demo to the approved leave day; zoom on "On approved leave".

### 9. Next
#### [9.1] Close
**Say:** Your day is recorded. Next, watch 13.8, Timesheet, time off and pay periods, to see your hours, ask for time off, and check when you'll be paid.
**Show:** End card: "13.8 Timesheet, time off and pay periods".
