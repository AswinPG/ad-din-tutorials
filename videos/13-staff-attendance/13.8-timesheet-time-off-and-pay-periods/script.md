# 13.8 Timesheet, time off and pay periods

## Goal
Check your recorded day in the Ad-Din app, ask for time off, and see when each pay period is signed off and paid.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- The Ad-Din app, signed in with your own account
- Your organisation has set you up for Staff Attendance

## Covers
- Day view, Request time off, pay periods

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Staff Attendance also shows your recorded hours, lets you ask for time off, and tells you when you'll be paid. This video shows staff where to find each one.
**Show:** Title card: "Timesheet, time off and pay periods".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** You're already set up and recording your time. Open **My Attendance**. The tabs along the bottom are **Today**, **Timesheet**, **Time off** and **Pay periods**.
**Show:** Phone frame. Menu → My Attendance. Spotlight the four tabs at the bottom.

### 2. Your day in detail
#### [2.1] Timesheet
**Say:** Tap **Timesheet**. It opens on today: **Hours recorded**, **Breaks taken**, and under **Clock events**, every tap, newest first.
**Show:** Tap "Timesheet". Zoom on Hours recorded and Breaks taken, then scroll through the clock events.

#### [2.2] Open a tap
**Say:** Tap one to see when you tapped, what it was **Recorded as**, and where your location was checked.
**Show:** Tap "Checked in". Zoom on "You tapped at", "Recorded as" and "Location checked at".

#### [2.3] History
**Say:** **History** shows anything the system or your administrator changed on this day, and why.
**Show:** Tap the previous-day arrow to open yesterday, then tap "History". Spotlight "These hours were set by …" and the reason.

#### [2.4]
**Say:** Use the arrows to move between days. If the system recorded a day for you and it looks wrong, tap **Doesn't look right** and tell your administrator what happened.
**Show:** Step back to the provisional day. Spotlight "Doesn't look right"; don't send.

### 3. Ask for time off
#### [3.1] Request time off
**Say:** To ask for time off, open **Time off** and tap **Request time off**.
**Show:** Tap the "Time off" tab. Tap "Request time off"; "Ask for time off" opens.

#### [3.2] Dates and category
**Say:** Choose your **First day** and **Last day**. For a single day, leave the last day blank. Pick a **Category**, paid or unpaid, and add a note if you like.
**Show:** Set First day and Last day. Tap "Annual leave". Type the note.

#### [3.3] Send
**Say:** Tap **Send**. Under **Your requests**, it shows **Waiting for a decision** until your administrator answers. You'll get an email when they do.
**Show:** Tap "Send". Zoom on the new request with "Waiting for a decision".

### 4. Pay periods
#### [4.1] This period
**Say:** Open **Pay periods**. The top card shows the **Current pay period**, when its hours are due to be signed off, and when payment is scheduled.
**Show:** Tap the "Pay periods" tab. Zoom on the top card: Current pay period, Sign-off due, Payment scheduled.

#### [4.2] Every period
**Say:** Scroll down for every period, past and upcoming, or filter them, for example by **Signed off**. Payment dates are a schedule, not a confirmation that you've been paid.
**Show:** Scroll down the list. Tap "Signed off", then "All". Spotlight the note at the bottom.

### 5. Next
#### [5.1] Close
**Say:** Now you know where your hours, time off and pay dates live. If anything looks wrong, tell your administrator right from the app.
**Show:** End card: "13.7 Check in, breaks and check out".
