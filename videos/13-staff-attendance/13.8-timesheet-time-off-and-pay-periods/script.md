# 13.8 Timesheet, time off and pay periods

## Goal
Check your recorded day in the Ad-Din app, ask for time off, and see when each pay period is signed off and paid.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- The Ad-Din app, signed in with your own account
- Your organisation has set you up for Staff Attendance
- Ad-Din Premium; your organisation usually gives you a seat

## Covers
- Day view, Request time off, pay periods
- The correction sheet on the day view (Doesn't look right → What happened?)
- Why check your own record
- Provisional and Why this day is Provisional; the location sentence on each tap (no map); Previous day, Next day, Today; On hold
- Time off: taking single days out of a range, the 31-day and 90-days-back limits, no categories yet
- Your requests: Approved, Not approved, Answered by, Reason
- Pay period filters, next payment, more projected periods

- An earlier provisional day recorded by the system, so "Doesn't look right" shows
- The organisation's correction reason list (defaults), so the What happened? sheet has choices

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Staff Attendance also shows your recorded hours, lets you ask for time off, and tells you when you'll be paid. In this video for staff and teachers, you'll find each one.
**Show:** Title card: "Timesheet, time off and pay periods".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** You're already set up and recording your time. Open the menu, then **My Attendance**. The tabs along the bottom are **Today**, **Timesheet**, **Time off** and **Pay periods**.
**Show:** Phone frame (side panel: "Signed in as: Khadija Usman, office staff"). Menu → My Attendance. Spotlight the four tabs at the bottom.

#### [1.2a] Why
**Say:** Check your hours here before the end of each pay period. If something is wrong, tell your admin while there is still time to fix it, before the period is signed off.
**Show:** Tap "Pay periods"; zoom on "Sign-off due" on the top card. Tap "Today".

### 2. Your day in detail
#### [2.1] Timesheet
**Say:** Tap **Timesheet**. It opens on today: **Hours recorded**, **Breaks taken**, and under **Clock events**, every tap, newest first.
**Show:** Tap "Timesheet". Zoom on Hours recorded and Breaks taken, then scroll through the clock events.

#### [2.1a] Provisional
**Say:** If a day shows **Provisional**, the system had to fill in part of it, for example when you forgot to check out. Tap the word to read why. It goes away once your admin settles the day.
**Show:** On the earlier provisional day, tap "Provisional": the popup "Why this day is Provisional" opens; zoom on it, then close it. Return to today.

#### [2.2] Open a tap
**Say:** Tap one to see when you tapped, what it was **Recorded as**, and where your location was checked.
**Show:** Tap "Checked in". Zoom on "You tapped at", "Recorded as" and "Location checked at".

#### [2.2a]
**Say:** Each tap says, in words, whether your position was recorded. There's no map and no coordinates, and your location is never read between taps.
**Show:** Zoom on "Your position was recorded with this tap."

#### [2.3] History
**Say:** **History** shows anything the system or your admin changed on this day, and why.
**Show:** Tap the previous-day arrow to open yesterday. Spotlight the box "These hours were set by …" with its reason, above the tabs. Then tap "History" and spotlight "Your hours for this day were set by hand."

#### [2.4]
**Say:** Use the arrows to move between days. If the system recorded a day for you and it looks wrong, tap **Doesn't look right**.
**Show:** Step back to the provisional day. Spotlight the sentence above the button, then tap "Doesn't look right".

#### [2.4b] Moving between days
**Say:** Use **Previous day** and **Next day** to move, and **Today** to come back. If a day says **On hold**, its pay period was already signed off without it. Ask your admin: they can reopen the period to bring it in.
**Show:** After the correction is sent, tap "Previous day" a few times to the day in last month; zoom on "On hold — not counted in the signed-off period". Tap "Today".

#### [2.4a] What happened?
**Say:** In **What happened?**, pick the closest answer, add a note if you like, and tap **Send**. Your admin reviews the day, and you'll see their answer here.
**Show:** The "What happened?" sheet rises over the dimmed day. Tap the first answer, type a short note under "Add a note (optional)", tap "Send". The sheet closes; the day now says the administrator will review it.

### 3. Ask for time off
#### [3.1] Request time off
**Say:** To ask for time off, open **Time off** and tap **Request time off**.
**Show:** Tap the "Time off" tab. Tap "Request time off"; "Ask for time off" opens.

#### [3.2] Dates and category
**Say:** Choose your **First day** and **Last day**. For a single day, leave the last day blank. Pick a **Category**, paid or unpaid, and add a note if you like.
**Show:** Set First day and Last day. Tap "Annual leave". Type the note.

#### [3.2a] Take days out
**Say:** The days you picked show one by one, so you can take out a day you don't need, such as a weekend. One request can have up to thirty-one days, and can go back up to ninety days.
**Show:** Zoom on "3 day(s) selected" and the three day chips. Spotlight the remove button on one chip; don't tap it.

#### [3.2b]
**Say:** The categories, and whether each one is paid, come from your admin. If none are offered yet, ask your admin to add them in the Staff Attendance settings.
**Show:** Zoom on the Category choices: Annual leave, Sick leave, Unpaid leave, Other, each marked Paid or Unpaid.

#### [3.3] Send
**Say:** Tap **Send**. Under **Your requests**, it shows **Waiting for a decision** until your admin answers. You'll see their answer here, and get an email too.
**Show:** Tap "Send". Zoom on the new request with "Waiting for a decision".

#### [3.3a] The answer
**Say:** An answered request shows **Approved** or **Not approved**, who answered it and when, and their reason if they gave one.
**Show:** Scroll to the earlier requests: zoom on "Approved", "Answered by Yusuf Rahman", then on "Not approved" with "Reason: Please use Annual leave for this."

### 4. Pay periods
#### [4.1] This period
**Say:** Open **Pay periods**. The top card shows the **Current pay period**, when its hours are due to be signed off, and when payment is scheduled.
**Show:** Tap the "Pay periods" tab. Zoom on the top card: Current pay period, Sign-off due, Payment scheduled.

#### [4.2] Every period
**Say:** Scroll down for every period, past and upcoming, or filter them, for example by **Signed off**. Payment dates are a schedule, not a confirmation that you've been paid.
**Show:** Scroll down the list. Tap "Signed off", then "All". Spotlight the note at the bottom.

#### [4.2a]
**Say:** The other filters are **In review**, **In progress** and **Upcoming**. The next payment date shows near the top, and a button at the end of the list adds periods further ahead.
**Show:** Spotlight the filter chips. Zoom on "Next payment …". Scroll down and tap "Show 3 more projected periods".

### 5. Next
#### [5.1] Close
**Say:** Now you know where to find your hours, time off and pay dates. If a day looks wrong, tap **Doesn't look right** on that day. To record your day, watch 13.7, Check in, breaks and check out.
**Show:** End card: "13.7 Check in, breaks and check out".
