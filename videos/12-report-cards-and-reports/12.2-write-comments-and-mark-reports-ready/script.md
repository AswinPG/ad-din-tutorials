# 12.2 Write comments and mark reports ready

## Goal
Open your class's report cards, write each student's comment yourself or with AI, and mark each card ready.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Comments, Marked as Ready
- Ad-Din's AI Comment Generator (Write with AI)
- Why comments and Ready matter; Report 1, 2 and 3 and the missing-term message
- Moving between students (the list, the count, the arrows)
- Marks on the card: typing a mark, the changed-mark colour, syncing a subject from Academic Activities, AutoSave; Days Absent and Times Late; Refresh
- The 350-character limit, Cancel, Insert blocked when too long; the translated comment
- Download this report, Save All to PDF and Download All for teachers (Email is for admins, 12.3)
- What happens next: parents see nothing until the admin releases the card

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Before report cards go home, each student needs a comment, and each card needs to be marked ready. In this video for teachers and admins, you'll do both.
**Show:** Title card: "Write comments and mark reports ready".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your admin has already mapped the report card to your class, and the marks come in from your Academic Activities.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

#### [1.2a] Why it matters
**Say:** The comment is the part of the card parents read most closely, so make it personal. Marking a card ready tells your admin it's finished, and only ready cards can go home.
**Show:** Cutaway: a finished card; zoom on the "Comment :" box, then on a tick in the Students list.

### 2. Open your report cards
#### [2.1] Report Builder
**Say:** Go to **Reports**, then **Report Builder**, and tap **Select** beside your report. If you can't tap **Select**, ask your admin to assign you a subject in that class.
**Show:** Sidebar → Reports → Report Builder. Spotlight "Teacher access". Click "Select" on "Term Report".

#### [2.2] Report Card Builder
**Say:** In the **Report Card Builder**, choose your class and section, then the report for this term.
**Show:** Pick "Maktab Level 1" and section "A". Click the "Report 1" card.

#### [2.2a] One report per term
**Say:** **Report 1** is for the first term. If your admin has mapped more terms, **Report 2** and **Report 3** appear too. If you see **Missing terms mappings, please contact your Ad-Din Administrator.**, ask your admin to map a term.
**Show:** Cutaway from the two-term mapping: spotlight the "Report 1" and "Report 2" cards. Back to the main take with "Report 1" selected.

#### [2.3]
**Say:** Leave **Add digital signature** ticked, and tap **Start**. Your students are listed on the left, and the first card opens.
**Show:** Spotlight the ticked "Add digital signature". Click "Start". The Students list fills; Zaid Karim's card opens on the right.

#### [2.3a] Move between students
**Say:** At the top, the list shows every student, and the count shows where you are, such as one of three. Pick a name to jump to that student, or use the arrows.
**Show:** Spotlight the student list showing "1. Zaid Karim" and the count "1 / 3". Open the list; pick "3. Ibrahim Nasser", then pick "1. Zaid Karim" again.

#### [2.4] The marks
**Say:** The card shows each subject's marks for the term, taken from Academic Activities, with the total and the average. Under each term, it also prints **Days Absent** and **Times Late** from attendance.
**Show:** Pan down Zaid's card: the subject rows, "TOTAL MARKS", "AVERAGE %", then the term box with "Days Absent: 1 | Times Late: 2".

#### [2.5] Change a mark
**Say:** To correct a mark, tap it in this term's highlighted column and type the new one. A mark you changed shows in a different colour. The round arrows beside a subject fetch its marks from Academic Activities again.
**Show:** Double-click Zaid's Quran mark in the TERM I column; type 19 and press Enter: it turns amber. Click the round arrows beside "Quran": toast "Successfully synced marks for Quran (Term I)", and the mark is back to 18.

#### [2.6] Saving and Refresh
**Say:** If your admin has turned on AutoSave Academic Reports, your changes save by themselves, and the page shows **Saving**, then **Saved**. **Refresh** updates the student's name, address, principal, attendance totals and school details from Ad-Din.
**Show:** Zoom on "* Report will be automatically saved upon changes" and "Saved" under the toolbar. Click "Refresh" in THIS REPORT: toast "Report refreshed successfully. When all edits are complete, click “Save to PDF” to reflect in PDF."

### 3. Write the comment
#### [3.1] Edit
**Say:** Scroll down to **Comment**. To write it yourself, tap **Edit**, type your comment, and tap **Save**. A comment can be up to three hundred and fifty characters.
**Show:** Scroll to the comment box. Click "Edit"; the "Edit Comment" window opens. Type two sentences; the counter shows "112/350". Click "Save". Toast "Successfully saved the academic report".

#### [3.1a]
**Say:** The counter shows how many characters you've used. Past the limit, it says **— Maximum characters reached**, and **Save** can't be tapped. **Cancel** closes the window without changing the comment.
**Show:** Cutaway: the Edit Comment window with a long text, counter "362/350" in red and "— Maximum characters reached"; Save greyed. Click "Cancel".

#### [3.2] Write with AI
**Say:** Or tap **Write with AI**. **Ad-Din's AI Comment Generator** opens. It sends the student's results to an AI service, which drafts a comment.
**Show:** Open the next student, Hana Mirza. Click "Write with AI"; "Ad-Din's AI Comment Generator" opens with Student, Grade and Annual Average. The generated comment appears.

#### [3.3]
**Say:** Add your own guidance under **Custom Instructions (Optional)**, and tap **Regenerate** for a new draft. Always read it and change anything that isn't right.
**Show:** Type "mention improvement in Tajweed, keep it formal". Click "Regenerate". Spotlight the note under the comment about checking AI text.

#### [3.4]
**Say:** Tap **Insert** to add it after any existing comment, or **Replace** to swap it in.
**Show:** Hana has no comment yet, so Replace is greyed out. Click "Insert". The comment appears on the card.

#### [3.4a]
**Say:** If the existing comment and the new one together would be too long, **Insert** can't be tapped, and Ad-Din suggests **Replace**. If your admin added a second language, the translated comment appears beside yours once it's saved.
**Show:** Cutaway: AI window over a card with a long comment; spotlight the note "Total length with insertion would exceed 350 characters. Use 'Replace' instead." Then the Arabic take: the second comment box beside "Comment :".

### 4. Mark it ready
#### [4.1] Mark As Ready
**Say:** When a card is finished, tap **Mark As Ready**. The button changes to **Ready**, and a tick appears beside the student's name.
**Show:** In the toolbar under THIS REPORT, click "Mark As Ready". It turns to "Ready". Zoom on the tick beside "Hana Mirza" in the Students list.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** While a card is **Ready**, the **Refresh** button can't change it by accident. To make more changes, tap **Ready** again to unmark it.
**Show:** Spotlight "Refresh" in THIS REPORT, then "Ready"; don't click.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** Tap **Save to PDF** to update this student's PDF. Then use the arrows to move to the next student.
**Show:** Click "Save to PDF". Toast "Report saved and PDF updated successfully." Click the next-student arrow.

#### [4.3a] Download and bulk actions
**Say:** **Download** saves this student's PDF to your computer. Under **BULK**, **Save All to PDF** and **Download All** work on every card marked ready. Emailing parents is for admins, as video 12.3 shows.
**Show:** Spotlight "Download" in THIS REPORT, then "Save All to PDF" and "Download All" under BULK (a teacher sees no Email buttons). Don't click.

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Done
**Say:** Every card with a tick is ready to go home. You and your admin can see how many are done in the **Report Readiness** box on the dashboard.
**Show:** Students list with ticks beside each name. Quick cut to the dashboard's "Report Readiness" box.

#### [6.1a] What happens next
**Say:** Parents don't see anything yet. Your admin releases the cards, and parents see each one once it's ready and the release date has arrived. To change a card after that, tap **Ready** to unmark it, edit it, then mark it ready again.
**Show:** Spotlight "Ready" on Hana's card; the tick in the Students list.

#### [6.2] Close
**Say:** Next, video 12.3: Release report cards to parents. To set up a new report card, video 12.1: Map a report card template.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "12.3 Release report cards to parents" and "12.1 Map a report card template".
