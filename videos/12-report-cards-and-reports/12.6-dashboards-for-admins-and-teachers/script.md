# 12.6 Dashboards for admins and teachers

## Goal
Read the widgets on your dashboard as an admin or a teacher, pin Quick Actions, and choose which tiles you see.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Widgets, Quick Actions, Manage Dashboard Tiles
- The admin section of the app home: Waiting on you, with Review and Nudge
- Why check the dashboard each day; the greeting and the Madressah ID or Masjid ID
- Each widget's Select Term; Report Readiness per report; the Student and Staff switch when Staff Attendance is on; tapping a calendar date, Details and View All
- The Masjid Dashboard: Donations (Total Raised, Goal Remaining, Total Donors, Donation Campaigns, Donation Methods, Monthly Trend, Publish KPI), Facility & Operations Dashboard and Community Outreach & Social Impact (Edit KPIs, Add KPI)
- Manage Dashboard Tiles lists the tiles of the dashboard you're on
- The app's admin home: today's overview, quick actions, routine, Waiting on you details (Maktab and Hifz counts, oldest days, All caught up), Class Announcements

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Your dashboard is the first page you see on the web. In this video for admins and teachers, you'll learn what each box tells you, and how to make the page your own.
**Show:** Title card: "Dashboards for admins and teachers".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** You're signed in to Ad-Din on the web. Tap **Dashboard** in the sidebar at any time to return here.
**Show:** Sidebar → Dashboard. The dashboard loads.

#### [1.2a] Why check it
**Say:** A quick look each morning tells you what needs attention today: attendance not taken, report cards not finished, or events coming up. Each box links to the page where you act on it.
**Show:** The whole dashboard at a glance; slow zoom out.

### 2. The top of the page
#### [2.1] Greeting
**Say:** The top shows your home organisation and its ID. If you run both a masjid and a madrasah, switch between the **Madressah/School Dashboard** and the **Masjid Dashboard**.
**Show:** Spotlight the greeting and the ID badge. Click "Masjid Dashboard", then "Madressah/School Dashboard".

#### [2.1a]
**Say:** The greeting, **As-salamu Alaikum**, shows your first name. The ID beside your organisation, such as the **Madressah ID**, is your organisation's number in Ad-Din.
**Show:** Zoom on "As-salamu Alaikum Yusuf" and the "Madressah ID" badge.

#### [2.2] Your role
**Say:** On the right, the role menu sets whose view you see. If you're an admin and also a teacher, switch between **Admin** and **Teacher** here.
**Show:** Click the role menu (shows "Admin"); the list shows Admin and Teacher. Close it.

### 3. Madrasah widgets
#### [3.1] Total Counts
**Say:** The cards across the top count your students, boys and girls, staff, admins, classes and alumni.
**Show:** Spotlight the row of count cards.

#### [3.2] Hifz and activities
**Say:** **Quran Hifz Assignments** and **Academic Activities Insights** chart this term's work. Choose a term, or tap **Add** to set a new assignment.
**Show:** Spotlight both charts. Change "Select Term" to "Term 1 2026–27". Hover "Add".

#### [3.2a]
**Say:** Each chart has its own term list, so you can compare this term with the last one.
**Show:** Open "Select Term" on Academic Activities Insights; spotlight the terms; pick Term 1 again.

#### [3.3] Report Readiness
**Say:** **Report Readiness** shows how many report cards are complete for the term, so you can see who still has cards to finish.
**Show:** Spotlight "Report Readiness" and its count, for example "18/24".

#### [3.3a]
**Say:** Below the total, each report has its own count and percentage. Choose a term to see its reports. When every card is ready, it's time to release them, as video 12.3 shows.
**Show:** Spotlight "Term Report" with its count and percentage, then "Weekend Quran Report". Open "Select Term" and close it.

#### [3.4] Attendance
**Say:** **Attendance** shows how much of today's attendance is done. Further down, the **Event Heatmap** shows your busiest days for events.
**Show:** Scroll to "Attendance"; spotlight "Today's Attendance", "Completed Attendance" and "Pending Attendance". Scroll to the event heatmap.

#### [3.4a]
**Say:** If your madrasah uses Staff Attendance, the box also has **Student** and **Staff** switches, so you can check staff check-ins too.
**Show:** Cutaway from a take with Staff Attendance on: spotlight the "Student" and "Staff" switches in the Attendance box; tap "Staff", then "Student".

#### [3.5] Right column
**Say:** On the right, you'll see today's prayer times with Adhan and Iqamah, a calendar of **Events** and **Holidays**, and your latest messages and news.
**Show:** Pan down the right column: prayer times, the calendar with its Events and Holidays tabs, messages.

#### [3.5a]
**Say:** Tap a date on the calendar to see its events and holidays. **Details** opens an event, and **View All** opens the full list.
**Show:** Click a date with an event: "Family Quran Night" shows under Events. Spotlight "Details" and "View All".

### 4. The teacher's view
#### [4.1]
**Say:** Switch the role menu to **Teacher**. The same boxes now cover the classes you teach, and **Add** takes you to the Teacher Portal.
**Show:** Role menu → Teacher. The charts and Report Readiness reload with smaller numbers. Hover "Add".

### 4a. The Masjid Dashboard
#### [4a.1] Donations
**Say:** Switch the role menu back to **Admin**, then tap **Masjid Dashboard** at the top. **Donations** shows the **Total Raised**, the **Goal Remaining**, **Total Donors**, your campaigns, the methods people use, and a **Monthly Trend** you can view by year.
**Show:** Role menu back to Admin. Click "Masjid Dashboard". Spotlight the Donations box: Total Raised, Goal Remaining, Total Donors, Donation Campaigns, Donation Methods, Monthly Trend with its year list.

#### [4a.2] Publish KPI
**Say:** **Publish KPI** chooses who else sees this box on their dashboard: **Volunteer**, **Staff** or **Congregants**. Leave them unticked to keep it for admins.
**Show:** Click "Publish KPI": the window "Choose the roles that will have access to this KPI." opens. Spotlight Volunteer, Staff, Congregants. Close without changes.

#### [4a.3] Your own figures
**Say:** **Facility & Operations Dashboard** and **Community Outreach & Social Impact** hold figures you enter yourself. Tap **Edit KPIs**, then **Add KPI**, choose an icon, and type a **Card Label**, the **Data** and a **Description**. Tap **Save**.
**Show:** Spotlight the two KPI cards in Facility & Operations Dashboard. Click "Edit KPIs": the "Edit KPI" table. Click "Add KPI"; type "Families served" and "45". Spotlight "Save"; click "Cancel". Spotlight "No KPIs available." in Community Outreach & Social Impact. Click "Madressah/School Dashboard".

### 5. Quick Actions
#### [5.1] Pin a page
**Say:** **Quick Actions** are shortcuts to the pages you use most. Tap **Search module, Select to favorite** to add one.
**Show:** Scroll back up. Spotlight "No quick actions defined yet". Click "Search module, Select to favorite"; "Add Quick Actions" opens.

#### [5.2]
**Say:** Search for a page and tap **Pin**. Pin as many as you like, then tap **Done**.
**Show:** Type "Report" in "Search modules..."; click "Pin" beside Report Builder (it turns to "Pinned"). Type "Attendance"; pin it. Click "Done".

#### [5.3]
**Say:** Each shortcut is now a card on your dashboard; tap one to open that page. To remove one, open the list again and tap **Pinned**.
**Show:** The two cards appear under Quick Actions; click "Report Builder" to show it opens, then go back.

### 6. Manage Dashboard Tiles, and the app home
#### [6.1]
**Say:** To choose which boxes you see, tap **Manage Dashboard Tiles**. Each tile has a switch.
**Show:** Click "Manage Dashboard Tiles" on the right; the list of switches opens.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** Turn off what you don't need, and the box disappears straight away. Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you on any computer.
**Show:** Switch off "Event Heatmap"; the heatmap disappears. Switch it back on. Close the panel.

#### [6.2a]
**Say:** The list shows the tiles of the dashboard you're on. On the Masjid Dashboard, it has the masjid tiles, such as **Donations**.
**Show:** Cutaway: Manage Dashboard Tiles opened on the Masjid Dashboard; spotlight Donations, Facility & Operations Dashboard and Community Outreach & Social Impact.

#### [6.3] On the app
**Say:** In the Ad-Din app, admins get a home screen of their own. Tap **Admin** at the top, and **Waiting on you** lists what needs you today.
**Show:** Phone frame: the app home. Tap "Admin" in the role tabs. Scroll to the "Waiting on you" card.

#### [6.3a]
**Say:** Your card says it's today's overview. Below it are your pinned quick actions and today's routine. If Class Announcements is on, you can also post a new one from here.
**Show:** Spotlight "Administrator · today's overview" under Yusuf's name, the Quick actions card, the routine card, then "New" on the Class Announcements card.

#### [6.4]
**Say:** Beside **Enrollment applications**, tap **Review** to answer new applications. **Classes without attendance** names the classes not marked today. **Nudge** sends no message; it opens the attendance page, so you can take or check it.
**Show:** Spotlight the "Enrollment applications" row and its "Review", then the "Classes without attendance" row and its "Nudge". Tap "Nudge"; the attendance page opens. Go back.

#### [6.4a]
**Say:** The enrolment row also says how many applications are for Maktab and for Hifz, and how many days the oldest has waited. When nothing needs you, the card says **All caught up — nothing needs you right now.**
**Show:** Zoom on "1 Maktab · 1 Hifz · oldest 3 days". Cutaway from the second take: "All caught up — nothing needs you right now."

### 7. Next
#### [7.1] Done
**Say:** Your dashboard now shows what matters to you, with your favourite pages one tap away.
**Show:** The tidy dashboard with two Quick Actions.

#### [7.2] Close
**Say:** For the numbers behind these boxes, video 12.4: Madrasah reports library. To finish report cards, video 12.3: Release report cards to parents.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "12.4 Madrasah reports library" and "12.3 Release report cards to parents".
