# 12.5 Masjid reports

## Goal
Run, download and pin your masjid's staff list and prayer times chart.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Masjid Staff List, Masjid Prayer Times Chart

## Demo data
<!-- Records the demo organisation must have for this video (classes, students, events...). -->
- Signed-in demo account that is Masjid Admin of the demo masjid "Masjid Al-Noor"
- Staff on the masjid: "Sheikh Yusuf Rahman" (Staff), "Bilal Ahmed" (Staff), "Amina Khan" (Admin), "Omar Siddiqui" (Volunteer), with demo addresses, phone numbers and emergency contacts only (no real personal or medical data)
- Prayer times calculated for at least the next two months
- No pinned reports at the start

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Ad-Din has two ready-made reports for your masjid: a staff list and a prayer times chart. This video shows a masjid admin how to run and download them.
**Show:** Title card: "Masjid reports".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your prayer times are already set up, and your staff are added to the masjid.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

### 2. Open Masjid Report
#### [2.1]
**Say:** Go to **Reports**, then **Masjid Report**. Your home masjid is chosen under **Masjid Name:**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Reports → Masjid Report. Spotlight the masjid picker showing "Masjid Al-Noor".

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Under **Masjid Reports** you'll find the **Masjid Staff List** and the **Masjid Prayer Times Chart**.
**Show:** Spotlight the Masjid Reports card and its two rows.

### 3. Staff list
#### [3.1] Run it
**Say:** Tap **Run** beside **Masjid Staff List**. Choose a staff type, or leave **All**, and tap **View Report**.
**Show:** Click "Run". Open the staff type list: All, Staff, Admin, Volunteer. Keep "All". Click "View Report"; "Generating Report Please Wait." then the list.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Each person's contact details, address and emergency contact are listed. It's private information, so keep the report within your admin team.
**Show:** Scroll the table: Contact Details, Address Details, Contact Informations, Important Other Details, Emergency Contact. Demo data only.

#### [3.3]
**Say:** Tap **Download** to save it as a PDF.
**Show:** Click "Download"; the PDF downloads.

### 4. Prayer times chart
#### [4.1] Run it
**Say:** Back in **Masjid Report**, run the **Masjid Prayer Times Chart**. Pick a **From** date and a **To** date, and tap **View Report**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Reports → Masjid Report. Click "Run" beside "Masjid Prayer Times Chart". Pick the first of next month in From and the last day of the month after in To. Click "View Report".

#### [4.2]
**Say:** You get a table for each month, with the Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha times for every day.
**Show:** Scroll through the two month tables; spotlight the column headings.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** Tap **Download** for a PDF you can print and put up in the masjid.
**Show:** Click "Download"; the PDF downloads.

### 5. Pin and finish
#### [5.1] Pin
**Say:** Tap the pin beside a report to keep it under **My Favorite Reports** at the top. Pins are saved in this browser.
**Show:** Sidebar → Reports → Masjid Report. Click the pin beside "Masjid Prayer Times Chart"; it appears under "My Favorite Reports".

#### [5.2] Close
**Say:** That's your masjid's reports. Next, see what the dashboard shows you, or fine-tune your Iqamah and Jumu'ah times.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "12.6 Dashboards for admins and teachers" and "04.2 Set iqamah and jumu’ah times".
