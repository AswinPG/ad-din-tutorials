# 12.5 Masjid reports

## Goal
Run, download and pin your masjid's staff list and prayer times chart.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Masjid Staff List, Masjid Prayer Times Chart
- Why: a printed timetable for the notice board, a contact list for emergencies and events
- Who can open Masjid Report; choosing a masjid when you run more than one
- The staff types (All, Staff, Admin, Volunteer); the report header; the private details it includes
- The prayer chart's date range and month tables; running it again when times change
- Unpinning; pins per masjid in this browser

## Demo data
<!-- Records the demo organisation must have for this video (classes, students, events...). -->
- Signed-in demo account Yusuf Rahman, Masjid Admin of the demo masjid "Maple Grove Masjid"
- Staff on the masjid, from the demo world: "Yusuf Rahman" (Admin), "Bilal Hussain" (Staff), "Omar Khalid" (Staff), "Fatima Siddiqui" (Volunteer), with demo addresses, phone numbers and emergency contacts only (no real personal or medical data)
- Prayer times calculated for at least the next two months
- No pinned reports at the start
- Fatima Siddiqui's demo profile has a demo allergy note, so Important Other Details isn't empty (demo data only)

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Ad-Din has two ready-made reports for your masjid: a staff list and a prayer times chart. In this video for masjid admins, you'll run, download and pin them.
**Show:** Title card: "Masjid reports".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your prayer times are already set up, and your staff are added to the masjid.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

#### [1.2a] Why use them
**Say:** The prayer times chart gives you a printed timetable for the notice board or the newsletter. The staff list puts everyone's contact and emergency details in one place, which helps when you plan an event or something goes wrong.
**Show:** Cutaway: a printed-style page of the chart's first month, then the staff list table (demo data).

### 2. Open Masjid Report
#### [2.1]
**Say:** Go to **Reports**, then **Masjid Report**. Your home masjid is chosen under **Masjid Name:**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Reports → Masjid Report. Spotlight the masjid picker showing "Maple Grove Masjid".

#### [2.1a]
**Say:** Only masjid admins see these reports. If you run more than one masjid, pick the one you want in the list first.
**Show:** Open the "Masjid Name:" list: "Select an Organisation" and "Maple Grove Masjid". Keep Maple Grove Masjid.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Under **Masjid Reports** you'll find the **Masjid Staff List** and the **Masjid Prayer Times Chart**.
**Show:** Spotlight the Masjid Reports card and its two rows.

### 3. Staff list
#### [3.1] Run it
**Say:** Tap **Run** beside **Masjid Staff List**. Choose a staff type, or leave **All**, and tap **View Report**.
**Show:** Click "Run". Open the staff type list: All, Staff, Admin, Volunteer. Keep "All". Click "View Report"; "Generating Report Please Wait." then the list.

#### [3.1a] Staff types
**Say:** **Staff**, **Admin** and **Volunteer** are the roles you give people at your masjid, as video 03.3 shows. Pick one type to print, for example, just your volunteers.
**Show:** Open the staff type list again and pick "Volunteer"; click "View Report": only Fatima Siddiqui is listed. Switch back to "All" and view again.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Each person's contact details, address and emergency contact are listed. Tap **Download** to save it as a PDF. It's private information, so keep the report within your admin team.
**Show:** Scroll the table: Contact Details, Address Details, Contact Informations, Important Other Details, Emergency Contact. Demo data only. Click "Download"; the PDF downloads.

#### [3.2a] What's in it
**Say:** The top of the report shows the masjid, the staff type, who ran it, when, and how many people it lists. **Important Other Details** can include medical card numbers, allergies and special notes.
**Show:** Zoom on the report header (Maple Grove Masjid, the type, run by Yusuf Rahman, today's date, the count). Then zoom on the Important Other Details column of Fatima's row (demo note only).

### 4. Prayer times chart
#### [4.1] Run it
**Say:** Back in **Masjid Report**, run the **Masjid Prayer Times Chart**. Pick a **From** date and a **To** date, and tap **View Report**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Reports → Masjid Report. Click "Run" beside "Masjid Prayer Times Chart". Pick the first of next month in From and the last day of the month after in To. Click "View Report".

#### [4.2]
**Say:** You get a table for each month, with the Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha times for every day.
**Show:** Scroll through the two month tables; spotlight the column headings.

#### [4.2a]
**Say:** Choose whole months for a tidy printout, for example the first to the last day of a month. If your times change, run the chart again and print the new copy.
**Show:** Spotlight the From and To boxes with the first and last dates of the month; the header line "Generating Report Please Wait." while it loads again.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** Tap **Download** for a PDF you can print and put up in the masjid.
**Show:** Click "Download"; the PDF downloads.

### 5. Pin and finish
#### [5.1] Pin
**Say:** Tap the pin beside a report to keep it under **My Favorite Reports** at the top. Pins are saved in this browser, so they don't follow you to another computer.
**Show:** Sidebar → Reports → Masjid Report. Click the pin beside "Masjid Prayer Times Chart"; it appears under "My Favorite Reports".

#### [5.1a]
**Say:** Pins are kept for each masjid. To remove one, tap the pin icon beside it again, either in the list or under **My Favorite Reports**.
**Show:** Click the unpin icon beside "Masjid Prayer Times Chart" under My Favorite Reports; it disappears. Pin it again.

#### [5.2] Close
**Say:** Your masjid's reports are ready to print and share. Next, video 12.6: Dashboards for admins and teachers. Or video 04.2: Set Iqamah and Jumu'ah times.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "12.6 Dashboards for admins and teachers" and "04.2 Set iqamah and jumu’ah times" (the tile uses the video's own title).
