# 04.8 Gallery and downloadable forms

## Goal
Add photos and YouTube videos to your masjid's Gallery, and upload forms people can download.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Images and videos
- Forms up to 2 MB
- why use the gallery and forms (examples)
- organisation pickers, who sees Add, the empty gallery
- photo good practice (consent, captions); See how to do it for YouTube
- downloading a photo; delete confirmation; no message to followers
- clear file names; replacing a form with a new version

## Demo data
<!-- Records the demo organisation must have for this video (classes, students, events...). -->
- Demo masjid "Maple Grove Masjid", signed in as its masjid admin; Gallery with six existing photos (Eid prayer, open day, food drive)
- A new photo to upload titled "Iftar Night", caption "Community iftar in the main hall"
- A YouTube embed code for a demo masjid video titled "Masjid Tour"
- The demo account also follows a second masjid, so the pickers list more than one
- Forms list with two existing forms; a new PDF under 2 MB, "Membership Application.pdf", and a 3 MB PDF or JPG for the size message

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Share your masjid's moments, and the forms people need. In this video for masjid admins, you'll add photos and videos to the Gallery, and upload forms to download.
**Show:** Title card: "Gallery and downloadable forms".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your masjid is already set up. Everyone who follows it can see the gallery and forms, but only admins can add to them.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

#### [1.2a] Why use it
**Say:** Photos show your community what happens at the masjid and invite them to join. Forms save you printing and handing out paper: for example a membership application, a volunteer sign-up or a hall booking form.
**Show:** The Gallery grid with the six demo photos, then a quick cut to the Masjid Forms list.

### 2. Add a photo
#### [2.1] Where it lives
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Masjid**, then **Gallery**. Your masjid is picked at the top. Tap **Add**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid → Gallery. The photo grid; click Add.

#### [2.1a]
**Say:** If you follow several masjids, use **Select an Organisation** at the top to switch. **Add** shows only for the admins of the masjid that's picked. An empty gallery says **No Images Found.**
**Show:** Open the organisation dropdown and close it again with Maple Grove Masjid still picked. Spotlight Add.

#### [2.2] Title and caption
**Say:** In **Add Files**, type a **Title** and a short **Caption**. Leave the type on **Image**.
**Show:** Type "Iftar Night" and the demo caption. Image is selected.

#### [2.2a] Good practice
**Say:** Choose clear, bright photos, and ask people before you share pictures where they can be recognised, especially children. A short caption tells members what they are looking at.
**Show:** Zoom on the Title and Caption boxes with the demo text.

#### [2.3] Pick the photo
**Say:** Under **Add Images**, tap **Select File**, choose a photo, and crop it to fit. Then tap **Save**.
**Show:** Click Select File; pick the demo photo; crop; the preview shows. Click Save: toast "Successfully added an Item to Gallery". The photo joins the grid.

### 3. Add a video
#### [3.1]
**Say:** Videos come from YouTube. Tap **Add**, type a title, and choose **Video**.
**Show:** Click Add; type "Masjid Tour"; click Video.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** On YouTube, choose Share, then Embed, and copy the code. Paste it under **Add Videos** and tap **Save**.
**Show:** Paste the demo embed code into the box. Click Save; the tile shows a Video badge.

#### [3.2a]
**Say:** If you're not sure how to get the code, **See how to do it** opens YouTube's own help page. The video stays on YouTube, so it plays from there inside your gallery.
**Show:** Click Add again, choose Video, and spotlight "See how to do it" under Add Videos without clicking it. Click Cancel.

### 4. Manage the gallery
#### [4.1] View
**Say:** Tap any item to open it full size. Use the arrows to move through the gallery.
**Show:** Click "Iftar Night"; it opens large with its title and caption; click the right arrow once.

#### [4.1a] Download
**Say:** Anyone viewing a photo can tap the download icon to save it. Videos have no download button.
**Show:** Spotlight the download icon at the top of the open photo; move to the "Masjid Tour" video; the download icon is gone.

#### [4.2] Rename or delete
**Say:** Tap the pencil to change the title or caption, then tap **Submit**. The delete icon deletes the item, after you confirm.
**Show:** In the open viewer, click the pencil: "Rename Gallery" opens with Title and Caption; hover Submit, then click Cancel (the viewer closes with it). Open the item again and hover the bin; don't delete.

#### [4.2a]
**Say:** Ad-Din asks before deleting, because a deleted item can't be brought back. New photos and videos show straight away for your followers, without a message being sent.
**Show:** Click the delete icon: "Are you sure you want to delete "Iftar Night"? This action can't be undone."; click Cancel.

### 5. Upload a form
#### [5.1] Where it lives
**Say:** Now open **Forms** from the **Masjid** menu. Tap **Add New Form**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid → Forms. "Masjid Forms" with the existing forms; click Add New Form.

#### [5.1a]
**Say:** The picker at the top, **Select your Masjid**, chooses whose forms you see. **Add New Form** shows only for that masjid's admins.
**Show:** Spotlight the "Select your Masjid" picker with Maple Grove Masjid, then the open add panel.

#### [5.2] Describe it
**Say:** Under **File description**, say what the form is for, such as a membership application.
**Show:** Type "Membership application for new families".

#### [5.3] Pick the file
**Say:** Drag a file onto the box, or tap **Browse files**. Use a PDF, Word, Excel or JPG file, up to 2 MB.
**Show:** Click Browse files; pick "Membership Application.pdf"; its name and size show under the box.

#### [5.3a] Tip
**Say:** Members see the file's own name in the list, so give it a clear name before you upload it, such as Membership Application. A PDF opens on almost any phone or computer.
**Show:** Zoom on the file name "Membership Application.pdf" and its size under the box.

#### [5.4] Upload
**Say:** Tap **Upload & Save**. The form appears in the list with its date and size. A bigger file is turned away, with a message asking for one under 2 MB.
**Show:** Click Upload & Save; the panel closes. Cut past a page refresh: the new card is in the list. Then click Add New Form, type any description, pick the 3 MB demo file and click Upload & Save: toast "File too large to process. Upload a file of < 2MB in size." (the size is only checked on Upload & Save, not when the file is picked).

#### [5.5] Manage forms
**Say:** Members tap the download icon to save a form. As an admin, you can also rename it or delete it.
**Show:** Spotlight the download, pencil and bin icons on the new card. Click the pencil: "Rename Form"; click Cancel.

#### [5.5a] A new version
**Say:** Members aren't sent a message when you add a form. When a form changes, upload the new version and delete the old one, so nobody downloads the wrong copy.
**Show:** Spotlight the two cards: the existing form and the new Membership Application card with its date and size.

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Close
**Say:** Your gallery and forms are ready. Next, video 04.9: Introduction & Services pages. Or video 15.6: Tasbeeh, live streams, gallery and forms, to see what members see.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "04.9 Introduction & Services pages" and "15.6 Tasbeeh, live streams, gallery and forms".
