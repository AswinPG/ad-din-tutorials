# 04.8 Gallery and downloadable forms

## Goal
Add photos and YouTube videos to your masjid's Gallery, and upload forms people can download.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Albums, images and videos
- Forms up to 2 MB

## Demo data
<!-- Records the demo organisation must have for this video (classes, students, events...). -->
- Demo masjid "Masjid Al-Noor", signed in as its masjid admin; Gallery with six existing photos (Eid prayer, open day, food drive)
- A new photo to upload titled "Iftar Night", caption "Community iftar in the main hall"
- A YouTube embed code for a demo masjid video titled "Masjid Tour"
- Forms list with two existing forms; a new PDF under 2 MB, "Membership Application.pdf", and a 3 MB file for the size message

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Share your masjid's moments, and the forms people need. In this video, a masjid admin adds photos and videos to the Gallery, and uploads forms to download.
**Show:** Title card: "Gallery and downloadable forms".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your masjid is already set up. Everyone who follows it can see the gallery and forms, but only admins can add to them.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

### 2. Add a photo
#### [2.1] Where it lives
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Masjid**, then **Gallery**. Your masjid is picked at the top. Tap **Add**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid → Gallery. The photo grid; click Add.

#### [2.2] Title and caption
**Say:** In **Add Files**, type a **Title** and a short **Caption**. Leave the type on **Image**.
**Show:** Type "Iftar Night" and the demo caption. Image is selected.

#### [2.3] Pick the photo
**Say:** Under **Add Images**, tap **Select File**, choose a photo, and crop it to fit. Then tap **Save**.
**Show:** Click Select File; pick the demo photo; crop; the preview shows. Click Save: toast "Successfully added an Item to Gallery". The photo joins the grid.

### 3. Add a video
#### [3.1]
**Say:** Videos come from YouTube. Tap **Add**, type a title, and choose **Video**.
**Show:** Click Add; type "Masjid Tour"; click Video.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** On YouTube, copy the video's embed code, and paste it under **Add Videos**. Tap **Save**.
**Show:** Paste the demo embed code into the box. Click Save; the tile shows a Video badge.

### 4. Manage the gallery
#### [4.1] View
**Say:** Tap any item to open it full size. Use the arrows to move through the gallery.
**Show:** Click "Iftar Night"; it opens large with its title and caption; click the right arrow once.

#### [4.2] Rename or delete
**Say:** Tap the pencil to change the title or caption, then tap **Submit**. The bin deletes the item, after you confirm.
**Show:** Click the pencil: "Rename Gallery" opens; click Cancel. Hover the bin; don't delete.

### 5. Upload a form
#### [5.1] Where it lives
**Say:** Now open **Forms** from the **Masjid** menu. Tap **Add New Form**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid → Forms. "Masjid Forms" with the existing forms; click Add New Form.

#### [5.2] Describe it
**Say:** Under **File description**, say what the form is for, such as a membership application.
**Show:** Type "Membership application for new families".

#### [5.3] Pick the file
**Say:** Drag a file onto the box, or tap **Browse files**. Use a PDF, Word, Excel or image file, up to 2 MB.
**Show:** Click Browse files; pick "Membership Application.pdf"; its name and size show under the box.

#### [5.4] Upload
**Say:** Tap **Upload & Save**. The form appears in the list with its date and size. A bigger file is turned away, with a message asking for one under 2 MB.
**Show:** Click Upload & Save; the new card appears. Then show the toast for the 3 MB file: "File too large to process. Upload a file of < 2MB in size."

#### [5.5] Manage forms
**Say:** Anyone can tap download to save a form. As an admin, you can also rename it or delete it.
**Show:** Spotlight the download, pencil and bin icons on the new card. Click the pencil: "Rename Form"; click Cancel.

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Close
**Say:** Your gallery and forms are ready. Next, add pages for your masjid's services.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "04.9 Introduction & Services pages" and "15.6 Tasbeeh, live streams, gallery and forms".
