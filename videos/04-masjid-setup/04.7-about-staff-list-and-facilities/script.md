# 04.7 About, staff list and facilities

## Goal
Write your masjid's About page, check who appears on the Staff List, and set which facilities you offer with their quantity and capacity.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- About Masjid, Staff List, Facility Details (quantity and capacity)
- why it matters for new families and visitors
- Select an Organisation and who sees Edit; what to write; Cancel; no message to members, changing it later
- staff search, the organisation picker, a biography tip (00.5)
- facilities come from Ad-Din's list; what members see; website add-on; sub-facilities; keeping numbers current

## Demo data
<!-- Records the demo organisation must have for this video (classes, students, events...). -->
- Demo masjid "Maple Grove Masjid" with address, phone, email and logo, signed in as its masjid admin; no About description yet (so the empty state shows)
- About text to paste on camera: title "Welcome to Maple Grove Masjid", two short paragraphs and a bullet list of services
- Three staff on the masjid with titles: "Sheikh Idris Malik" (Imam, with a biography; not Yusuf Rahman, who is the demo admin account), "Bilal Ahmed" (Muezzin, with a biography), "Amina Khan" (Office Manager, no biography)
- The demo account also follows a second masjid (for example Cedar Hill Masjid), so the organisation pickers list more than one
- A facility with a sub-facility row (with Q: or C:) for [4.4a]
- Ad-Din app capture of the finished About Us page ([1.2a])
- Facilities from the catalogue, for example Parking (quantity), Prayer Hall (capacity), Wudu Area and Sisters' Prayer Area active, and one inactive facility to switch on

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Your community wants to know about your masjid: its story, its people and what it offers. In this video for masjid admins, you'll fill in these pages.
**Show:** Title card: "About, staff list and facilities".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your masjid's name, address and logo are already set up. Members see these pages in Ad-Din, on the web and in the app.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

#### [1.2a] Why it matters
**Say:** New families, visitors and people new to Islam often look here first. A clear story, the names of your Imam and staff, and a list of facilities help them feel welcome before they arrive.
**Show:** Phone-frame capture of the Ad-Din app showing the finished About Us page of Maple Grove Masjid.

### 2. About Masjid
#### [2.1] Where it lives
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Masjid**, then **About Masjid**. The **About Us** page shows your masjid's name, address, phone and email.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid → About Masjid. Spotlight the masjid card at the top.

#### [2.1a] Pick the masjid
**Say:** If you follow more than one masjid, choose one under **Select an Organisation**. Members can read the About page of any masjid they follow. Only that masjid's admins see the **Edit** button.
**Show:** Open the organisation dropdown: "Maple Grove Masjid" and the other masjids the demo account follows. Keep Maple Grove Masjid; spotlight Edit.

#### [2.2] Add a description
**Say:** Until you add one, the page says **No description yet**. Tap **Add description**, or **Edit** at the top.
**Show:** Spotlight the empty card; click Add description. The edit page opens.

#### [2.3] Write it
**Say:** Give it a **Title**, then tell your story in the editor: your history, services and timings. Use the toolbar for headings, lists and pictures.
**Show:** Type the demo title; paste the demo text; make the services a bullet list.

#### [2.3a] What to write
**Say:** Keep it short and useful: when the masjid opened, what services you run, when the office is open, and how to reach you. If you change your mind, **Cancel** leaves the page as it was.
**Show:** Scroll through the pasted demo text; spotlight Cancel beside Save without clicking it.

#### [2.4] Save
**Say:** Tap **Save**. Ad-Din confirms, and the page shows your new description.
**Show:** Click Save: alert "Successfully Updated."; click OK. The About Us page with the new text.

#### [2.4a]
**Say:** Members see the new text at once, but Ad-Din doesn't send them a message about it. To change it later, tap **Edit** on the **About Us** page, and save again.
**Show:** Spotlight the Edit button on the finished About Us page.

### 3. Staff List
#### [3.1] Who's on it
**Say:** Next, open **Staff List**. It shows everyone with the staff role at your masjid, with their title.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid → Staff List. "3 staff members"; the list with names and titles.

#### [3.1a] Search
**Say:** Members see the same list. In a long list, type in the search box to find a person by name. Use the organisation picker to see another masjid's staff.
**Show:** Type "Bil" in the "Search by name…" box: only Bilal Ahmed stays. Clear the search; spotlight the "Select Organisation" picker.

#### [3.2] Biography
**Say:** Tap a name to read their **Biography**. Each person writes their own biography in their Ad-Din profile.
**Show:** Click "Sheikh Idris Malik": the biography shows. Click "Amina Khan": "No Biography found".

#### [3.2a] Tip
**Say:** Ask each staff member to write a few lines about themselves, such as their role and studies. Video 00.5: Your profile, notifications and GUID shows where to add a biography.
**Show:** Zoom on Sheikh Idris Malik's biography.

#### [3.3] Adding staff
**Say:** You don't add people here. Staff and their titles are added under **Admin Portal**, then **User Management**, as shown in video 03.3.
**Show:** Small tile: "03.3 Add admins, staff and volunteers to a masjid".

### 4. Facility Details
#### [4.1] Where it lives
**Say:** Now open **Facility Details**. Here you show what your masjid offers, such as parking or a wudu area.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid → Facility Details. Spotlight the description under the picker.

#### [4.1a]
**Say:** The list of facilities comes from Ad-Din, so you only switch on the ones you have. Members see the active ones without the switches. If your masjid has an Ad-Din website, it can show them too.
**Show:** Scroll the facility list; spotlight one switch. Zoom on the description text ending "displayed on the Masjid’s website".

#### [4.2] Active and inactive
**Say:** **Active Facilities** are the ones members see. Switch one off, and it moves to **Inactive Facilities**. Switch it on again to bring it back. Changes save straight away.
**Show:** Turn off a facility's switch; open Inactive Facilities: it's there. Turn on the inactive demo facility; it moves up to Active Facilities.

#### [4.3] Quantity and capacity
**Say:** Some facilities have a quantity or a capacity. Tap the facility, enter the **Quantity** or **Capacity**, and tap **Save**.
**Show:** Click Parking: type 40 in Quantity; Save. Click Prayer Hall: type 300 in Capacity; Save.

#### [4.4]
**Say:** The cards now show the numbers, so visitors know there's parking for forty cars and room for three hundred worshippers.
**Show:** Zoom on the Parking card ("Quantity 40") and the Prayer Hall card ("Capacity 300").

#### [4.4a] Keep it current
**Say:** Some facilities have smaller parts listed under them, each with its own numbers. Update the numbers when something changes, for example when you add parking spaces or open a new hall.
**Show:** Spotlight a sub-facility row under a facility, with its "Q:" or "C:" value.

### 5. Next
#### [5.1] Close
**Say:** Your About page, Staff List and facilities are ready for your community. Next, video 04.8: Gallery and downloadable forms. Or video 03.3: Add admins, staff and volunteers to a masjid.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "04.8 Gallery and downloadable forms" and "03.3 Add admins, staff and volunteers to a masjid".
