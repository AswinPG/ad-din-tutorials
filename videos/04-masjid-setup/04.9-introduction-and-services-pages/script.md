# 04.9 Introduction & Services pages

## Goal
Create a service page for your masjid, save it as a draft, publish it, and know how to add it to your website menu.

- Introduction & Services for madrasahs (same page, under Madrasah / School)

### 1. Before you start
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Service pages tell your community what your masjid offers, such as Nikah, funeral services or classes. In this video for masjid admins, you'll create one, save a draft, and publish it.
**Show:** Title card: "Introduction & Services pages".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your masjid is already set up. Published pages appear in Ad-Din on the web and in the app, and you can add them to your website menu.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

### 2. Open Services
#### [2.1] Where it lives
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Masjid**, then **Introduction & Services**. The **Services** page lists your pages.
**Show:** Sidebar → Masjid → Introduction & Services. The Services page with two published pages.

#### [2.1a] Madrasahs too
**Say:** If you also run a madrasah, open **Madrasah / School**, then **Introduction & Services**. It's the same page, and every step in this video works the same way.
**Show:** Expand Sidebar → Madrasah / School; click Introduction & Services: the Services page with the breadcrumb "Madrasah". Then back to Sidebar → Masjid → Introduction & Services.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** The note at the top explains how services reach your website; that comes at the end of this video. To start a page, tap **Add New**.
**Show:** Spotlight the note "Showing services on your website", then click Add New. "Manage Service" opens.

### 3. Build the page
#### [3.1] Picture and icon
**Say:** Under **Appearance & Details**, tap the picture to add a cover image, and crop it. Then tap the pencil on the small icon to choose one that fits.
**Show:** Click the cover area ("Replace image"); pick and crop the demo photo. Click the pencil on the icon; pick an icon from the grid.

#### [3.2] Title
**Say:** Type a **Title**, such as Nikah Services, and a short **Sub Title**. The title is the only thing you must fill in.
**Show:** Type the demo title and sub title.

#### [3.3] Content
**Say:** Under **Content**, write the details in the editor: what you offer, how to book, and who to contact.
**Show:** Type or paste the demo content; make one line bold.

### 4. Save a draft
#### [4.1]
**Say:** If it isn't ready to share yet, tap **Save as Draft**. You're back on the list, and the page waits under **Drafts**.
**Show:** Click Save as Draft. The Services page now has a Drafts section with "Nikah Services".

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Use **All**, **Published** and **Drafts** to filter the list, or search by name.
**Show:** Click Drafts, then All. Type "Nikah" in the search box, then clear it.

### 5. Publish
#### [5.1]
**Say:** When it's ready, tap the three dots on the page, then **Edit**.
**Show:** Click the three-dot menu on "Nikah Services"; click Edit.

#### [5.2]
**Say:** Tap **Publish**. Ad-Din asks you to confirm, because the page goes live for your followers straight away. Tap **Confirm**.
**Show:** Click Publish: "Are you sure ?" with the publish message. Click Confirm.

#### [5.3]
**Say:** The page now shows under **Published**. Tap it to see it the way your members do.
**Show:** "Nikah Services" in the Published section; click it: the page view with picture, title and content.

### 6. Link to your website
#### [6.1]
**Say:** Published services appear in Ad-Din automatically, but your public website is separate. If your masjid has an Ad-Din website, tap **Manage Website Menu** and add the service there.
**Show:** Back on Services. Spotlight the note, then click Manage Website Menu; the website menu page opens.

#### [6.2]
**Say:** If your masjid doesn't have an Ad-Din website, the button reads **Get an Ad-Din-Powered Website** instead.
**Show:** Cut to the note for a masjid without the website add-on; spotlight the button.

### 7. Next
#### [7.1] Remove a page
**Say:** To remove a page, tap the three dots, then **Delete**, and confirm. It can't be recovered.
**Show:** Open the three-dot menu on a page; hover Delete; don't click.

#### [7.2] Close
**Say:** Your service page is live. Next, video 09.3: Build your website menu, if your masjid has an Ad-Din website. Or video 04.10: Live streaming.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "09.3 Build your website menu" and "04.10 Live streaming".
