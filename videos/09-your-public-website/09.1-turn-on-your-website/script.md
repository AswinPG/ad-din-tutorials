# 09.1 Turn on your website

## Goal
Activate your masjid's Ad-Din website, choose its web address, and, if you already own a domain, point it to your new site.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Activate website, website address, custom domain
- website home page for admins: site card, Quick actions, Browse website
- Why a website: it fills itself from Ad-Din; no web designer needed
- Site card details (theme colour, number of menu items); Connected organizations and the link cards (09.4)
- Choosing a good address and checking it; Save new address appears only after a change; choose it before you share it
- Why a subdomain: secure, your current website stays untouched, easy to undo
- Find your domain provider: three ways; check your domain after 24 to 48 hours; Not secure warning without SSL

### 1. Your masjid on the web
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Ad-Din can build a public website for your masjid from what you already keep in Ad-Din: prayer times, events, news and more. In this video, you switch it on and choose its web address.
**Show:** Title card: "Turn on your website".

#### [1.1a] Why a website
**Say:** You don't need a web designer. When you add an event, post news or change a prayer time in Ad-Din, the website shows it. Visitors don't need an account or the app.
**Show:** Split card: an event added in Ad-Din on the left, the same event appearing on the demo website on the right.

#### [1.2]
**Say:** The website is an add-on; video 02.5 shows how to add it. You can switch the website on and set it up first. Visitors see it once the add-on is active.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

### 2. Activate the website
#### [2.1] Masjid Website
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Website** and tap **Masjid Website**. For a madrasah, it's **Madrasah Website**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Website, expanded. Spotlight "Masjid Website" and "Madrasah Website"; click "Masjid Website".

#### [2.2]
**Say:** The welcome page explains what you get. Tap **Alright, activate website for my organization**. Only an admin of the masjid can do this.
**Show:** The welcome page with the showcase picture. Click "Alright, activate website for my organization"; a short spinner.
**Caption:** Tap "Alright, activate website for my organization". Only an admin can do this.

### 3. Your website at a glance
#### [3.1] Organization details
**Say:** Your website's home page opens. At the top is your new web address. Tap the copy button to share it.
**Show:** The "Organization details" page, with Home highlighted in the top bar. Spotlight the address in the site card; click the copy icon; toast "Address copied".

#### [3.2] Live
**Say:** A green **Live** tag means visitors can see the site. **Open website**, or **Browse website** at the top right, shows it in a new tab.
**Show:** Spotlight the "Live" chip and the "Open website" button, then "Browse website" at the top right.


#### [3.2a] The site card
**Say:** The card also shows your **Theme color** and how many menu items your site has. Below, **Connected organizations** shows your masjid. The card beside it links your madrasah, as video 09.4 shows.
**Show:** Spotlight "Theme color" and "5 menu items" on the site card. Scroll to "Connected organizations": the Maple Grove Masjid card and the "Link your Madrasah" card.
#### [3.3] No add-on yet
**Say:** Without the add-on, you'll see **Subscription needed**, and visitors get a "temporarily unavailable" page. Tap **Subscribe to preview** to add it.
**Show:** Cut to the second demo organisation: "Subscription needed" chip and "Subscribe to preview" at the top right. Quick cut to the visitor's "Temporarily Unavailable" page.
**Caption:** Without the add-on: Subscription needed. Tap Subscribe to preview to add it.

#### [3.4] The tabs
**Say:** Along the top are **Home**, **Menu**, **Settings** and **Statistics**, with shortcuts to add pages, news, events and prayer times.
**Show:** Spotlight the top bar tabs one by one.

#### [3.4a] Quick actions
**Say:** Further down, **Quick actions** take you straight to what you change most: the menu, the theme, prayer times and events.
**Show:** Scroll to "Quick actions"; spotlight "Edit website menu", "Customize theme", "Update prayer times" and "Post an event" in turn.

### 4. Choose your web address
#### [4.1] Website address
**Say:** Ad-Din picked an address from your masjid's name. To change it, tap **Settings** and go to **Website address**.
**Show:** Click "Settings". In the side list, click "Website address". The "Your website address" card.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Type a short, easy name. Use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only.
**Show:** Clear the box and type "maplegrove". Spotlight the rule "Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens (-) only".


#### [4.2a] A good address
**Say:** The address becomes part of your web link, so pick something short that people can remember and type, such as maplegrove. Check it before you save: no spaces, no capitals, and no other signs.
**Show:** Spotlight "maplegrove" in the box; open the "?" beside "Choose your address" to show its tip "This becomes part of your web link, so pick something short and memorable." Point out that "Save new address" appeared only once the box changed.
#### [4.3]
**Say:** Tap **Save new address**. If someone else already has it, try another. Then use **Visit site** or **Copy link** to share it.
**Show:** Click "Save new address"; spinner, success toast. Spotlight "Visit site" and "Copy link".


#### [4.3a]
**Say:** Choose your address before you print it on posters or share it, so everyone has the same link.
**Show:** Spotlight the saved address beside "Visit site" and "Copy link".
#### [4.4]
**Say:** Your Ad-Din address is secure from day one, with the padlock in the browser. There's nothing extra to set up.
**Show:** Open "Why does Ad-Din give you a subdomain?"; spotlight "Secure from day one." Close it.


#### [4.4a] Your current website
**Say:** If you already have a website, it stays as it is while you build your Ad-Din site. When you're ready, point your domain to it. To undo that, change it back at your domain provider.
**Show:** Open "Why does Ad-Din give you a subdomain?" again; spotlight "Your current website stays untouched." and "Easy to switch, easy to undo." Close it.
### 5. Use a domain you already own
#### [5.1] Custom domain
**Say:** If you already own a web address for your masjid, such as yourmasjid.org, open **Already own a domain? Use it instead**.
**Show:** Scroll down; click "Already own a domain? Use it instead". The three steps open.

#### [5.2] Step one
**Say:** First, log in where you bought the domain. Pick your provider for the exact steps. If you're not sure who your provider is, tap **How do I find my provider?**
**Show:** Click the provider tabs: GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, Squarespace, Other; the steps change. Click "How do I find my provider?", show the popup, close it.


#### [5.2a] Find your provider
**Say:** The help window gives three ways to find your provider: search your email for the receipt or a renewal reminder, look up your domain in the free ICANN Lookup tool, or check your bank statement for a yearly charge.
**Show:** In "Find your domain provider", spotlight "Search your email inbox", "Run a free domain lookup" and "Check your card or bank statement" in turn. Don't open the lookup link. Close the window.
#### [5.3] Step two
**Say:** Then set your domain to forward to your Ad-Din address. Tap **Copy** and paste it at your provider.
**Show:** Spotlight step 2 "Point it to this address" and the address box; click "Copy".

#### [5.4] Step three
**Say:** Last, switch on the free SSL setting at your provider. SSL gives your domain the padlock, so it opens securely. Changes can take a day or two to work.
**Show:** Spotlight step 3 "Turn on free SSL for your domain", then the notice "Domain changes take 24–48 hours to take effect".
**Caption:** Turn on SSL at your provider. Domain changes take 24 to 48 hours.


#### [5.4a] Check it
**Say:** After a day or two, open your own domain in a browser; it should show your Ad-Din site. If SSL is off, visitors may see a "Not secure" warning, so check that setting first.
**Show:** Spotlight the line "Look for a setting called SSL, HTTPS, or Secure certificate" and the notice "Domain changes take 24–48 hours to take effect".
### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Close
**Say:** Your website is switched on and has its address. Next, video 09.2: Brand your website. Or video 09.3: Build your website menu.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "09.2 Brand your website" and "09.3 Build your website menu".
