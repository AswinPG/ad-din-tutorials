# 09.5 Website analytics

## Goal
Read how your website performs in Google search, over any period, by search, page, country and device, and know when you'll see one combined report or two separate ones.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Visitor statistics
- combined or separate analytics
- Why look: which searches bring people, and what to add to your site
- Data range and Last update
- Custom Date Range: From, To, Apply, Clear Custom Range
- DATES and SEARCH APPEARANCE tabs; No data available
- Compare in detail: QUERY, PAGE, COUNTRY, DEVICE, Queries containing, the "vs" tiles, Clear comparison
- A worked example: a search you can answer on your site

## Demo data
<!-- Records the demo organisation must have for this video (classes, students, events...). -->
- Masjid Admin account, admin of "Maple Grove Masjid" and "Maple Grove Madrasah", both websites active and not linked (so the sidebar shows the two separate analytics items)
- Mocked Search Console data for `maplegrove`: 28 days with about 420 clicks, 9,800 impressions, CTR around 4.3%, average position around 11; top searches such as "maple grove masjid prayer times", "jummah near me", "maple grove iftar"; pages Home, Monthly Prayer Times, Events; countries and devices (mostly mobile)
- A second state of the sidebar with the websites linked, showing the single Website Analytics item
- Compare data for Queries containing "prayer" vs Queries containing "iftar"; a custom range of the last 14 days

### 1. How people find you
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Website analytics shows whether people find your masjid's website when they search on Google. In this video, you learn to read it.
**Show:** Title card: "Website analytics".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** These numbers come from Google Search: how often your site appears in results, and how often people click through to it.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need, with a small Google Search results illustration.


#### [1.2a] Why look
**Say:** The report tells you which searches bring people to your site, and which pages they open. Use it to check that what people look for, such as Jumu'ah times, is easy to find on your website.
**Show:** Card: a phone search "jummah near me" leading to the Maple Grove Masjid website's prayer times page.
### 2. Open your analytics
#### [2.1] Separate websites
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Website**. If your masjid and madrasah have separate websites, each has its own: **Masjid Website Analytics** and **Madrasah/School Website Analytics**.
**Show:** Sidebar → Website, expanded. Spotlight the two analytics items.

#### [2.2] Linked websites
**Say:** If you've linked them into one website, there's just one, combined: **Website Analytics**.
**Show:** Cut to the linked state of the sidebar: one "Website Analytics" item.

#### [2.3]
**Say:** We'll open the masjid's. You can also reach it from the **Statistics** tab on your website pages.
**Show:** Click "Masjid Website Analytics". The page "Performance for 'maplegrove…'" opens.


#### [2.3a]
**Say:** Under the buttons, **Data range** shows the dates in the report, and **Last update** shows when the numbers were last updated.
**Show:** Spotlight "Data range:" and "Last update:" with their values.
### 3. The four numbers
#### [3.1] Clicks and impressions
**Say:** **Total clicks** is how many times someone clicked through to your site. **Total impressions** is how many times your site showed up in results.
**Show:** Spotlight the two tiles in turn.

#### [3.2] CTR and position
**Say:** **Average CTR**, the click-through rate, is the share of people who clicked after seeing you. **Average position** is how high you usually appear; lower is better.
**Show:** Spotlight the other two tiles.
**Caption:** Average CTR: the share who clicked. Average position: how high you appear (lower is better).


#### [3.2a]
**Say:** Each tile has a small help note that explains the number, if you need a reminder.
**Show:** Open the help note on "Average position"; spotlight its first line; close it. Don't open its link.
#### [3.3] The chart
**Say:** Each ticked number is drawn on the chart, day by day. Untick one to hide it.
**Show:** All four tiles start ticked. Untick "Average CTR" and "Average position"; the chart keeps the clicks and impressions lines.

### 4. Choose the period
#### [4.1] Periods
**Say:** The report starts on the last **28 days**. Switch to **7 days**, **3 months** or **6 months**, or tap **More** for your own dates.
**Show:** Click "3 months"; the tiles and chart update. Click "More"; the "Custom Date Range" box opens; close it.


#### [4.1a] Your own dates
**Say:** In **Custom Date Range**, pick the **From** and **To** dates and tap **Apply**. The button now reads **Custom**. **Clear Custom Range** takes you back.
**Show:** Click "More"; choose From and To for the last 14 days; click "Apply": the button reads "Custom" and the tiles update. Open it again and click "Clear Custom Range".
### 5. Dig into the details
#### [5.1] What people search
**Say:** Below the chart, **QUERIES** lists the words people searched before finding you.
**Show:** Scroll to the table on the QUERIES tab; spotlight "maple grove masjid prayer times".

#### [5.2] Pages, countries, devices
**Say:** **PAGES** shows which pages they landed on. **COUNTRIES** and **DEVICES** show where they are, and whether they used a phone or a computer.
**Show:** Click PAGES, then COUNTRIES, then DEVICES; each table changes.


#### [5.2a] More tabs
**Say:** **DATES** lists the same numbers day by day, and **SEARCH APPEARANCE** shows how your site appeared in Google. If a tab says **No data available**, try a longer period.
**Show:** Click DATES; the day-by-day table. Click SEARCH APPEARANCE; its table, or the note "No data available for the selected period and filters." Click QUERIES again.
#### [5.3] Compare
**Say:** Tap **Compare** to put two searches, pages, countries or devices side by side.
**Show:** Click "Compare"; the compare box opens; click Cancel.


#### [5.3a] A comparison
**Say:** For example, choose **QUERY**, then **Queries containing** prayer, against **Queries containing** iftar. The tiles then show both numbers, with vs between them. Tap **Clear comparison** to go back.
**Show:** Click "Compare"; QUERY tab; type "prayer" in the first Keyword and "iftar" in the second; apply. The bar "Comparing: prayer vs iftar" and tiles "… vs …". Click "Clear comparison".

#### [5.3b] Use what you learn
**Say:** If many people search for something your site doesn't answer well, add it. For example, if they search for iftar, post your iftar times as news and add an event, so the next search finds them.
**Show:** QUERIES tab: spotlight "maple grove iftar". Card: "Add news · Add an event · Check the menu (09.3)".
### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Close
**Say:** A new website shows little until Google has found it, so look again every few weeks. Next, video 09.6: What visitors see. Or video 09.4: Link masjid and madrasah websites; enquiries.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "09.6 What visitors see" and "09.4 Link masjid and madrasah websites; enquiries".
