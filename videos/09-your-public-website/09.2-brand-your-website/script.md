# 09.2 Brand your website

## Goal
Give your Ad-Din website your colour, logo, banner slides, welcome text, social media links and contact emails.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- Your masjid's website switched on, and the Website add-on
- Your logo (PNG with a white or clear background) and wide photos for the banner (1920 × 600)

## Covers
- Banner, logo, colour theme, slides, social links, contact emails
- Why brand it: visitors recognise your masjid
- Custom colour Cancel; logo in the header and browser tab, Change image, the name shows when there's no logo
- Banner picture size; Title and Description counters; Learn More button, Service page and External link targets, Desktop and Mobile preview
- Edit and delete a banner (no question before delete); Cancel discards
- Slide every Faster and Slower; Organization name on banner is required; text limits (350 and 1800)
- Social icons at the top and bottom of every page; leave a box empty to hide its icon
- Edit or remove a contact email; Enter a valid email address

## Demo data
<!-- Records the demo organisation must have for this video (classes, students, events...). -->
- Masjid Admin account, admin of "Maple Grove Masjid", website active at the demo address `maplegrove` with the Website add-on
- Settings start plain: default colour, no logo, no banner slides, Organization name on banner "Maple Grove Masjid", no social links or contact emails
- Files ready: Maple Grove Masjid logo PNG; two banner photos 1920 × 600 ("prayer hall", "Ramadan iftar")
- Typed on camera: slide 1 title "Welcome to Maple Grove Masjid", description "Prayer, learning and community for every family."; action button to Internal page "All Masjid Events"; banner text "A home for prayer and learning in Maple Grove."; Facebook URL `https://facebook.com/maplegrovemasjid.example.com`, YouTube URL `https://youtube.com/@maplegrovemasjid.example.com`; contact emails `office@maplegrove.example.com` and `imam@maplegrove.example.com`
- Demo website open in a second tab for the closing shot

#### [1.1a] Why it matters
**Say:** When your website uses the same colour and logo as your posters and newsletters, visitors know straight away that it's really your masjid.
**Show:** Card: a Maple Grove Masjid poster beside the demo website header, both green with the same logo.
### 1. Make it look like your masjid
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Your Ad-Din website is switched on. In this video, you make it look like your masjid: colour, logo, banner and links.
**Show:** Title card: "Brand your website".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Website**, tap **Masjid Website**, then tap **Settings**. The sections are listed on the left.
**Show:** Sidebar → Website → Masjid Website → Settings tab. Spotlight the side list: Appearance, Logo, Banner, About, Social links, Contact emails, Website address.

### 2. Colour and logo
#### [2.1] Color theme
**Say:** Under **Appearance**, pick a **Color theme**. Menus, buttons and headings on your site use it.
**Show:** Click a green swatch; it shows a tick.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** For your exact brand colour, tap **Custom**, pick it, and tap the tick.
**Show:** Click "Custom"; the picker opens; choose a shade; click the tick.


#### [2.2a]
**Say:** If you change your mind, tap the cross instead, and the colour stays as it was.
**Show:** Click "Custom" again; spotlight the tick and the cross; click the cross. The picker closes.
#### [2.3] Logo
**Say:** Under **Logo**, tap **Select an image** or drag your logo in. A simple PNG with a white or clear background works best.
**Show:** Click "Select an image"; choose the logo file. It appears on the theme colour, as it will in the website header.


#### [2.3a]
**Say:** The logo shows in your website's header and in the browser tab, at a small size, so a simple mark works better than detailed artwork. To replace it, tap **Change image**. With no logo, the header shows your organisation's name.
**Show:** Spotlight the logo on the green preview, then "Change image". Don't change it.
### 3. Banner slides
#### [3.1] Add your first banner
**Say:** The **Banner** is the first thing visitors see. Tap **Add your first banner**.
**Show:** Scroll to Banner. Click "Add your first banner". The "Add banner" editor opens with a live preview on the left.


#### [3.1a]
**Say:** Banner pictures work best at 1920 by 600 pixels and under 1 MB, so they load quickly on phones.
**Show:** Spotlight "1920 × 600 px · under 1 MB" beside Carousel images, and "JPG or PNG · 1920 × 600 recommended" in the editor.
#### [3.2] Content
**Say:** Type a **Title** and a short **Description**. Then tap **Add image** and choose a wide photo.
**Show:** Type the demo title and description. Click "Add image"; choose the prayer hall photo; crop and upload. The preview updates.


#### [3.2a]
**Say:** Keep the title short. The counters show how many characters you've used: up to 35 for the title, and 300 for the description, so the words fit on a phone.
**Show:** Spotlight the "/ 35" and "/ 300" counters under the demo title and description.
#### [3.3] Action button
**Say:** To put a button on the slide, turn on **Action button** and choose where it links: a service page, an outside link, or a page on your site.
**Show:** Toggle "Action button" on. Click "Internal page"; choose "All Masjid Events" (the banner's internal-page list has no prayer-times page). Switch the preview to Mobile, then back to Desktop.


#### [3.3a] Where it links
**Say:** The button on the slide reads **Learn More**. For one of your own pages, choose **Service page**. For another website, choose **External link** and paste its address; it opens in a new tab.
**Show:** Spotlight "Learn More" on the preview. Click "Service page": "Select a service page" appears. Click "External link": the URL box appears. Click "Internal page" again with "All Masjid Events".
#### [3.4]
**Say:** Tap **Save changes** in the banner editor. Add more banners the same way, and drag them into the order you want.
**Show:** Click "Save changes". The banner strip shows slide 1. Click "Add banner", add the iftar photo, save. Drag slide 2 before slide 1.


#### [3.4a] Change or remove a banner
**Say:** To change a banner, tap its pencil. The delete icon removes a banner at once, with no question first, so take care. In the editor, **Cancel** closes it without saving.
**Show:** On the banner strip, spotlight the pencil and the delete icon on slide 1; don't click delete. Click the pencil: "Edit banner" opens; click "Cancel".
#### [3.5] Speed and text
**Say:** Set how many seconds each slide shows. Then check the **Organization name on banner**, and add a short **Banner text** of welcome.
**Show:** Click the plus beside "Slide every" twice. Spotlight the organisation name. Type the demo banner text; the counter shows its length out of 350.


#### [3.5a]
**Say:** The plus and minus buttons beside **Slide every** change the seconds. **Organization name on banner** is required. Banner text can be up to 350 characters.
**Show:** Spotlight the minus and plus buttons, then the organisation name box, then the "/ 350" counter.
### 4. About, social links and emails
#### [4.1] About
**Say:** Under **About your organization**, write a short introduction for your website's About area, up to 1800 characters. Say who you are, when you're open, and what you offer.
**Show:** Scroll to About; type two lines.

#### [4.2] Social links
**Say:** Under **Social links**, paste the full web address of your Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X or YouTube pages. They show as icons at the top and bottom of every page. Leave a box empty to hide its icon.
**Show:** Paste the demo Facebook and YouTube addresses. Leave the others empty.

#### [4.3] Contact emails
**Say:** Under **Contact emails**, add up to three addresses. They get every message sent through your website's contact form.
**Show:** Type "office@maplegrove.example.com", click "Add"; type "imam@maplegrove.example.com", click "Add". Two email chips appear.


#### [4.3a]
**Say:** Tap an email to correct it, then tap **Update**. The cross removes it. If an address isn't valid, Ad-Din says **Enter a valid email address.**
**Show:** Click the "imam@" chip: it moves into the box with "Update" and "Cancel"; click "Cancel". Spotlight the cross on a chip; don't click it.
### 5. Save and check
#### [5.1] Save changes
**Say:** Now tap **Save changes** at the bottom. This saves everything except the banner slides, which saved already.
**Show:** Click the floating "Save changes" button; "Saving…", then a success toast.

#### [5.2] See it live
**Say:** Open your website. There's your colour, your logo, your banner slides, and your social icons.
**Show:** Switch to the demo website tab and refresh. Spotlight the logo, the banner slide with its button, and the social icons in the header.


#### [5.2a] What happens next
**Say:** Visitors see your changes the next time they open or refresh the site. Nobody gets a notification. You can return to **Settings** and change any of this whenever you like.
**Show:** Back in Ad-Din: Settings tab, scroll from Appearance to Contact emails once.
### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Close
**Say:** Your website now looks like your masjid. Next, video 09.3: Build your website menu. Or video 09.6: What visitors see.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "09.3 Build your website menu" and "09.6 What visitors see".
