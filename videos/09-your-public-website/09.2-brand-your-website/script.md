# 09.2 Brand your website

## Goal
Give your Ad-Din website your colour, logo, banner slides, welcome text, social media links and contact emails.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->
- Your masjid's website switched on, and the Website add-on
- Your logo (PNG with a white or clear background) and wide photos for the banner (1920 × 600)

## Covers
- Banner, logo, colour theme, slides, social links, contact emails

## Demo data
<!-- Records the demo organisation must have for this video (classes, students, events...). -->
- Masjid Admin account, admin of "Masjid Al-Noor", website active at the demo address `alnoor` with the Website add-on
- Settings start plain: default colour, no logo, no banner slides, Organization name on banner "Masjid Al-Noor", no social links or contact emails
- Files ready: Masjid Al-Noor logo PNG; two banner photos 1920 × 600 ("prayer hall", "Ramadan iftar")
- Typed on camera: slide 1 title "Welcome to Masjid Al-Noor", description "Prayer, learning and community for every family."; action button to Internal page "All Masjid Events"; banner text "A home for prayer and learning in the heart of Riverside."; Facebook URL `https://facebook.com/masjidalnoor.example`, YouTube URL `https://youtube.com/@masjidalnoor.example`; contact emails `office@alnoor.example` and `imam@alnoor.example`
- Demo website open in a second tab for the closing shot

### 1. Make it look like your masjid
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Your Ad-Din website is switched on. Now let's make it look like your masjid: colour, logo, banner and links.
**Show:** Title card: "Brand your website".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** In the sidebar, open **Website**, tap **Masjid Website**, then tap **Settings**. The sections are listed on the left.
**Show:** Sidebar → Website → Masjid Website → Settings tab. Spotlight the side list: Appearance, Logo, Banner, About, Social links, Contact emails, Website address.

### 2. Colour and logo
#### [2.1] Color theme
**Say:** Under **Appearance**, pick a **Color theme**. Menus, buttons and headings on your site use it.
**Show:** Click a green swatch; it shows a tick.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Need your exact brand colour? Tap **Custom**, pick it, and tap the tick.
**Show:** Click "Custom"; the picker opens; choose a shade; click the tick.

#### [2.3] Logo
**Say:** Under **Logo**, tap **Select an image** or drag your logo in. A simple PNG with a white or clear background works best.
**Show:** Click "Select an image"; choose the logo file. It appears on the theme colour, as it will in the website header.

### 3. Banner slides
#### [3.1] Add your first banner
**Say:** The **Banner** is the first thing visitors see. Tap **Add your first banner**.
**Show:** Scroll to Banner. Click "Add your first banner". The "Add banner" editor opens with a live preview on the left.

#### [3.2] Content
**Say:** Type a **Title** and a short **Description**. Then tap **Add image** and choose a wide photo.
**Show:** Type the demo title and description. Click "Add image"; choose the prayer hall photo; crop and upload. The preview updates.

#### [3.3] Action button
**Say:** Want a button on the slide? Turn on **Action button** and choose where it links: a service page, an outside link, or a page on your site.
**Show:** Toggle "Action button" on. Click "Internal page"; choose "All Masjid Events" (the banner's internal-page list has no prayer-times page). Switch the preview to Mobile, then back to Desktop.

#### [3.4]
**Say:** Tap **Save changes** in the banner editor. Add more banners the same way, and drag them into the order you want.
**Show:** Click "Save changes". The banner strip shows slide 1. Click "Add banner", add the iftar photo, save. Drag slide 2 before slide 1.

#### [3.5] Speed and text
**Say:** Set how many seconds each slide shows. Then check the **Organization name on banner**, and add a short **Banner text** of welcome.
**Show:** Click the plus beside "Slide every" twice. Spotlight the organisation name. Type the demo banner text; the counter shows its length out of 350.

### 4. About, social links and emails
#### [4.1] About
**Say:** Under **About your organization**, write a short introduction for your website's About area.
**Show:** Scroll to About; type two lines.

#### [4.2] Social links
**Say:** Under **Social links**, paste the full web address of your Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X or YouTube pages. They show as icons on your site.
**Show:** Paste the demo Facebook and YouTube addresses. Leave the others empty.

#### [4.3] Contact emails
**Say:** Under **Contact emails**, add up to three addresses. They get every message sent through your website's contact form.
**Show:** Type "office@alnoor.example", click "Add"; type "imam@alnoor.example", click "Add". Two email chips appear.

### 5. Save and check
#### [5.1] Save changes
**Say:** Now tap **Save changes** at the bottom. This saves everything except the banner slides, which saved already.
**Show:** Click the floating "Save changes" button; "Saving…", then a success toast.

#### [5.2] See it live
**Say:** Open your website. There's your colour, your logo, your banner slides, and your social icons.
**Show:** Switch to the demo website tab and refresh. Spotlight the logo, the banner slide with its button, and the social icons in the header.

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Close
**Say:** Your website now looks like your masjid. Next, build the menu visitors use to find their way around.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "09.3 Build your website menu" and "09.6 What visitors see".
