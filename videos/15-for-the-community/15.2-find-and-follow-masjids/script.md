# 15.2 Find and follow masjids

## Goal
Find masjids and madrasahs near you on the map, follow the ones you visit, learn about them, and get directions to them in the Ad-Din app.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Masjid Finder map, follow, directions
- About Masjid, Staff List, Facility Details (member view)
- Why use it; location unavailable and the locate button; found count, live distance and direction arrow; No results nearby
- Calling, emailing and copying contacts (Copied); a madrasah's details; Claim Admin Role (points to 01.3)
- What following does (followers' messages in the Message inbox; Prayer Times, Live Streaming and masjid pages); home masjid unchanged (points to 00.4)
- Staff List search; Quantity and Capacity on facilities

## Demo data
- A community member account with no admin role (not the admin demo account Yusuf Rahman), signed in on the app, home masjid "Maple Grove Masjid", not yet following "Cedar Hill Masjid"
- Four or five demo masjids and one demo madrasah ("Riverbend Quran Academy") within a few km of the mocked phone location, with addresses, phone numbers and today's prayer times
- Phone location mocked to the demo city; Google Maps hand-off shown only as the system's app chooser (no real address on screen)
- Cedar Hill Masjid has an About text with a title, two staff members with photos, titles and a short biography ("Abdullah Hassan", title Imam; "Omar Siddiq", title Muazzin), and a few facilities switched on by its admin (whatever names the facility list has, for example parking)
- A second take with location off on the phone, for the toast and the "Location access needed" dialog in [2.1a]
- Riverbend Quran Academy has an address, phone and email; one Cedar Hill Masjid facility shows a Capacity
- One message from Cedar Hill Masjid to its followers in the member's Message inbox, for the [4.1a] cutaway

## Script

### 1. Find a masjid near you
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** In this video, you'll find masjids and madrasahs around you, at home, at work or when you visit a new area. You'll follow the ones you use and get directions.
**Show:** Title card: "Find and follow masjids". Phone frame on the app home. Side panel label: "Signed in as: a community member".

#### [1.1a] Why use it
**Say:** Use the finder when you move to a new area, when you travel, or when you look for a madrasah for your children. Following a masjid keeps its news and prayer times close at hand.
**Show:** Hold on the app home, with the "Search" tile in view.

#### [1.2] Open the finder
**Say:** On the home screen, tap **Search**. You can also open the menu, then **Masjid**, then **Masjid Finder**.
**Show:** Spotlight the "Search" tile and tap it. The finder opens: map with pins and a blue "You are here" dot.

### 2. The map and the list
#### [2.1]
**Say:** Ad-Din centres the map on where you are. Each pin is a masjid or a madrasah nearby.
**Show:** Zoom on the map pins around the blue dot. Tap the locate button once to recentre.

#### [2.1a] If your location is off
**Say:** If Ad-Din can't read your location, it says **Location unavailable, showing the default area.** Tap the locate button. Ad-Din explains how to allow location, or you can choose a place instead.
**Show:** Second take with location turned off on the phone: the toast "Location unavailable, showing the default area." Tap the locate button: the "Location access needed" dialog; spotlight "Allow location" and "Choose a place instead". Close it and return to the first take.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Swipe the panel up to see the list. Each card shows the name, the address and how far away it is.
**Show:** Drag the bottom sheet up. Spotlight the first card: name, address, "1.2 km away".

#### [2.2a] Distance and direction
**Say:** The top of the list shows how many places were found. The distance updates as you walk. If your phone has a compass, a small arrow beside the distance points towards each place.
**Show:** Zoom on the "found" count in the sheet header, then on the arrow and "km away" on the first card. Turn the phone a little: the arrow turns.

#### [2.3] Search and filter
**Say:** Type a name or an area in the search box. Use **All**, **Masjid** or **Madrasah** to show just one kind.
**Show:** Type "Cedar" in "Search masjid, madrasah or area…" and press search. Tap "Masjid", then "All".

#### [2.3a] No results
**Say:** If nothing matches, you see **No results nearby**. Try a different name or area, or tap **All** to show every kind of place again.
**Show:** Type "zzz" in the search box and press search: "No results nearby". Clear the search box and press search again.

#### [2.4]
**Say:** If you move the map somewhere else, tap **Search this area** to look there.
**Show:** Drag the map a little; tap the "Search this area" button that appears. The list refreshes.

### 3. See the details
#### [3.1] View details
**Say:** Tap **View details** on a card, or tap its pin on the map.
**Show:** On the Cedar Hill Masjid card, tap "View details". The details panel opens.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** You'll see the address, phone and email, today's prayer times, and links to the masjid's **Facility Details** and **Gallery**.
**Show:** Scroll the panel: address with copy button, phone, prayer times table, then the "Facility Details" and "Gallery" links.

#### [3.2a] Contact the masjid
**Say:** Tap the phone number to call the masjid, or the email to write to it. The copy button beside the address, phone or email copies it, and Ad-Din shows **Copied**.
**Show:** Spotlight the phone number and the email link without tapping. Tap the copy button beside the address: toast "Copied".

#### [3.2b] A madrasah and admin requests
**Say:** A madrasah's details show its address and contacts, and you can follow it too. If you run this organisation, **Claim Admin Role of the organizations** lets you ask to become its admin. Video 01.3 shows how.
**Show:** Close the panel; tap "View details" on "Riverbend Quran Academy": the Madrasah chip, address and Follow button, with no prayer times. Spotlight "Claim Admin Role of the organizations" without tapping. Close the panel and reopen Cedar Hill Masjid.

### 4. Follow and get to know the masjid
#### [4.1] Follow
**Say:** To keep up with this masjid, tap **Follow**. The button changes to **Following**.
**Show:** Tap "Follow": the heart fills and the button reads "Following". Zoom on it.

#### [4.1a] What following does
**Say:** When you follow a masjid, the messages it sends to its followers reach your **Message** inbox. The masjid is also added to the masjid lists on Prayer Times, Live Streaming and the masjid pages.
**Show:** Cutaway: the Message inbox with a Cedar Hill Masjid message, then the Prayer Times masjid name with its arrows.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** On your Prayer Times page, you can now switch to its times with one tap.
**Show:** Quick cut to Prayer Times: tap the arrow beside the masjid name to show Cedar Hill Masjid.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** To stop following a masjid, tap **Following** again.
**Show:** Back in the details panel, tap "Following": it returns to "Follow". Tap "Follow" again so the demo ends following.

#### [4.3a] Your home masjid
**Say:** Your home masjid stays as it is. To change your home masjid, or to see everything you follow in one list, watch video 00.4: Choose your home masjid and madrasah.
**Show:** Home screen with "Maple Grove Masjid" in the top bar.

#### [4.4] About, staff and facilities
**Say:** To get to know a masjid you follow, tap the **Masjid** tile on the home screen. Open **About Masjid**, **Staff List** or **Facility Details**, then choose the masjid at the top.
**Show:** Home → "Masjid" tile: spotlight About Masjid, Staff List, Facility Details. Tap "About Masjid"; open the masjid picker at the top and choose "Cedar Hill Masjid".

#### [4.5]
**Say:** **About Masjid** has its story and contact details. **Staff List** introduces its imam and staff; tap a name to read their **Biography**. **Facility Details** shows what the masjid offers.
**Show:** The About Us page for Cedar Hill Masjid: logo, address, phone, then the about text. Go back; tap "Staff List", pick Cedar Hill Masjid, tap "Abdullah Hassan": his Biography shows. Go back; tap "Facility Details", pick Cedar Hill Masjid: the "Active Facilities" cards.

#### [4.5a] Search staff and see facilities
**Say:** In **Staff List**, type in **Search by name…** to find someone quickly. In **Facility Details**, some facilities also show a **Quantity** or a **Capacity**, such as the number of parking spaces.
**Show:** On the Staff List for Cedar Hill Masjid, type "Omar" in "Search by name…": Omar Siddiq shows. Then Facility Details: zoom on a card with "Capacity".

### 5. Get directions
#### [5.1] Directions
**Say:** When you're ready to go, tap **Directions** on the masjid's card in the finder. Your maps app opens with the route.
**Show:** Back in the finder with the details panel closed. Tap "Directions" on the Cedar Hill Masjid card; the phone hands over to the maps app. Cut before any real address shows.

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Close
**Say:** Now you can find a masjid anywhere, follow it, get to know it, and get there. Next, video 15.1: Prayer times, monthly timetable and Qiblah. Or video 15.3: Events: going, interested and sharing.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "15.1 Prayer times, monthly timetable and Qiblah" and "15.3 Events: going, interested and sharing".
