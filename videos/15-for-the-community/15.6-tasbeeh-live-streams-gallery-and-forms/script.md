# 15.6 Tasbeeh, live streams, gallery and forms

## Goal
Count your dhikr with the Tasbeeh counter, watch or listen to your masjid's live streams, browse its photo gallery and download its forms in the Ad-Din app.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Tasbeeh counter, watching streams, gallery, downloading forms
- Manage Tasbeeh: add your own dhikr (name, type, target), edit or delete it
- Why use them; counting past the target; the six types; the Manage tasbeehs list and the delete question; a same-name dhikr replaces the built-in one
- Which streams show (Facebook Live Streaming, no card when none is set; admins in 04.10)
- Photo captions, other masjids' galleries, No Images Found; other masjids' forms, No forms yet (admins in 04.8)

## Demo data
- A community member account with no admin role (not the admin demo account Yusuf Rahman: an admin sees Edit and Delete on every stream card), on the app, home masjid "Maple Grove Masjid"
- No tasbeehs of the member's own at the start (only the three built-in chips, SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah and Allahu Akbar, each target 33, counts at 0); "Astaghfirullah", General, 100 is added on camera in [2.4]
- Maple Grove Masjid live streaming: an audio stream and a video stream link set (demo embeds that play a short clip); no Facebook stream
- The member also follows "Cedar Hill Masjid", so the Live Streaming picker in [3.3] lists more than one masjid
- Maple Grove Masjid gallery: eight photos (masjid building, Eid prayer, iftar) and one video, all demo images with no faces of real people
- Maple Grove Masjid forms: "Madrasah Enrolment Form" (PDF) and "Nikah Booking Form" (DOCX) with descriptions
- One Maple Grove Masjid photo has a caption; Cedar Hill Masjid has no stream links, no photos and no forms
- Phone frame for every shot

## Script

### 1. Four handy tools
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** The Ad-Din app has a few useful tools for everyday life at your masjid. In this video, you'll use the Tasbeeh counter, live streams, the photo gallery, and forms you can download.
**Show:** Title card: "Tasbeeh, live streams, gallery and forms". Phone frame on the app home. Side panel label: "Signed in as: a community member".

#### [1.1a] Why use them
**Say:** Count your dhikr after Salah without beads. Listen to a talk or the khutbah when you can't be at the masjid. See photos from masjid events, and get a form without a trip to the office.
**Show:** Quick cuts: the Tasbeeh counter, the Live Streaming page, the Gallery grid, the Masjid Forms page.

### 2. Tasbeeh counter
#### [2.1] Open Tasbeeh
**Say:** On the home screen, tap **Tasbeeh**. Pick a dhikr at the top, such as **SubhanAllah**.
**Show:** Tap the "Tasbeeh" tile. The counter opens; tap the "SubhanAllah" chip.

#### [2.2] Count
**Say:** Now tap anywhere on the big card to count. The bar fills as you get closer to the target.
**Show:** Tap the card several times: the number rises, "target 33" and the bar fill. Speed up to reach 33; the card changes to its done state.

#### [2.2a] Past the target
**Say:** Counting doesn't stop at the target, so you can keep going. The card simply shows that you've reached it.
**Show:** Tap the done card twice more: 34, 35. Zoom on the card in its done state.

#### [2.3] Reset
**Say:** Tap **Reset** to start again from zero.
**Show:** Tap "Reset": the count returns to 0.

#### [2.4] Your own dhikr
**Say:** To add your own dhikr, tap **Manage tasbeehs**, then **Add**. Give it a name, a type and a target, and tap **Save**.
**Show:** Tap "Manage tasbeehs". Tap "Add": the "Manage Tasbeeh" form shows Name, Type, Target with "Astaghfirullah", General, 100 filled in. Tap "Save". Go back; the new chip appears on the counter.
**Caption:** Add your own: Manage tasbeehs, Add, then a name, type and target, and Save.

#### [2.4a] Types
**Say:** The **Type** helps you sort your dhikr: **General**, **Durood**, **Kalima**, **Allah's Name**, **Verse / Surah** or **Other**. The **Target** is how many times you want to say it, such as 100.
**Show:** Cutaway in the Manage Tasbeeh form: open the Type list and spotlight the six types; spotlight Target "100".

#### [2.5]
**Say:** Your own dhikr keeps its count in your account, so it's still there next time. The three built-in ones count on this phone only. To change or remove your own, select it under **Manage tasbeehs** and tap **Edit** or **Delete**.
**Show:** Tap the "Astaghfirullah" chip, tap a few times; spotlight the count. Tap "Manage tasbeehs": tick the Astaghfirullah row; spotlight "Edit" and "Delete" without tapping. Go back.

#### [2.5a] The list
**Say:** The list shows each dhikr's **Type**, **Target**, **Count** and **Last Updated** date. Tick one row before you tap **Edit**. **Delete** asks **Are You Sure you want to delete this Tasbeeh?** first.
**Show:** Cutaway on Manage tasbeehs: spotlight the columns. Tick Astaghfirullah, tap "Delete": the question shows with "NO" and "YES"; tap "NO". Go back to the counter.

#### [2.5b] A tip
**Say:** If you add your own dhikr with the same name as a built-in one, such as SubhanAllah, yours takes its place, and its count is kept in your account.
**Show:** Card: "Your own SubhanAllah → replaces the built-in chip → count saved in your account".

### 3. Live streams
#### [3.1] Streaming
**Say:** When your masjid streams a talk or the Jumu'ah khutbah, tap **Streaming** on the home screen.
**Show:** Back on home, tap the "Streaming" tile. The "Live Streaming" page opens with Maple Grove Masjid selected.

#### [3.2]
**Say:** Tap **Audio Live Streaming** to listen, or **Video Live Streaming** to watch.
**Show:** Tap the "Audio Live Streaming" card: it opens with the audio player. Collapse it; tap "Video Live Streaming" and press play on the video.

#### [3.2a] Which streams show
**Say:** You see only the streams your masjid has set. Some also stream on Facebook, under **Facebook Live Streaming**. If no card shows, your masjid hasn't added a stream link yet. Its admins set these in video 04.10.
**Show:** Spotlight the two cards. Cutaway on Cedar Hill Masjid with no stream links: the page with no cards.

#### [3.3]
**Say:** If you follow more than one masjid, choose another one from the list at the top.
**Show:** Open the masjid picker at the top; show the followed masjids, then keep Maple Grove Masjid.

### 4. Photo gallery
#### [4.1] Gallery
**Say:** To see photos from your masjid, tap the **Masjid** tile, then **Gallery**.
**Show:** Home → "Masjid" tile → "Gallery". The photo grid for Maple Grove Masjid appears.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** Tap a photo to see it full size. Swipe left and right to move through them.
**Show:** Tap the Eid prayer photo: the full-screen viewer opens with "3 / 9". Swipe to the next photo, then tap the video tile's play badge.

#### [4.2a] Captions and other masjids
**Say:** A photo can have a caption, shown under it. Pick another masjid you follow at the top to see its photos. If a masjid hasn't added any, you see **No Images Found.**
**Show:** Zoom on a caption in the viewer. Close it. Open the masjid picker; choose Cedar Hill Masjid: "No Images Found." Choose Maple Grove Masjid again.

#### [4.3]
**Say:** To keep a photo, tap the download button.
**Show:** On a photo, tap the download icon at the top. Close the viewer.

### 5. Forms
#### [5.1] Forms
**Say:** Your masjid can share forms too, like an enrolment or a booking form. Tap the **Masjid** tile, then **Forms**.
**Show:** Home → "Masjid" tile → "Forms". The "Masjid Forms" page opens with Maple Grove Masjid selected.

#### [5.2]
**Say:** Each card shows the form's name, a short description, the date and the size.
**Show:** Spotlight the "Madrasah Enrolment Form" card: PDF badge, name, description, date and size.

#### [5.2a] Other masjids
**Say:** Use the list at the top to see the forms of another masjid you follow. If a masjid has none, the page says **No forms yet**. Your masjid's admins add photos and forms in video 04.8.
**Show:** Open the masjid list on Masjid Forms; choose Cedar Hill Masjid: "No forms yet". Choose Maple Grove Masjid again.

#### [5.3]
**Say:** Tap the download button to open the form. Fill it in, then hand it to the masjid as it asks.
**Show:** Tap the download icon on "Madrasah Enrolment Form"; the PDF opens.

### 6. Next
#### [6.1] Close
**Say:** That's your Tasbeeh, live streams, gallery and forms. Next, video 15.7: Become a volunteer. Or video 15.5: Read and listen to the Quran.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "15.7 Become a volunteer" and "15.5 Read and listen to the Quran".
