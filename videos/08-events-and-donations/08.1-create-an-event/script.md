# 08.1 Create an event

## Goal
Create a public or private event for your masjid, with its category, date and time, venue, ticket link, invitees and pictures, and publish it to your community.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Public or private, invitees, categories, venue, Ticket URL, images for web and app
- Why events: one event in the app, on the website and on the TV; Going and Interested
- The Events page: search, Filters, date chips; Add New Event only for admins
- Saving to bar; the organisation can't be changed after saving
- Required title and category; all seventeen categories; Tickets Available on the event page; Clear buttons
- Dates can't be in the past; After specific salah: After salah, Start delay, End after
- Private events need at least one invitee group
- Who is told about a public event; Push Notification also puts a message in the inbox; people's own preferences
- Pictures are optional (wide and tall crops); the event page with going, interested and share counts; Edit sends an update

- Masjid Admin account with home masjid "Maple Grove Masjid" (Premium), the masjid's address saved; the account is also admin of "Maple Grove Madrasah"
- Event list already showing the demo world's two upcoming Maple Grove Masjid events with posters ("Family Quran Night", "Youth Open House")
- New event typed on camera: "Family Picnic", category Religion changed to Food, description "Bring a dish to share. Games for the children.", Ticket URL `https://tickets.example.com/family-picnic`, next Saturday 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM, venue "Riverside Park" found with the location search
- Two picture files ready: a 3:2 picnic poster for Image for Mobile/Web and a tall 960 × 1080 poster for Image for Prayer Dashboard

- After saving, the "Family Picnic" event page with demo counts (for example 12 going, 8 interested, 3 shares)

#### [1.1a] Why events
**Say:** One event reaches people in several places: the Events page in the Ad-Din app, your website if you have one, and your masjid's TV screen. People mark **Going** or **Interested**, so you can see how many to expect.
**Show:** Card with three small pictures: the app's event card, the website's events section, a TV screen with an event poster. Then spotlight the "Going" and "Interested" buttons on the "Family Quran Night" card.
### 1. Share what's happening
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Lectures, iftars, picnics and classes: events tell your community what's coming up. In this video, you create an event and publish it.
**Show:** Title card: "Create an event".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your masjid is already set up with its address. To add events, you need Ad-Din Premium on your own account.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

### 2. Start a new event
#### [2.1] Events
**Say:** In the sidebar, tap **Events**. You'll see the events coming up, with a calendar on the side.
**Show:** Sidebar → Events. The Events page with the two demo event cards and the month calendar on the right.


#### [2.1a]
**Say:** Above the list are a search box, **Filters**, and chips such as **Today** and **Week**. The **Add New Event** button shows only for admins.
**Show:** Spotlight "Search events", the "Filters" button, then the "All", "Today" and "Week" chips. Spotlight "Add New Event" at the top right.
#### [2.2]
**Say:** Tap **Add New Event**. At the top, **Select an Organisation** already shows your home masjid. Change it if the event belongs to your madrasah.
**Show:** Click "Add New Event". The form opens; spotlight the organisation picker showing "Maple Grove Masjid-(masjid)".


#### [2.2a] Saving to
**Say:** The bar at the bottom shows where the event is saving to. Check it now: after you save, you can't move the event to another organisation.
**Show:** Spotlight "Saving to Maple Grove Masjid" in the bottom bar.
### 3. Event details
#### [3.1] Title and type
**Say:** Type the **Event Title**. Then choose the **Event Type**. A **Public** event is open to everyone who follows your masjid.
**Show:** Type "Family Picnic" in Event Title. Spotlight the Public / Private switch with Public selected. The preview on the right shows the title.


#### [3.1a] Required fields
**Say:** The title and the category are required. If one is empty, a red message shows under it, such as **Title is required**.
**Show:** Clear the Event Title box and click outside it: "Title is required" shows in red. Type "Family Picnic" again.
#### [3.2] Category
**Say:** Open **Select a Category**. It starts on **Religion**, and there are seventeen to choose from, such as **Education**, **Food**, **Sport** and **Fundraising**. People can filter events by category.
**Show:** Open the category list (it starts on Religion); scroll the options, choose Food. The preview shows a "Food" tag.

#### [3.3] Description and ticket
**Say:** Add an **Event Description**. If people book through another website, paste that link in **Ticket URL**.
**Show:** Type the demo description, then paste the demo link into Ticket URL.


#### [3.3a] Tickets
**Say:** When you add a ticket link, the event's page shows it with **Tickets Available**. Leave it empty for an event that needs no booking.
**Show:** Quick cut: the "Youth Open House" event page with its "Tickets" row and "Tickets Available". Back to the form.

#### [3.3b] Clear
**Say:** Each card has a **Clear** button that empties it. Clear under **Event details** also unticks the notification channels and the TV switch, so check them again before you publish.
**Show:** Spotlight the "Clear" button on the Event details card and on the Event Duration card. Don't click them.
### 4. Date and time
#### [4.1] Starts and ends
**Say:** Under **Event Duration**, choose when it **Starts** and **Ends**: the date and the time.
**Show:** Click Start Date; pick next Saturday, 11:00 AM. Click End Date; pick the same day, 3:00 PM. The preview shows the date badge.


#### [4.1a] Dates that work
**Say:** An event can't start in the past, and it must end after it starts. If the dates don't fit, Ad-Din won't save the event and shows a message.
**Show:** Spotlight the Starts and Ends boxes with the chosen dates.
#### [4.2]
**Say:** For an event that runs all day, turn on **All Day** and pick dates only.
**Show:** Spotlight the All Day switch; toggle it on, show the date-only picker, then toggle it off and re-pick the times.

#### [4.3] After a salah
**Say:** For a masjid, **After specific salah** starts the event a set number of minutes after a prayer begins, such as a talk after Isha.
**Show:** Spotlight the "After specific salah" switch and its note "Start relative to a prayer instead of a clock time". Leave Repeat on Never.


#### [4.3a] Prayer and minutes
**Say:** When you turn it on, choose the prayer under **After salah**, and the date. Type the **Start delay** and **End after**, in minutes. Ad-Din works out the times from that day's prayer times.
**Show:** Toggle "After specific salah" on: the All Day switch hides and the date pickers show dates only. Choose Isha in After salah; type 10 in Start delay and 60 in End after. Then toggle it off again and re-pick 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
**Caption:** Choose the prayer and the date, then the Start delay and End after in minutes.
### 5. Venue
#### [5.1] Same address
**Say:** Under **Venue Location**, **Same As Organisation** is on, so the event uses your masjid's address.
**Show:** Spotlight the Same As Organisation switch and the address box showing the masjid's address.

#### [5.2] Somewhere else
**Say:** If the event is somewhere else, turn the switch off, search for the place, and pick it from the list.
**Show:** Toggle Same As Organisation off. Type "Riverside Park" in the location search; pick the suggestion. The address box fills in.

### 6. Private events and invitees
#### [6.1] Private
**Say:** If only some people should see it, choose **Private**. A new card appears: **Select Invitees For This Event**.
**Show:** Click Private. The preview shows a lock and "Private". Scroll to the new invitees card.

#### [6.2] Who's invited
**Say:** For a masjid, tick Volunteers, Staff or Followers. For a madrasah event, tick Staff or Followers, or the Parents or Students of one class and section.
**Show:** Tick "Volunteers" and "Staff". Quick cut: the same card for Maple Grove Madrasah with Parents and Students and their class and section lists. Back on the masjid event, click Public again; the invitees card disappears.
**Caption:** Masjid: Volunteers, Staff or Followers. Madrasah: Staff, Followers, or Parents or Students of a class and section.


#### [6.2a]
**Say:** A private event needs at least one group, or Ad-Din won't save it. Only the groups you tick are invited and told about the event.
**Show:** Spotlight the ticked "Volunteers" and "Staff" boxes (from the same take, before clicking Public again).
### 7. Notifications and pictures
#### [7.1] Notification Channel
**Say:** Under **Notification Channel**, **Email** and **Push Notification** are both on, so followers get an email and a phone notification about the event. Untick one to skip it.
**Show:** Spotlight the Notification Channel chips, both ticked. Leave the TV switch above them as it is.


#### [7.1a] Who is told
**Say:** For a public masjid event, Ad-Din tells your followers, staff and volunteers. For a public madrasah event, it tells the parents, students, staff and followers.
**Show:** Card: "Public masjid event: Followers · Staff · Volunteers" and "Public madrasah event: Parents · Students · Staff · Followers".
**Caption:** Public masjid event: followers, staff and volunteers. Public madrasah event: parents, students, staff and followers.

#### [7.1b]
**Say:** **Push Notification** also puts a message in each person's Ad-Din inbox. If someone turned email or push off in their own preferences, they don't get that one, but the inbox message still arrives.
**Show:** Quick cut: a member's inbox with the message about "Family Picnic". Back to the Notification Channel chips.
#### [7.2] Pictures
**Say:** Add a poster under **Image for Mobile/Web**. It shows on the event card in the app and on the website.
**Show:** Click the "Image for Mobile/Web" tile; choose the picnic poster, crop it, upload. The tile and the preview show the picture.

#### [7.3]
**Say:** **Image for Prayer Dashboard** is a tall version for your masjid's prayer-times TV screen.
**Show:** Click the "Image for Prayer Dashboard" tile; upload the tall poster. Spotlight "How it will appear" with the finished card.


#### [7.3a] Picture tips
**Say:** Both pictures are optional; without one, the card shows a plain picture. The first is cropped wide, three by two. The TV one is cropped tall, so keep the important words in the middle.
**Show:** Reopen the "Image for Mobile/Web" cropper for a moment to show the wide crop frame; cancel. Spotlight the tall poster in the second tile.
### 8. Publish
#### [8.1] Save & Publish
**Say:** Check the preview, then tap **Save & Publish**. If something required is missing, Ad-Din points it out.
**Show:** Click "Save & Publish". Toast "Event Added Successfully". The Events page opens.

#### [8.2] Done
**Say:** Your event is now in the list, ready for your community to mark Going or Interested.
**Show:** Spotlight the new "Family Picnic" card with its poster and date badge.


#### [8.2a] The event page
**Say:** Tap the card to open the event. You see how many people are going and interested, and how many shared it. As an admin, you also see **Edit** and **Delete**.
**Show:** Click the "Family Picnic" card. The event page: spotlight the going, interested and share counts, then the "Edit" and "Delete" buttons.

#### [8.2b] Change it later
**Say:** To change the event, tap **Edit**, make your change, and tap **Save & Publish**. The same people get an update, by the channels you ticked. Video 08.2 shows how to delete one date or a whole series.
**Show:** Click "Edit"; the Manage Event page opens with "Select an Organisation" greyed. Spotlight it, then click "Cancel".
#### [8.3] Next
**Say:** Next, video 08.2: Repeating events and events on the masjid TV. Or video 15.3: Events: going, interested and sharing, to see how members find events.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "08.2 Repeating events and events on the masjid TV" and "15.3 Events: going, interested and sharing".
