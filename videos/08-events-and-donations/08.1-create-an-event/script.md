# 08.1 Create an event

## Goal
Create a public or private event for your masjid, with its category, date and time, venue, ticket link, invitees and pictures, and publish it to your community.

## You'll need
<!-- Shown on the opening card. Generated from prereq/plan/switch if left empty. -->

## Covers
- Public or private, invitees, categories, venue, Ticket URL, images for web and app

## Demo data
<!-- Records the demo organisation must have for this video (classes, students, events...). -->
- Masjid Admin account with home masjid "Masjid Al-Noor" (Premium), the masjid's address saved; the account is also admin of "Al-Noor Madrasah"
- Event list already showing two upcoming Masjid Al-Noor events with posters ("Tafsir Circle", "Youth Football")
- New event typed on camera: "Family Picnic", category Religion changed to Food, description "Bring a dish to share. Games for the children.", Ticket URL `https://tickets.example.org/family-picnic`, next Saturday 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM, venue "Riverside Park" found with the location search
- Two picture files ready: a 3:2 picnic poster for Image for Mobile/Web and a tall 960 × 1080 poster for Image for Prayer Dashboard
- Al-Noor Madrasah has classes and sections so the Parents and Students invitee lists can be shown

### 1. Share what's happening
#### [1.1] Opening
**Say:** Lectures, iftars, picnics and classes: events tell your community what's coming up. In this video, a masjid admin creates an event and publishes it.
**Show:** Title card: "Create an event".

#### [1.2]
**Say:** Your masjid is already set up with its address, and events come with Ad-Din Premium.
**Show:** Opening card from You'll need.

### 2. Start a new event
#### [2.1] Events
**Say:** In the sidebar, tap **Events**. You'll see the events coming up, with a calendar on the side.
**Show:** Sidebar → Events. The Events page with the two demo event cards and the month calendar on the right.

#### [2.2]
**Say:** Tap **Add New Event**. At the top, **Select an Organisation** already shows your home masjid. Change it if the event belongs to your madrasah.
**Show:** Click "Add New Event". The form opens; spotlight the organisation picker showing "Masjid Al-Noor-(masjid)".

### 3. Event details
#### [3.1] Title and type
**Say:** Type the **Event Title**. Then choose the **Event Type**. A **Public** event is open to everyone who follows your masjid.
**Show:** Type "Family Picnic" in Event Title. Spotlight the Public / Private switch with Public selected. The preview on the right shows the title.

#### [3.2] Category
**Say:** Pick a category, such as Religion, Education, Food or Fundraising. People can filter events by category.
**Show:** Open the category list (it starts on Religion); scroll the options, choose Food. The preview shows a "Food" tag.

#### [3.3] Description and ticket
**Say:** Add an **Event Description**. If people book through another website, paste that link in **Ticket URL**.
**Show:** Type the demo description, then paste the demo link into Ticket URL.

### 4. Date and time
#### [4.1] Starts and ends
**Say:** Under **Event Duration**, choose when it **Starts** and **Ends**: the date and the time.
**Show:** Click Start Date; pick next Saturday, 11:00 AM. Click End Date; pick the same day, 3:00 PM. The preview shows the date badge.

#### [4.2]
**Say:** For an event that runs all day, turn on **All Day** and pick dates only.
**Show:** Spotlight the All Day switch; toggle it on, show the date-only picker, then toggle it off and re-pick the times.

#### [4.3] After a salah
**Say:** For a masjid, **After specific salah** starts the event a set number of minutes after a prayer, such as a talk after Isha.
**Show:** Spotlight the "After specific salah" switch and its note. Leave it off. Leave Repeat on Never.

### 5. Venue
#### [5.1] Same address
**Say:** Under **Venue Location**, **Same As Organisation** is on, so the event uses your masjid's address.
**Show:** Spotlight the Same As Organisation switch and the address box showing the masjid's address.

#### [5.2] Somewhere else
**Say:** Holding it somewhere else? Turn the switch off, search for the place, and pick it from the list.
**Show:** Toggle Same As Organisation off. Type "Riverside Park" in the location search; pick the suggestion. The address box fills in.

### 6. Private events and invitees
#### [6.1] Private
**Say:** If only some people should see it, choose **Private**. A new card appears: **Select Invitees For This Event**.
**Show:** Click Private. The preview shows a lock and "Private". Scroll to the new invitees card.

#### [6.2] Who's invited
**Say:** For a masjid, tick Volunteers, Staff or Followers. For a madrasah event, tick Staff or Followers, or the Parents or Students of one class and section.
**Show:** Tick "Volunteers" and "Staff". Quick cut: the same card for Al-Noor Madrasah with Parents and Students and their class and section lists. Back on the masjid event, click Public again; the invitees card disappears.
**Caption:** Masjid: Volunteers, Staff or Followers. Madrasah: Staff, Followers, or Parents or Students of a class and section.

### 7. Notifications and pictures
#### [7.1] Notification Channel
**Say:** Under **Notification Channel**, **Email** and **Push Notification** are both on, so followers hear about the event. Untick one to skip it.
**Show:** Spotlight the Notification Channel chips, both ticked. Leave the TV switch above them as it is.

#### [7.2] Pictures
**Say:** Add a poster under **Image for Mobile/Web**. It shows on the event card in the app and on the website.
**Show:** Click the "Image for Mobile/Web" tile; choose the picnic poster, crop it, upload. The tile and the preview show the picture.

#### [7.3]
**Say:** **Image for Prayer Dashboard** is a tall version for your masjid's prayer-times TV screen.
**Show:** Click the "Image for Prayer Dashboard" tile; upload the tall poster. Spotlight "How it will appear" with the finished card.

### 8. Publish
#### [8.1] Save & Publish
**Say:** Check the preview, then tap **Save & Publish**. If something required is missing, Ad-Din points it out.
**Show:** Click "Save & Publish". Toast "Event Added Successfully". The Events page opens.

#### [8.2] Done
**Say:** Your event is now in the list, ready for your community to mark Going or Interested.
**Show:** Spotlight the new "Family Picnic" card with its poster and date badge.

#### [8.3] Next
**Say:** Next, set up an event that repeats every week and choose whether it shows on the masjid TV.
**Show:** End card with two tiles: "08.2 Repeating events and events on the masjid TV" and "15.3 Events: going, interested, calendar".
