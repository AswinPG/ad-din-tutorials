## Needs your decision

1. **12.1, 12.3: one release date per report, not per term (app bug?).** Parents see a card once it is Ready and the report's release date for the class has passed (`AcademicReportsRepository.cs` 1726-1730). The mapping has one date, "(For Current Term)". If the admin moves it forward for Term 2, every Term 1 card of that report disappears from the parents' archive until the new date. Raised by: PO. Options: (a, recommended) change the app to keep a release date per term, or to keep showing cards that were already released; (b) keep it, and add one line to 12.1 [5.2]: "Change the date only on the day you release the new term's cards"; (c) accept it as it is.
2. **12.3: emails don't wait for the release date.** The Email All path (`AcademicReportsService.cs` ~540-815) has no release-date check, so parents get the PDF as soon as the admin taps Send, even before the date. The video sets the date to today first, so it shows nothing wrong. Raised by: PO, user. Options: (a, recommended) confirm this is intended and add to 12.3 [5.4]: "Emails go out as soon as you tap Send"; (b) change the app to hold emails until the release date.
3. **Payment Reports shows with the Payment Module off (app).** The Madrasah Report page always shows the Payment Reports card: Accounts Receivable and Aged Receivable for admins, Fees Due Student for teachers, parents and students (`report.component.ts` 163-186; Exam Reports has a switch, this card has none). The scripts now keep it off camera in 12.4, 14.4 and 14.5. Raised by: director, PO. Options: (a, recommended) hide the card when the Payment Module is off, like Exam Reports, and log it in APP-ISSUES; (b) keep recording around it.
4. **Track 14: who is the parent?** The brief named Noor Mirza as the parent and Hana Mirza as the student. The world's demo account Yusuf Rahman is already the parent of Maryam Rahman, who has the published Term 1 report card, and 07.4 shows Yusuf as the parent of Maryam and Hamza. So 14.1 to 14.4 keep Yusuf, Maryam and Hamza (set to the Parent role only for 14.1), and 14.5 now uses Hana Mirza as the student. New names to add to the world for 14.1: Zainab Rahman (a pending enrolment), and a Parent role plus a child for Fatima Siddiqui. Raised by: PM, director. Options: (a, recommended) keep it this way; (b) recast 14.1 to 14.4 with Noor Mirza as the parent of Hana and two siblings who are not in the world yet.
5. **AI and children's data.** 12.1 [3.5] (AI translation of the card) and 12.2 [3.2] (Ad-Din's AI Comment Generator) send a student's results and card text to an AI service. The scripts don't say so. Raised by: PO, user. Options: (a, recommended) say nothing in the videos, as long as the privacy policy covers it; (b) add one line: "The AI sees the student's results, not their contact details", once that is confirmed.
6. **14.4 folder name.** The folder is still `14.4-fees-report-cards-and-interview-bookings` after the fees cut on 2026-11-03. Raised by: PM. Options: (a, recommended) rename it to `14.4-report-cards-and-interview-bookings` in the main session (git mv, then `npm run plan`); (b) leave it.
