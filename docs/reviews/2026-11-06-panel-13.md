## Needs your decision

The scripts now use Khadija Usman (office) and Idris Malik (caretaker), with world teachers Bilal Hussain and Omar Khalid. The world also needs Staff Attendance on, the zone "Main building", the "Office hours" template, terms "Autumn Term" and "Spring Term" (as in Track 10's decision 2), and review, period and payroll data. Raised by: PM, director, writer. Options: (a, recommended) add the two staff and a Staff Attendance state to `engine/demo-world.md` and `world.mjs`, built from the sanitised launch captures in `staff-attendance-launch/video/fixtures`; (b) pick other names.
2. **Re-locked periods aren't emailed, but the app says they are (app text).** The Settings line says "When a period locks, the payroll file is sent to …" (`en.json` 3526), but only version 1 is emailed by itself; v2 and later must be sent by hand (`IPayrollOutputSendService.cs` 76-78). 13.4 and 13.6 now say so. Raised by: PO, user. Options: (a, recommended) change the app text to "When a period first locks, …" and keep the scripts as they are; (b) make every re-lock email its new version, and then drop 13.6 [6.2a].
3. **Location and privacy settings are in no video.** Settings has a Location and privacy group: Location retention (6, 12 or 24 months), Location accuracy limit, and Rollout pause for location cards (`en.json` 3456-3500, 3639). The staff notice in 13.7 quotes the retention. Raised by: PM, PO. Options: (a, recommended) add one short chapter to 13.4 on Location retention, about 20 seconds (13.4 is at 3:08); (b) leave it out, and note it in FEATURE-MAP Gaps.
4. **Premium for every staff member.** Each person who checks in needs their own Ad-Din Premium, for example a seat (decision "Who needs a seat?"). For a masjid with volunteer caretakers this is a real cost, and 13.1 and 13.7 now say it plainly. Raised by: user, PO. Options: (a, recommended) keep it as it is now; (b) also name the seat video (02.3) in 13.1.
