## Needs your decision

1. **00.4 [3.4]/[4.1]: Skip for now loses the masjid (app bug, blocks release).** A member with a masjid but no madrasah taps Skip for now, and the masjid they picked is lost. Save & Continue stays disabled until a madrasah is chosen. This is the most common viewer. Raised by: user, PM, PO. Evidence: `masjid-selector.component.ts` 216, 234-240, 661-663. Options: (a, recommended) fix the app so Skip on step 2 saves the masjid, then add "No madrasah? Tap Skip for now"; (b) publish with a line telling viewers to set the masjid later from their profile; (c) hold 00.4.
2. **00.5 [7.1]: Delete Account fails for App Store and Google Play subscribers (app bug, store compliance risk).** Deleting calls CancelSubscription, which throws NotImplementedException for Apple and Play plans. The user sees "Unknown error" and the account stays. The script also says "any subscriptions are cancelled". Raised by: PO (and user, on refunds). Evidence: `AccountController.cs` ~2308; `AppleSubscriptionRepository.cs` 216-219; `PlaySubscriptionRepository.cs` 168-171. Options: (a, recommended) fix the API, then add "cancel a store plan in the store first"; (b) add that line now and leave the bug logged; (c) hold 00.5.
3. **00.5: how to say "GUID".** It's still a placeholder (G-U-I-D), and voicing 00.5 in other languages waits on it. Raised by: PM. Options: (a, recommended) letters, G-U-I-D, as now; (b) "goo-id"; (c) say "your account code" and show GUID on screen.
4. **01.1 [1.1]/[8.1] and 01.2 [6.2]: is registering free, and does a madrasah need Premium?** The madrasah admin pages need Premium (`organizationSubscriptionGuard`), and the app's own claim text says so. The videos say "you can start setting it up". Raised by: PO, user. Options: (a, recommended) add one line: "Registering and running a masjid's page is free; running a madrasah needs Ad-Din Premium (video 02.1)"; (b) say only "registering is free"; (c) leave it as it is.
5. **01.2 [3.3]: how long approval takes.** It's the main question while people wait. The popup only says "shortly". Raised by: user, PO. Options: (a, recommended) you give the real number, for example "a few working days"; (b) say "if it takes longer than a week, contact Ad-Din Support"; (c) say nothing.
6. **01.3 [4.3]/[6.2]: what if no current admin answers?** The video doesn't say. The app's guide mentions the current admin emailing support. Raised by: user, PO. Options: (a, recommended) "If you hear nothing, contact Ad-Din Support from the Profile menu", if support handles this; (b) say nothing.
7. **01.3: the file check is reversed (app bug).** Files under 10 KB are refused with a "more than 10 MB" message. Raised by: PM, PO. Options: (a, recommended) fix it before publishing, and log it in APP-ISSUES; (b) formally accept it. The script states no size limit either way.
8. **02.1 [6.1]: say "thirty days" in public?** The grace period exists only on the server (GRACE_MS, UserClaimRepository), and premium displays get 15 days. Naming it makes it a promise. Raised by: PM, PO. Options: (a, recommended) keep "thirty days" if you're happy to commit to it; (b) say "a short time".
9. **02.1 [3.1]: speak "Priority Support" and "Future Modules"?** They are card bullets. Spoken in six languages, they become promises. Raised by: PO. Options: (a, recommended) keep them on screen but don't say them; (b) keep them as they are.
10. **02.1 [5.2]: say that add-ons cost extra, and mention the 15-day free trial of Prayer Times Premium?** Raised by: PO. Options: (a, recommended) one line: "Each add-on is its own paid subscription; Prayer Times Premium starts with a free trial"; (b) leave it to 02.5.
11. **02.2 [1.1]: who needs personal Premium?** A member with no role gets nothing from it, and there are no refunds. People may pay by mistake. Raised by: PO, user. Options: (a, recommended) "You need Premium only if you use a Parent, Student, Teacher or Admin portal. Ask your madrasah first; many give families a seat"; (b) leave it as it is.
12. **02.2 [2.2], 02.3 [4.1]: currency.** The cards show "$", but Stripe charges CAD and adds sales tax. The stores show local prices. Raised by: user, PO, PM. Options: (a, recommended) fix the card label in the app to show CAD, and say nothing about price in the video; (b) say "Prices on the web are in Canadian dollars, plus tax where it applies".
13. **02.2 [2.3]: say "no refunds" plainly?** The app's Subscription terms say "No refund is processed". The script says only "read how renewal and refunds work". Raised by: PO, user. Options: (a, recommended) "Payments aren't refunded: if you cancel, you keep Premium to the end of the period"; (b) keep the soft wording.
14. **02.2 chapters 6-7: the app's Premium page and the store sheet.** The Premium page is native, and the store sheet is part of the phone's system. The recorder draws a likeness.
