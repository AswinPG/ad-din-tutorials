No video covers it, and FEATURE-MAP doesn't list it. Raised by: PM, PO. Evidence: `prayerTimes/display/display.component.html` 104-190. Options: (a, recommended) add a short chapter to 04.2, which is about Jumu'ah, and add it to FEATURE-MAP; (b) a scene in 04.4, where the section is; (c) list it as left out on purpose.

The video doesn't say whether Iqamah times or hand-typed times stay as they are. A volunteer will worry about this. Raised by: user. Options: (a, recommended) confirm with the team, then add one line, for example "Your Iqamah times stay as they are"; (b) say nothing.
4. **04.5 [1.2]: mention the free trial?** Decision 10 approved this line for 02.1: "Each add-on is its own paid subscription; Prayer Times Premium starts with a free trial". The premium display videos only say that you need the add-on, and 04.5 now points to 02.5. Raised by: PO, user. Options: (a, recommended) add the same words to 04.5 [1.2]; (b) leave it to 02.5.
