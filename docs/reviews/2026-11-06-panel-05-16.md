## Needs your decision

1. **16.1: students in the top class.** Migration moves a section into another class. The video doesn't say what to do with students who are leaving the madrasah, and the Alumni list is hidden. Raised by: user, PO. Evidence: `MigrationRepository.cs` 87-141 sets the old enrolment to Alumni and adds a new active one; there is no "leaving" option. Options: (a, recommended) you tell us the intended way (for example, mark them inactive on their profile), and we add one line; (b) say nothing.
2. **16.1: Migration Type starts on Exam Based.** Exam Marks is off by default and is out of the series, yet the form opens on Exam Based, with exam boxes. Raised by: user, PO. Evidence: `migrationAdd.component.ts` 120, `migrationAdd.component.html` 120-124. Options: (a, recommended) change the app to start on Transfer, or hide Exam Based when Exam Marks is off. The script then drops "starts on Exam Based". (b) keep it; the script now says to choose Transfer.
3. **05.7: a holiday notifies everyone at once, with no switch.** Every teacher, parent and student gets an email and a push as soon as you save. A wrong date goes to everyone. The script now warns about this. Raised by: user, PO. Evidence: `HolidayController.cs` 156. Options: (a, recommended) keep it as it is; the warning is enough; (b) add a "Notify" switch to Add Holiday, like Send Communication in the Schedule Builder.
4. **05.5 [5.1]: what "Mark as Homeroom" does.** Viewers outside Canada won't know the word, and the script doesn't explain it. In the code, the homeroom subject's course code prints on the grade 9-12 report card (`OntGrade9-12ProvincialFirstReportService.cs` 368-371). Nothing else was found. Raised by: user. Options: (a, recommended) you give a one-line meaning and we add it; (b) leave it as an on-screen label only.
5. **Demo world: classes and terms.** The names of the madrasah and its people now match the mocked world. The classes and terms these videos use ("Hifz Boys", "Maktab Level 1", "Hifz Grades", "Term 1", "Autumn Term", "Spring Term") are not in `engine/demo/world.mjs`. The world has Hifz Class A/B and Weekend Quran instead. Raised by: director, PM. Options: (a, recommended) add them per video through `world()` in each `record.mjs`, because most are created on camera; (b) rename them in the scripts to the world's classes.
