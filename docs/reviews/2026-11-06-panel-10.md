## Needs your decision

1. **10.2: Send Attendance Notifications.** The attendance toolbar has a second button, Send Attendance Notifications. It sends the current status to every parent in the class, with the note "Please align with teachers on who is responsible…" so that parents don't get duplicates. No video mentions it, and teachers who save with Notify parents on and then press it will notify twice. Raised by: user, PO, PM. Evidence: `attendance.add.component.html` 138-145 and 289, `en.json` 528-531. Options: (a, recommended) add one scene after [5.2]: "**Send Attendance Notifications** sends the final status to every parent in the class. Agree with your admin who sends it, so families aren't told twice." Add it to Covers. (b) leave it out.
2. **Demo world: classes, subjects and terms.** This is the same question as decision 5 of the 05-16 panel. Track 10 uses "Maktab Level 1" (sections A, B, C), "Hifz Boys", the subjects Fiqh, Quran and Hifz, and "Autumn Term" and "Spring Term". None of them is in `engine/demo/world.mjs`, and none is created on camera here. Raised by: director, PM. Options: (a, recommended) add them once to the world, shared by the whole track, because almost every video uses "Maktab Level 1 A"; (b) rename them in the scripts to the world's Hifz Class A/B and Weekend Quran.
3. **10.7: the Excel shots.** Scenes [2.3], [2.4], [3.3], [4.3] and [4.4] show Excel. Excel is not part of the mocked browser world. Raised by: director. Options: (a, recommended) record Excel on its own on the recording machine, with demo workbooks, and cut it in; (b) draw a simple spreadsheet in the stage.
4. **10.8: reminders by text message.** Send Reminder goes out by email, text message (SMS) and app notification (`MadressahNotificationService.cs`, SendInterviewReminderNotification). We don't know if text messages cost the madrasah anything or need an add-on. Raised by: user, PO. Options: (a, recommended) you confirm that texts are free and included, and the script stays as it is; (b) if they are paid or need an add-on, the script says so, or drops "text message".
5. **10.10: an award notifies twice.** Saving an award already sends the email and app notification. Send Certificate sends the same notification again. The script now says this. Raised by: PO, user. Evidence: `RecognitionController.cs` 205-211, 1336-1345. Options: (a, recommended) keep it as it is; (b) change the app so that Save stays quiet and only Send Certificate tells people. The script then goes back to its old wording.
