## Needs your decision

1. **02.4 [2.1]: masjid events don't need Premium.** Today's line says masjid admins need a seat for "its events, donations and reports". In the code, Events has no Premium check; Donations, Reports and User Management do. A masjid admin without a seat can still add events. Raised by: PO, PM. Evidence: `routes.ts` 376-382 (events: no SubscriptionGuard), `events/events.routes.ts` 12-13 (the guard is commented out), `routes.ts` 384-394 and 400-411, `settings.routes.ts` 27. Options: (a, recommended) say "the admins who run its donations, reports and user list", and change the Show to spotlight those; (b) keep "events", if Events will get a Premium check in the app; (c) leave it.
2. **03.3: no way to remove a staff member or volunteer from a masjid.** The masjid user list has only Add, Edit, Manage Subscription and Manage Skills, and on Manage Administrators the Staff and Volunteer boxes are locked. The madrasah list has Deactivate (03.4). Viewers will ask what to do when someone leaves. Raised by: user, PM, PO. Evidence: `masjidUserList.component.ts` 96-120, `masjidNewAdmin.component.html` 33-50. Options: (a, recommended) confirm with the team how it's done today; if there's no way, log it as an app issue and add a Deactivate button like the madrasah's; (b) add one line, "To remove someone, contact Ad-Din Support", if support does this; (c) say nothing.
3. **02.4 [5.3]: Cancel Subscription on a profile has no confirmation.** One click takes the seat away at once. It also does nothing when the person has no home madrasah (it returns before calling the server). The script now warns viewers and doesn't click it. Raised by: PO, user. Evidence: `subscription-detail.component.ts` 223-245. Options: (a, recommended) log it as an app issue and add an "Are you sure?" step; (b) accept it; the script already warns.
