## Needs your decision

1. **06.2: tell parents what enrolling costs, and what to do while they wait.** A parent's first worries are "will this cost me?" and "how long until they answer?". The video answers neither. In the code, a parent pays Ad-Din nothing to enrol: when the madrasah approves, it gives the family one of its own Premium seats (`approve-enrollment.component.html` 135-175). Ad-Din sets no time limit for the answer. Raised by: user, PO. Options: (a, recommended) add one line to [7.1]: "Enrolling through Ad-Din costs you nothing. Any fees are between you and your madrasah. If you hear nothing, contact your madrasah."; (b) say only the waiting part; (c) say nothing.
2. **06.3: Download Application and Add more course.** The Pending Enrollments list has a **Download Application** button, which saves the filled form as a PDF (several as a zip). The request window has **Add more course**. Neither is in Covers or FEATURE-MAP. Raised by: PM, PO. Evidence: `approve-enrollment.component.ts` 141-148, `.html` 135-137. Options: (a, recommended) add one sentence to 06.3 [2.3] for Download Application, list both in FEATURE-MAP, and leave Add more course out on purpose; (b) list both as left out on purpose.
3. **07.3: add parents to its audience.** Chapter 5 is the parent side: messaging a teacher. But the audience is only Madrasah Admin and Teacher, and 07.3 isn't in the Parent playlist. Raised by: PM. Options: (a, recommended) add Parent to 07.3's audience and add "07.3" to the Parent playlist; (b) leave it, and let parents learn it from 07.4 [6.3] (Message teacher).
4. **Possible app bug: the enrolment request reads the admin's home madrasah.** The Enrollment Request window shows the parent and child details only when the admin has a home madrasah. It then loads their documents and custom fields with the home madrasah's ID, not the madrasah being managed (`approve-enrollment.component.ts` 383, `getUserHomeMadressah()`; `.html` `*ngIf="userMadressah"`, `[orgId]="userMadressah.madressahID"`). An admin of two madrasahs, or one whose home madrasah is somewhere else, may see an empty or wrong panel. This was read from the code only; nothing was run. Recording isn't affected, because the demo admin's home madrasah is Maple Grove. Raised by: PO. Options: (a, recommended) log it in APP-ISSUES and check it on dev; (b) ignore it.
